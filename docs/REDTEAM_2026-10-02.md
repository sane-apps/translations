# Red team: Fathers translation process (2026-10-02, Mini, read-only)

## Headline

Eustathius did not "pass every check". It never went through the two-pass /
dual-checker pipeline at all, and the publication gate let it through anyway.
The same is true for most of the published corpus. Fix the gate before
adding new checks, or new checks will be bypassed the same way.

Evidence:
- All 24 Eustathius justifications FAIL `pipeline/check_pass_ab.py` (ok=0
  fail=24). Example `reviews/justifications/u01_open.json`: `pass_a_gloss` is
  the Greek pasted in; `pass_b_english` is the scaffold string
  "Unit 1. Lemma-led open — ἐν ἐνυπνίοις"; notes contain the banned
  contamination "Melito skipped; never Cyril Matthew densify". No lemmas, no
  choices. The 576-2,564-word English files have no receipt bound to them.
- The work was admitted on `reviews/audit/tip.review.json`, reviewer
  "cursor-held-eeng-20260924 (Mini)": every flag true, notes
  "Pass A/B OET tip." for every section. That is a hand stamp.
- `pipeline/verify_translation_qa.py:142-163` checks only the SHAPE of a review
  (flags true, notes nonempty). A one-line note passes. Its own docstring says
  it "cannot prove a reviewer actually understood it".
- Site gate is fail-open: `fathers.saneapps.com/scripts/catalogue_quality.py:376-379`
  ("No scope packet. Publish the current sections.") and `:432-433` (no review
  entry -> `continue`, i.e. publish). Legacy hash match at `:429` also publishes.
- Corpus-wide: 13,010 of 20,393 justifications fail check_pass_ab; 340 of 433
  books fail on EVERY justification. Top causes: no choices (10,847), no lemmas
  (10,749), Pass A = copied Greek (9,465), Pass B = placeholder (8,767).
- `data/publication-review.json`: 4,709 provisional-legacy entries, 5,992
  section reviews; 4,275 of those reviews have notes under 60 characters.
  Top reviewers are "cursor-held-*-20260924" batch stamps (100+ receipts).
- Only ~72 book slugs have ever produced an `outputs/ai-promote/*/summary.json`.
  Eustathius (engastrimytho), Didymus Dialexis and Origen Song Hom. 1 have none.

So the per-section, dual-family checker path the owner trusts covers a minority
of what is live. For the rest, nothing model-based ever compared the English to
the Greek.

## What the drafter and checkers actually see

- Draft prompt (`scripts/llm_bakeoff.py:73-103` SYS_TMPL, `:123-139` user_prompt,
  used by `scripts/draft_claim.py:339-343` and chunk loop `:360-372`): one
  section's Greek, a section id, editor OCR notes. No work title, author,
  addressee, opponent, glossary, previous/next section, or summary. A chunked
  section is drafted chunk by chunk with no memory of the prior chunk.
- `scripts/redraft_b.py:176-187`: Pass B written from the gloss clause by
  clause. Same isolation.
- `books/ante-nicene-topics/scripts/topic_bulk.py:331-363`: adds a voice card
  for the author, nothing about the work or speaker.
- `books/ante-nicene-topics/docs/GLOSSARY.md` exists but no prompt reads it;
  only `qa_professional.py:32` soft-checks it on topic MVP English.
- Checkers (`scripts/ai_promote.py:80-109` CHECK_SYS, `:356-387` prompt): one
  section's Greek + Pass A + Pass B. Seven accuracy flags. No readability,
  term-consistency, speaker or orientation flag. Line 109: "Minor stylistic
  differences alone are not errors." The checker also reads the drafter's own
  Pass A, so a misreading in A is anchored into B and then "confirmed".
- Only work-level deterministic check: `check_pass_ab.py:353-373` requires the
  English and source section-id sets to match. Nothing else spans sections.
- Closest existing work-level tool: site `scripts/prose_audit.py` (English-only
  CF judge, 3 sections per call, flag/score). Extend it; do not build a second.

## Evidence from spot checks

1. **Eustathius, On the Belly-Talker (Greek polemic).** Confirms the trigger
   list and adds: negation flip in the first sentence (A1); "[Exodus 22:18
   allusion]" bracket format (S6); "[2 Kings 21:4]" should be 21:6 (A5); prose
   is a gloss ("so that the superior one may be distinguished from the one of
   the two"). Five renderings of the title term in 12 files.
2. **Didymus, Dialogue with a Montanist (Greek dialogue, published).** Opens
   mid-sentence ("will be done away with"); speaker tags {M.}/{O.} never
   explained and change to [M.]/[O.] in the next file; two wrong verse refs and
   one misquoted verse in ~900 words; "I both condemn and glorify the
   Paraclete" (self-contradiction the orthodox speaker would not say; likely a
   misreading); "[things] of Paul"; section ends cut ("and the"). Unreadable
   for a first-time reader without knowing who Montanus is or who "O." is.
3. **Origen, Song of Songs Homily 1 (Jerome's Latin, published).** Good prose,
   mostly right. Defects are orientation only: "Climbing next into the volume
   of Kingdoms" and "take the land under Jesus" (Joshua). Shows the older,
   carefully done works are fine on accuracy but still need a name map.
4. **Ante-Nicene topics, soteriology_free_will.json (6 random of 711, all
   marked `source_verified`).** Tatian 11: good. Minucius 25: opponent's claim
   unmarked, "******", and an invented closing sentence. Lactantius 7.25,
   Novatian 4, Tertullian Marc. 3.3: gloss English with calques and false
   friends ("documents of powers", "a portion of a certain death"); Hippolytus
   Ref. 10.34: garbled clause "he was giving (because he is one human)".
   Roughly 4 of 6 would confuse a lay reader.

Verdict: Eustathius is typical of the 2026-09-24 "tip" batch and of polemic,
dialogue and catena works; worse than average only in term drift. Corpus
scans: chunk-cut starts/ends in 100 works, bracket fillers in 47, mixed
citation styles in 74. Expect at least half of published works to need a
work-level repair pass; expect the receipts of ~340 books to need regenerating.

## Proposed controls, ranked by value / cost

**1. Close the gate (deterministic, small code, highest value).**
- `catalogue_quality.py:376-379, 432-433`: missing review = HOLD, not publish.
  (Owner decision: this would hide most of the catalogue. Alternative: keep
  live but tag "unreviewed" and put these works at the head of the backlog.)
- `verify_translation_qa.validate_semantic_review`: require per-section notes
  that quote >=25 chars that really occur in the source AND >=25 chars in the
  English (substring check), reviewer ids from >=2 model families different
  from the drafter, and a receipt hash of the exact English snapshot (ai_promote
  already embeds `judged_snapshot`; require it to match the file).
- Require `check_pass_ab` PASS on the justification of every published section.
- Proof: re-run the gate on today's manifest; Eustathius and every
  "cursor-held" receipt must fail; a real ai_promote receipt must pass.

**2. Work lint (deterministic, new mode of `check_pass_ab.py`, cheap).**
Runs over a whole work's english files. Checks: (a) glossary drift: every
lemma in `books/<slug>/glossary.json` that occurs in a section's source must
show an approved rendering in that section, and banned variants anywhere fail;
(b) boundaries: section starts lowercase / ends without full stop;
(c) one citation style (full book names, parentheses); (d) ancient names map
(Kingdoms -> Samuel/Kings, Paralipomena -> Chronicles, "Jesus son of Nave" ->
Joshua; flag when present without the modern name); (e) bracket fillers,
stray roman numerals, asterisks, speaker sigla not defined in the brief;
(f) Latin false-friend list for `lat` works; (g) LXX Psalm map when the source
is Greek. Hook: `--tip-ready` and the site gate. Proof: seeded set below;
must also flag Eustathius term drift and the Didymus sigla.

**3. Per-work brief + fixed glossary given to drafter and checkers.**
File `books/<slug>/brief.json`: author/date, addressee, occasion, opponent(s)
and how their words are quoted, speaker labels, key terms with ONE rendering
each (ἐγγαστρίμυθος -> "medium" with first-use note), names map, Scripture
naming map. Plus previous section's last 2 English sentences and next
section's first source sentence. Drafted once from the whole source by
Nemotron 3 Ultra (long context), checked by Llama 3.3 70B (different
family), fixed by deterministic lint 2. Inject in `llm_bakeoff.user_prompt`,
`topic_bulk.draft/write_pass_b`, `redraft_b.DRAFT_SYS`, `ai_promote.checker_prompt`.
The same brief yields a 3-5 sentence reader orientation ("Before you read")
shown in About this text or above section 1 (site UX owner decision).
Proof: redraft 3 Eustathius sections with and without brief; lint 2 measures
term consistency; reader check 4 counts confusion items.

**4. First-time-reader check (free model, English only).** Extend site
`prose_audit.py`: feed the whole work in order (windows of ~3k words with a
running list of introduced names), no source, no brief. Ask for: unexplained
names/terms, "who is speaking here?", sentences it cannot parse, statements
that contradict an earlier section. Every item must quote the English; quotes
verified by substring. Model: Llama 3.3 70B or Gemma 4 26B (family different
from the drafter). Gate: issues per 1,000 words over a threshold blocks
publish; items route to redraft. Proof: seeded set; plus the 4 spot-check
works must each return the defects listed above.

**5. Work-level consistency pass (model).** Nemotron 3 Ultra gets the brief,
glossary and a deterministic concordance (every sentence containing each
glossary lemma: source + English side by side across sections) and flags
drift, speaker-boundary errors and broken continuity. Fixes go through the
normal redraft + dual checker. Runs once per work after all sections exist.

**6. Checker hardening (small prompt change + canaries).**
- Blind first pass: checker sees Greek + Pass B only for negation/agency/
  modality; Pass A shown only afterwards (breaks drafter anchoring, X4).
- Add flags `speaker_marked` (opponent/Scripture voice is clear) and
  `readable` (a lay reader can follow it); drop "minor stylistic differences
  are not errors" for gloss-English.
- Canaries: each batch slips in 1 known-bad section per checker (from the
  seeded set). A checker that passes a canary voids its batch passes.

**7. Provenance check for excerpts (deterministic + 1 model call).** Author,
work and locus must match the locked source file's header and an alignment
score from `topic_bulk.align`; the 32 mislabels become the regression set.

Not recommended: more per-section model checks, majority votes, or a softer
checker. They cannot see the failures in rows C, O, V.

## Proof plan

1. **Seeded-defect set** (`outputs/redteam-seeded/`, frozen, hashed):
   20 clean sections from 5 works (1 Latin homily, 1 Greek polemic, 1 dialogue,
   1 catena, 1 topic file) x 12 defect types: negation flip, agency swap,
   verse +-1 chapter, wrong book, dropped clause, invented sentence, opponent
   quote unmarked, term drift across 2 sections, chunk-cut start, ancient book
   name, false friend, garbled gloss sentence. Plus the real Eustathius
   defects (>=10 listed by owner and above) as a natural set.
2. **Baseline** today's pipeline on the set (check_pass_ab, ai_promote
   checkers, jev_cites, prose_audit). Record catch rate per type. Expect ~0%
   on drift, orientation, speaker, boundary types.
3. **After each control**, re-run. Targets: deterministic types >=95% recall;
   model types >=70%; false-flag rate on the clean 20 sections <=10%; Eustathius
   natural set >=8 of 10 flagged. Record receipts per run with model ids.
4. **Live canaries** (control 6) keep measuring checker recall every night.
5. **Backlog ranking**: run controls 1, 2, 4 (cheap, free) over all published
   works; rank by issues/1,000 words x traffic. Redraft top works with brief +
   glossary; nothing republishes without control 1's bound receipt.

Cost on free tiers: lint 2 is free; brief 3 is one long Nemotron call plus one
Llama check per work (~440 calls); reader check 4 is ~1 call per 3k words
(~16k-word work = 6 calls); consistency pass 5 is 1-3 calls per work.
