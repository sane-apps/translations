# Early Church Gap Inventory (writers who died by 450)

Built 2026-10-03. Data: `docs/early-inventory.json` (one row per work, sorted by year then author). Library match is against `books/*/book.yml` (slug, title, author, original_title) and the excerpt registry of `books/ante-nicene-topics`. Status `partial` means the library has only selections: either a book holding some books/fragments of the work, or excerpts inside the Ante-Nicene Dogmatics topical anthology.

Year = death year or floruit (composition date for anonymous texts), matching the site's author-dates where the writer is already dated.

## Coverage limits

- Granularity is Clavis-level work. Letters and sermon collections are one row per author; Chrysostom's ~900 homilies are grouped by series; Ephrem's attributed memre and Severian's oriental-only homilies are coarse rows.
- CPG/CPL numbers are mostly blank. No free full CPG/CPL listing was reachable (Pinakes blocks bots; Wikipedia rate-limited parts of the run). Wikidata covers about 360 works.
- Not enumerated: NT apocrypha and Gnostic/Nag Hammadi texts (out of scope by choice); later passions beyond Musurillo's list (BHG/BHL); minor catena fragments of many Greek exegetes; spurious works dated after 451 (17 Epiphanius pseudepigrapha in the library sit outside this inventory).
- Borderline dates kept with a note: Nestorius (d. c.451), Quodvultdeus (d. c.453), Marius Mercator, Julian of Eclanum (d. c.455, already in the library). Excluded: Theodoret, Leo the Great, Prosper, Salvian, Shenoute, Diadochus.
- Rows marked "unverified listing" in notes were not confirmed on a fetched page. PG volume links follow Migne's standard arrangement; column ranges were not checked.
- `english_exists` outside ANF/NPNF (New Advent index) is often a best guess and says so.
- Check: library labels the Didymus Tura commentaries' edition as "PG 39", which does not contain the Tura papyri texts.
