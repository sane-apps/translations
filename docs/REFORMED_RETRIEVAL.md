# Reformed retrieval lane

Same SOP as the Fathers pipeline (`docs/SOP.md`): lock public-domain **Latin** (prefer) or other allowed original-language editions; new English in two passes; no modern copyrighted English as copy-text; ship finished work to https://fathers.saneapps.com with an honest era note. This is **not** a patristic Rank-1 continuation and does not lift the 2026-09-13 quality pause on new Greek Rank-1.

## Why

The Reformed tradition would be in a healthier and stronger theological place if the major publishing houses were promoting and retrieving more works from men like John Davenant, Ludwig Crocius, Louis Le Blanc, Robert Baron, the theologians of Saumur, and the late 17th century Frankfurt (Oder) faculty, rather than continually recycling the same handful of stricter voices (Owen, Turretin, et al.) The breadth of the Reformed tradition deserves to be represented.

## Rules

- Prefer PD Latin editions (Internet Archive, HathiTrust, SLUB, Europeana). Document imprint, year, IA/Hathi id, license.
- Do **not** lock copyrighted critical editions or modern facing-page English as copy-text. Victorian PD English (e.g. Allport on Davenant Colossians) may be a sense-check only, never copy-text.
- Do **not** copy the 2024 Colloquia Scholastica / Reformed Books Online AI English of Le Blanc.
- Disclose era on the work page: these men wrote in the seventeenth century. The site must not silently read as ante-Nicene only.
- Tip first; do not stamp a collected folio as complete from an opening.
