Gregory Thaumaturgus ("the Wonderworker," c. 213–c. 270) left Pontus as a young law student and stayed five years at Caesarea as Origen's pupil before returning home to become bishop of Neocaesarea.

The Thanksgiving Address to Origen (c. 238) is his farewell oration — part gratitude, part spiritual autobiography, and our fullest portrait of Origen as a teacher.

This volume holds the opening of the oration: silence preferred, years without oratory, and fear before the subject.
