Cyril of Alexandria (c. 376–444) carried his case against Nestorius beyond bishops to the imperial court itself, lobbying the household of Theodosius II while the council gathered at Ephesus.

On the True Faith comprises two treatises written as the crisis broke for the women of that household — the princesses Arcadia and Marina, and the Augustas Pulcheria and Eudocia — arguing that Mary is truly Theotokos, Mother of God, because her Son is one divine person.

This volume holds both court treatises. The companion treatise To Theodosius, which already has a modern English version, is not included.
