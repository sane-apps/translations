Cyril of Alexandria (c. 376–444) wrote these fragments on the Epistle to the Hebrews. They run from the Son who is greater than the angels through the high priesthood, the tabernacle, and the call to faith.

The Son is eternal, and he is not a creature. He is anointed in his humanity. He humbles himself so that humanity is raised. He is the builder, greater than the house, and the high priest of the new covenant.

Noah is set out as an example of faith. The last fragments stay with the cross, with those who go outside the gate after Christ, and with the union of the Word and the flesh.
