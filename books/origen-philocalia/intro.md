Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) is the author of the excerpts in this anthology, the Philocalia. It is not one of his continuous commentaries.

The chapters take up how to read Scripture, what to do with a hard or strange passage, and whether philosophy may serve that reading. Later chapters take up providence, fate, free will, and the hardening of Pharaoh.

A reader meets the claim that Scripture is God-breathed and locked, and the warning that foreknowledge is not compulsion. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
