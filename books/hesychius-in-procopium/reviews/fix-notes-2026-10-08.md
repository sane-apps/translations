## Finding M-1 — §1: ὑπὲρ θεοῦ → "over God" (MAJOR, doctrinally material)

**Was:** "Jacob wrestled with God, but Procopius prevailed over God, setting up a noble trophy throughout the world."

**Finding:** The martyr's godly struggle became a victory *over* God — theologically incoherent.

**Now:** "Jacob wrestled with God, but Procopius wrestled for God, setting up a noble trophy throughout the world."

**Why (Greek):** «Ἰακὼβ ἐπάλαισε μετὰ θεοῦ, ὑπὲρ θεοῦ δὲ Προκόπιος» — ὑπὲρ + genitive = "for, on behalf of"; the verb ἐπάλαισε ("wrestled") is elided but understood in the second clause.
