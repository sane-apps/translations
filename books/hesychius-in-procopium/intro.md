Hesychius of Jerusalem (c. 412–450) was a priest in Jerusalem. He preached this homily for the feast of the martyr Procopius.

This English is On Saint Procopius. He holds the martyr up as a model of perseverance and innocence, and he sets him beside figures from Scripture.

A reader meets the praise of Procopius, a reflection on divine craftsmanship and human folly, the need for prudence and innocence, and perseverance in faith.
