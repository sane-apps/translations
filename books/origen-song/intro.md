Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) is the author of these homilies and of this commentary on the Song of Songs. The homilies are the two Jerome translated. The commentary is the four books Rufinus translated.

The homilies take up the kiss, the bride who is black and beautiful, the king on his couch, and the call to arise because winter is past.

The commentary reads Solomon's three books as morals, then nature, then contemplation. The bride is the church or the perfect soul, and the bridegroom is the Word. The surviving commentary ends with the little foxes. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
