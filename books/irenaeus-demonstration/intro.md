Irenaeus of Lyons (c. 130–c. 202) wrote this demonstration of the apostolic preaching for Marcianus. It is a handbook of the rule of faith. The English is a study text based on the public-domain Robinson translation. It is not his letter to Victor of Rome.

He sets the rule: one Father, the Word, and the Spirit. Then the making of the human being, the tree, the disobedience, Cain and Abel, the flood, and the blessing of Shem and Japheth.

The rest follows Abraham, the exodus, and the prophets' words about the Christ, through the passion and the Gentiles who leave their idols. It closes by telling Marcianus to keep the rule.
