This brief exegetical note is attributed to John of Damascus.

The note argues that the Spirit descends upon the apostles, that the Spirit is God, and that God is a consuming fire. Christ is said to baptize with fire because he poured out the grace of Christ on the holy disciples in the form of fiery tongues, or because of the punishing fire to come.

Key terms include Spirit, consuming fire, baptize, fiery tongues, grace, punishing, baptism, evil, sin, and endlessly. The final baptism is not saving but destructive of evil, and it punishes endlessly.
