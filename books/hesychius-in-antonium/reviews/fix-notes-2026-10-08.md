## Finding M-1 — §1: dative misread as comparison (MAJOR, doctrinally material)

**Was:** "…sang like David and drove out demons, presented himself as a temple greater than God, commanded lions like Daniel…"

**Finding:** The dative τῷ θεῷ was misread as a dative of comparison, producing the theologically impossible "a temple greater than God."

**Now:** "…sang like David and drove out demons, presented himself to God as a greater temple, commanded lions like Daniel…"

**Why (Greek):** «ναὸν ἑαυτὸν μείζονα τῷ θεῷ προσήγαγεν» — προσάγω + dative = "present to"; τῷ θεῷ is the indirect object ("to God"); μείζονα is absolute ("greater [than others]").
