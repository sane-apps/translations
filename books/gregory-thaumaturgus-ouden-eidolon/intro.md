Gregory Thaumaturgus wrote this treatise, of which a fragment survives. The text contrasts the honor given to the God who truly is with the honor given to a god who does not exist. It states the opposite effects each has on the one who gives honor.

The argument distinguishes two kinds of honor based on whether their object truly exists. Honor directed toward the God who truly is is itself an honor to the one who gives it. Honor directed toward a god who does not exist is, in reverse, dishonor to the one who gives it.
