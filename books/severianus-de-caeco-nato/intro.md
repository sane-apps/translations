Severianus of Gabala (fl. c. 400) was bishop of Gabala in Syria and a preacher in Constantinople. This sermon was formerly attributed to John Chrysostom.

This English is On the Man Born Blind. The same work is also known in a longer form that takes in the thief and faith. It is not his sermons on Job.

A reader meets the light of Christ, the church pictured as paradise and the serpent's error, the trial of the blind man, and the thief's confession.
