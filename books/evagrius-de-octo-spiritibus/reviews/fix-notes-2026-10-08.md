# FIX-NOTES — evagrius-de-octo-spiritibus (On the Eight Evil Spirits)

Full retranslation of all 8 sections (u01-open, u01-rem, u02-open, u02-rem) from the device Greek (`edos_*_source.json`). Systematic error profile: numeral misreads, false friends, voice/subject flips, dropped images — pervasive, so every paragraph was re-derived rather than patched. All 64 findings (M-1–M-64) addressed.

## Chapter-number corrections (M-1)
- **Was:** "CHAPTER 29" / "CHAPTER 2" — **Now:** "Chapter 30" / "Chapter 3" (ΚΕΦΑΛ. Λʹ=30, Γʹ=3). Why: Λʹ is 30, not 29; Βʹ=2, Γʹ=3 — the source heading read ΚΕΦΑΛ. Λʹ … Βʹ … Γʹ.
- **Was:** "CHAPTER 8" / "CHAPTER 9" — **Now:** "Chapter 9" / "Chapter 10" (ΚΕΦΑΛ. Θʹ=9, Ιʹ=10). Why: every numeral was read one low in the anger/gentleness chapters.

## Gluttony (ch. 30, 2, 3)
- M-2 — **Was:** muddled Num 24:20 clause — **Now:** "The beginning of the nations is Amalek: just as Amalek is the beginning of the nations, so gluttony is the beginning of the passions" (Num 24:20). Why: parallel cola «Ἀρχὴ ἐθνῶν Ἀμαλὴκ … οὕτω γαστριμαργία ἀρχὴ παθῶν» were conflated.
- M-3 — **Was:** "when the belly is weakened" — **Now:** "when the belly's strength runs out" (ἐπιλειπούσης δυνάμεως = strength failing).
- M-4 — **Was:** "with the jaw of an ass he destroyed a thousand" — **Now:** "with the jawbone of an ass he slew a thousand" (Judg 15:15; σιαγών = jawbone; ἀνεῖλεν = slew).
- M-5 — **Was:** "drove the nail into his jaw (Exodus 12)" — **Now:** "drove the tent-peg into his temple" (Judg 4:21; πάσσαλος = tent-peg; κρόταφος = temple). The (Exodus 12) ref was fabricated — dropped.
- M-6 — **Was:** "youth" / "genitals" (3×) — **Now:** "satiety" throughout (κόρος = satiety). Why: κόρος here is satiety; the genitals reading was a false friend. Applies to "Fornication will take satiety as an ally", "burning in satiety", "rests upon satiety".
- M-7 — **Was:** "articulate the word" — **Now:** "distinguish the word of truth" (διαρθροῦν = distinguish/articulate properly; 2 Tim 2:15).
- M-8 — **Was:** "mementos" — **Now:** "tombs" (μνήματα = tombs; Matt 23:27).
- M-9 — **Was:** "under tribute-paying" / "revenue" — **Now:** "a tributary [city] … give tribute to the king of the Assyrians" (ὑπόφορος = tributary; δασμόν = tribute; cf. 2 Kgs 17:3).
- M-10 — **Was:** "feasts" — **Now:** "camps out" (αὐλίζεται = camps/encamps; the Levite "camps out" in his body).
- M-11 — **Was:** "the fragrance of God" — **Now:** "God's sense of smell" (ὄσφρησις = sense of smell, not fragrance).
- M-12 — **Was:** "fattens … grease … straw" — **Now:** "fattens … rich … dry brushwood" (πιαίνει = fattens; λιπαρός = rich; φρύγανον = dry brushwood).
- M-13 — **Was:** "abundant" — **Now:** "needy" (ἐνδεής = needy; Prov 21:17).

## Fornication (chs. 4–6)
- M-14 — **Was:** "[It will be] moved by a passionate impulse" — **Now:** "[It] will neigh when moved by a passionate impulse" (χρεμετίσει = will neigh; the horse image was dropped).
- M-15 — **Was:** "Temperance gives birth to self-control" — **Now:** "Self-control gives birth to temperance" (subject is ἐγκράτεια).
- M-16 — **Was:** "does not compare them to public festivals, nor does he surround himself with them" — **Now:** "does not visit public festivals, nor does he go about gaping at the feasts" (παραβάλλει = visit/approach; περιάξει κεχηνώς = go about gaping).
- M-17 — **Was:** "become a tool for enemies" — **Now:** "become the enemies' by-product" (πάρεργον = by-product/collateral, not tool).
- M-18 — **Was:** "do not give them boldness, lest they ever tear you apart" — **Now:** "do not give them boldness to be bold toward you ever" (θαῤῥῆσαί σοί = to be bold toward you; no tearing).
- M-19 — **Was:** "Second, I saw" / "having smiled, and they laughed when [you] were poured out" — **Now:** "the second time you saw [them] … you smiled, and they laughed unrestrainedly" (εἶδες/ἐμειδίασας are 2nd singular; κεχυμένως = adverb "unrestrainedly", not "poured out").
- M-20 — **Was:** "being delighted by the passion" — **Now:** "shaping their gaze to proclaim the passion" (εὐαγγελιζόμεναι = proclaiming/announcing).
- M-21 — **Was:** "sinks the hand deeper" — **Now:** "sinks [one] worse than a wave" (βυθίζει is intransitive; no hand in the Greek).
- M-22 — **Was:** "a dog barks" — **Now:** "a dog wags [its tail]" (σαίνει = wag).
- M-23 — **Was:** "the idol of her" — **Now:** "her image" (εἴδωλον = mental image/phantasm, not idol).
- M-24 — **Was:** "well-proportioned crucible … [poorly made] for long [use] … surround the net of your soul … unlit places" — **Now:** "measured smelting purifies silver, but prolonged [smelting] easily destroys it … set fire to the threshing-floor of your soul … a spark lingering in chaff" (χωνεία = smelting; ἐπὶ πολὺ = prolonged; ἅλως = threshing-floor; ἄχυρον = chaff/straw).

## Love of money (chs. 7–8)
- M-25 — **Was:** "leaves the place without being crushed … departs straightway" — **Now:** "left the place without grief … departs cheerfully" (ἀλύπως = without grief; εὐθύμως = cheerfully).
- M-26 — **Was:** "prostrate with anxieties … forced to repent … circles the memories" — **Now:** "fettered by anxieties … forced to move away … carries the memories" (πεπέδηται = is fettered; μεταναστῆναι = move away).
- M-27 — **Was:** "his limb is pulled away" — **Now:** "unwilling, he is dragged" (ἄκων = unwilling).
- M-28 — **Was:** "this unceasing haste" — **Now:** "this fruitless haste" (ἀνήνυτος = unaccomplished/fruitless).
- M-29 — **Was:** "rejoices in many arrivals, but the ungreedy… [truncated]" — **Now:** "rejoices in many revenues, but the ungreedy in crowns of achievements" (πρόσοδοι = revenues; second colon restored).
- M-30 — **Was:** (Exodus 20:4) — **Now:** (Deut 27:15) for "Accursed is he who makes an idol and sets it in secret".

## Anger and gentleness (chs. 9–10)
- M-31 — **Was:** "a wrathless spirit is not seized by fury" / "scattered through all things" / "mists" / "set upright" — **Now:** "thumos does not snatch away a wrathless soul" (θυμός is the subject); "continually troubled" (διὰ παντός = continually); "bloodshot" (ὕφαιμοι); "rightly composed" (κατεσταλμένον).
- M-32 — **Was:** "The deed of a man is remembered" — **Now:** "A man's gentleness is remembered by God" (πραΰτης = gentleness).
- M-33 — **Was:** "the dwelling of the Holy Trinity becomes a peace-making intellect" — **Now:** "a peacemaking mind becomes a dwelling of the Holy Trinity" (subject is εἰρηνευομένη διάνοια).
- M-34 — **Was:** "beasts defile a troubled heart" — **Now:** "beasts make their lair in a troubled heart" (ἐμφωλεύω = make one's lair).
- M-35 — **Was:** "flees a shameful pursuit" — **Now:** "flees a shameful lodging" (καταγώγιον = lodging).
- M-36 — **Was:** "let fury not pipe in your heart … smoke without sound" — **Now:** "let thumos not encamp in your heart … the smoke of chaff" (αὐλίζομαι = encamp; ἄχυρον = chaff).
- M-37 — **Was:** "Pleasant is contemplation, a calm sea … thoughts are immersed" — **Now:** "A pleasant sight is a calm sea … god-befitting thoughts swim" (θεωρία = sight/spectacle here; ἐννήχεται = swim).

## Sadness (chs. 11–12)
- **Was:** "sorrow is a depression … anger is the defense of desire … failure of defense" — **Now:** "sadness is the dejection of the soul … thumos is the appetite for revenge, and the failure of revenge gave birth to sadness".
- **Was:** "A mother giving birth to a child, if she has been spared from the pain of labor, does not suffer much" — **Now:** "A mother giving birth to a child is in pain; once she has given birth, she is released from the pain" (inverted reading corrected).
- **Was:** "protesting against unrestrained folly … falling from defense … diverted that desire … the impassive one" — **Now:** "when he misses licentious folly … falls short of revenge … turned aside from the appetite … the dispassionate" (διαμαρτών = missing; ἀμύνη = revenge; ἀπαθής → dispassionate).
- **Was:** "A crucible cleanses unproven silver, and Godly sorrow in sins [is like this] … A cloud dims … The taste of an oyster" — **Now:** "A smelting-pot purified base silver, and godly sadness [purifies] a heart in sins … Darkness dims … Jaundice takes away the sense of taste" (ἀδόκιμον = base; γνόφος = darkness; ἴκτερος = jaundice).
- ἀπάθεια → "dispassion" throughout (was "apatheia"/"apathy").

## Acedia (chs. 13–14)
- M-38 — **Was:** "a plant of the desert" — **Now:** "brushwood of the desert" (φρύγανον = dry brushwood).
- M-39 — **Was:** "lover of women" — **Now:** "lover of pleasure" (φιλήδονος = lover of pleasure).
- M-40 — **Was:** "stares intently at the doors … the door has worn down, and he is driven out of himself … through the small opening he peers in" — **Now:** "stares intently at the windows … the door creaked, and he leapt up … peeped through the little window" (θυρίς = window; τρίζω = creak; ἐξάλλομαι = leap up).
- M-41 — **Was:** "experiences many gaps … scrutinizes the letter" — **Now:** "yawns repeatedly … finds fault with the writing" (χασμάομαι = yawn; ψέγω = find fault with).
- M-42 — **Was:** "cleansed of the strength of the body … broken in the tones of the soul" — **Now:** "deprived of the strength of his body … had the sinews of his soul loosened" (καθαιρέω = deprive; τόνοι = sinews).
- M-43 — **Was:** "pray sensibly and with speed" — **Now:** "pray sensibly and vigorously" (εὐτόνως = vigorously).

## Vainglory (chs. 15–16)
- M-44 — **Was:** "a useless worker … A shaken money-bag" — **Now:** "an unpaid worker … A pierced money-bag" (ἄμισθος = unpaid; τετρημένος = pierced).
- M-45 — **Was:** "a treacherous rock; if you lean upon it" — **Now:** "a submerged rock; if you are wrecked upon it" (ὕφαλος = submerged; προσρήσσω = be wrecked upon).
- M-46 — **Was:** "prays into his own treasury" — **Now:** "prays in his inner chamber" (ταμεῖον = inner chamber; Matt 6:6).
- M-47 — **Was:** "a crushed incense burner" — **Now:** "a shattered sacrificial victim" (ἱερεῖον = sacrificial victim).
- M-48 — **Was:** "agitates the mind of one fallen from God; it has made the weak man shout" — **Now:** "strings the mind that has fallen away from God; it made the weak man strong" (νευρόω = string a bow; ἐῤῥωμένον = strong).
- Minor: σμῖλαξ → "bindweed" (was "briar").

## Pride and humility (chs. 17–19)
- M-49 — **Was:** "a hollow swelling of the soul" — **Now:** "the swelling of a soul, full of pus" (ἰχώρ = pus; cf. Job 15:16).
- M-50 — **Was:** "will cause much sorrow" — **Now:** "will cause much disgust" (ἀηδία = disgust).
- M-51 — **Was:** "pride announces the presence of vainglory" — **Now:** "the presence of vainglory proclaims pride" (subject is παρουσία κενοδοξίας).
- M-52 — **Was:** "so the one trembling falls by his own power" — **Now:** "so he who trusts in his own power falls" (θαῤῥῶν = trusting).
- M-53 — **Was:** "A pruning hook carries away a fruitful branch" — **Now:** "A prop bears a fruit-laden branch" (χάραξ = prop/stake).
- M-54 — **Was:** "he falls under the shadow of an owl" — **Now:** "he cowers at the shadow of a bird" (καταπτήσσω = cower at; ὄρνεον = bird).
- M-55 — **Was:** "later is bloodless" — **Now:** "is later terrified" (ἐκδειματόω = terrify).
- M-56 — **Was:** "The scorpion" — **Now:** "…a worm" (σκώληξ = worm; the fragment closes ch. 18's "in a little while a worm").
- M-57 — **Was:** "A great man helped by God has been abandoned" — **Now:** "It is a great thing for a man to be helped by God; he was abandoned" (Μέγα is neuter: "it is a great thing").
- M-58 — **Was:** "recognize the one who is of the same kind, because He is of the same essence" — **Now:** "recognize your fellow human, that he is of the same substance" (ὁμόφυλος = fellow human).
- M-59 — **Was:** "he has been established more securely for you" — **Now:** "he stands more securely than you".
- M-60 — **Was:** "A proud tree is one without roots" — **Now:** "A proud monk is a rootless tree".
- M-61 — **Was:** "a blind intellect" — **Now:** "an unpretentious mind" (ἄτυφος = unpretentious).
- M-62 — **Was:** "A wind-blown grain is tossed about … a cracked vessel will be destroyed" — **Now:** "A breeze lifts a straw … a bubble that has burst will vanish" (κάρφος = straw; ἀπόνοια = madness; πομφόλυξ = bubble).
- M-63 — **Was:** "a polishing of the soul" — **Now:** "a poultice for the soul" (μάλαγμα = poultice).
- M-64 — **Was:** "Humility is the roof of a house" — **Now:** "Humility is the crown of the house" (στέφανος = crown).

## Structural notes
- The ch. 17 opening sentence spans the u02-rem-early/u02-rem-mid file split in the Greek; the English preserves that split (early ends "…full of pus;", mid resumes "if it is lanced…").
- Added/corrected scripture refs: Judg 15:15, 15:19, 4:21; Gen 3; Ex 17:12; Ex 2:12; Deut 27:15; 1 Tim 6:10. Removed the fabricated "(Exodus 12)".
- Minor corrupt-word note: ch. 9's «τοὺς στρόφιγγας» (lion in a cage) is uncertain/corrupt in the base text; rendered "stirs continually" without committing to "feathers".
