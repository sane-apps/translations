# Fix Notes — evagrius-scholia-ecclesiasten (Scholia on Ecclesiastes)

**Verdict:** Patched (2 flagged findings + systematic term-consistency pass across all 6 section files). The surrounding scholia read accurately per the review.

**Files written:** `corrected-english/ese_u01_open_english.json`, `ese_u01_rem_english.json`, `ese_u02_open_english.json`, `ese_u02_rem_english.json`, `ese_u03_open_english.json`, `ese_u03_rem_english.json`.

**Term-consistency pass applied:** ἀπάθεια→"dispassion" (ἀπαθής→"dispassionate"; νοητὴ ἀπάθεια→"noetic dispassion"); θυμός→"anger"; θεωρία→"contemplation".

## Per-finding notes

### M-1 — §1 (u01-open, on Eccl 1:11)
- **Was:** "'I remembered eternal years' (Ps 70:5)"
- **Finding:** Ps 70:5 ("I am poor and needy") contains no such line; the "eternal years" phrase is Ps 76:6 LXX (ἔτη αἰώνια ἐμνήσθην)
- **Now:** "'I remembered eternal years' (Ps 76:6)"
- **Why:** both quoted half-lines ("I remembered days of old", "I remembered eternal years") come from Ps 76:6 LXX; the (Ps 70:5) citation was wrong.

### M-2 — §1 (u01-open, on Eccl 1:2)
- **Was:** "marveling at the theory of events"
- **Finding:** τὴν θεωρίαν τῶν γεγονότων — θεωρία is Evagrius's technical term for contemplation
- **Now:** "marveling at the contemplation of the things that have come to be"
- **Why:** θεωρία→"contemplation" per the cross-work convention; "theory" systematically flattens the term.

## Term-consistency fixes (unflagged, in scope per the systematic-defect mandate)

- **u01-rem-early:** "a request is a mental apathy of a rational soul, overlaying holy knowledge" → "a request is the noetic dispassion of a rational soul, drawing holy knowledge to itself." (Greek: αἴτησις… ἐστὶ νοητὴ ἀπάθεια ψυχῆς λογικῆς ἁγίαν γνῶσιν ἐπισπωμένη — ἐπισπωμένη = "drawing to itself", not "overlaying".)
- **u01-rem-mid:** "they themselves shall know the vision of things happened" → "they themselves shall know the contemplation of the things that have come to be." (Greek: γνώσονται τὴν θεωρίαν τῶν γεγονότων.)
- **u02-open:** "A passionless mind" → "A dispassionate mind." (Greek: νοῦς ἀπαθής.)
- **u02-rem-mid:** "those worthy of impassibility and knowledge" → "those deemed worthy of dispassion and knowledge." (Greek: τῶν ἀπαθείας καταξιωθέντων καὶ γνώσεως.)
- **u03-open (Eccl 7:3):** "A good temper is better than laughter" → "Anger is better than laughter." (Greek: ἀγαθὸν θυμὸς ὑπὲρ γέλωτα.)
- **u03-open:** "When the temper, striving for virtue, is directed toward the demons" → "When anger, striving for virtue, boxes with the demons" (Greek: ὁ θυμὸς… πυκτεῦῃ τοῖς δαίμοσιν — πυκτεύω = "box/fight with fists", not "directed toward").
- **u03-open:** "but the righteous one rages against such passions" → "but the righteous man grows angry at such passions" (Greek: ὁ δὲ δίκαιος καὶ θυμοῦται κατὰ τῶν τοιούτων παθῶν.)
- **u03-rem-early:** "no one would say that the mind rests in the sensible bosom" → "no one would say that anger rests in the sensible bosom." (Greek: τὸ θυμὸν ἐν τῷ αἰσθητῷ ἀναπαύσασθαι κόλπῳ.)
- **u03-rem-close (Eccl 11:10):** "turn away the passion from your heart" → "turn away anger from your heart." (Greek: ἀπόστησον θυμὸν ἀπὸ καρδίας σου.)

## Unfixable / caveats
- u02-open cites "(Psalm 138:3, 40:14)" for "Enlighten my eyes, lest I sleep unto death" (properly Ps 13:3 LXX) and "(Proverbs 6:5)" for "like a dove from the snare" (the LXX has δορκάς = "gazelle", not "dove") — wrong-citation class errors in the same family as M-1, but not flagged in the review; left untouched, recommend a citation audit in a future pass.
