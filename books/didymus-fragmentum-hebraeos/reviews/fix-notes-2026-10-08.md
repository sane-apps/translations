# Fix Notes — Didymus the Blind, Fragmentum in Hebraeos (didymus-fragmentum-hebraeos)

Corrected files: `corrected-english/dfh_u01_open_english.json`, `dfh_u01_rem_english.json`. Schema, keys, and all unflagged passages are untouched.

## MAJOR 1 — δι' ἡμᾶς → "through us" (u01-open[0], u01-rem-close[0])
- Was: "even if through us he united to himself the created flesh…"
- Now: "even if for our sake he united to himself the created flesh…"
- Why: Greek εἰ καὶ δι' ἡμᾶς ἥνωσεν ἑαυτῷ τὴν κτιστὴν σάρκα (διά + acc. = for the sake of; "through us" wrongly makes humanity the instrument of the union).

## MAJOR 2 — νοερᾷ → "spiritual" (u01-open[0], u01-rem-close[0])
- Was: "a soul rational and spiritual" / "a soul both rational and spiritual"
- Now: "a soul rational and intelligent" / "a soul both rational and intelligent"
- Why: Greek ψυχῇ λογικῇ τε καὶ νοερᾷ (νοερός = intelligent, of νοῦς; the anti-Apollinarian formula).

## MAJOR 3 — u01-rem-early structurally broken (truncated mid-word)
- Was: single fragment ending "…saying, into the..."
- Now: the page carries the complete u01-open translation (its Greek duplicates the fragment's opening, carried completely only on u01-open), with MAJOR 1–2 and the MINOR fixes below applied.

## MINOR — Deut 4:19 quotation (u01-open[0])
- Ref: Jer 51:41 → Deut 4:19.
- Dropped μή restored: "…not looking up, saying, 'looking into heaven…'" → "…forbade worshiping a creature, saying: 'lest, looking up into heaven and seeing the sun and the moon and the stars and all the cosmos of heaven, going astray, you worship them'."
- Why: Greek μὴ ἀναβλέψας…εἰς τὸν οὐρανόν, καὶ ἰδὼν…πλανηθεὶς προσκυνήσῃς αὐτοῖς (Deut 4:19 LXX).
