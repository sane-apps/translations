Didymus the Blind (c. 313–c. 398) is the author of this fragment on Hebrews. It is not Severian's fragments on that letter.

He argues that if all creation worships Christ, Christ is not a creature. The fragment then speaks of Christ's union with created flesh.

A reader needs the line in which the angels worship the Son, and the difference between the Creator and a creature. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
