Evagrius Ponticus (c. 345–399) was born in Ibora in Pontus. Basil of Caesarea ordained him a reader, and he served as a deacon under Gregory of Nazianzus in Constantinople. He later lived as a monk in Egypt, at Nitria and Kellia.

This English is his Exposition on Proverbs. It is not his Notes on Proverbs, which are a different book. He explains selected sayings rather than commenting on every verse.

A reader meets discussions of fear and wisdom, the soul, virtue and knowledge, the ant and the honey of words, the deception of vice, and spiritual food. The sayings are read as teaching about knowledge and the life of the soul.
