## u01-open — section retranslated (systematic error profile)

- **M-1** Was: EN[0] "the [O] codices" — a dangling fragment. Finding: now EN[0] "Scholia on Proverbs. Asterisk, obelus. Sayings to which obeli are attached were found neither among the other translators nor in the Hebrew, but only among the Seventy…" Why: Greek opens "Σχόλια εἰς τὰς παροιμίας. ἀστερίσκος, ὀβελός" and the "ο" of "τοῖς ο" (the Seventy) was mangled into "[O] codices".
- **M-2** Was: EN[1] contained the gibberish token "ω6ρ6". Finding: the whole preface's long textual-critical note (on the asterisk/obelus marks, the sequence of the sayings among the Seventy vs. the other translators and the Hebrew) was re-derived from the Greek. The token ω6ρ6 is preserved as printed in the PG base text ("τούτῳ τῷ μονοσυλλάβῳ ω6ρ6") but it is an apparent encoding/OCR artifact in the base text, not a real word — flagged here for the base-text audit.
- **M-3** Was: EN[2] "77 a Proverb". Finding: the "77" is a phantom (page/column number artifact in the PG text); now "α. Proverb. A proverb is a word signifying intelligible things through sensible things."
- **M-4** Was: EN[2] stray "B" in "b The Kingdom of Israel". Finding: dropped; the scholia letters (α, β, δ) are now rendered "α.", "β.", "δ." throughout.
- **M-5** Was: (1 Kings 4:30) for the quote "Ἐβασίλευσεν ἐν Ἰσραὴλ τοῦ γνῶναι παιδείαν καὶ σοφίαν". Finding: now (Prov 1:2) — the quote is Prov 1:2 LXX ("γνῶναι σοφίαν καὶ παιδείαν"), rendered "He made instruction and wisdom reign in Israel, to know instruction and wisdom".
- **Terms**: θεωρίαν → "contemplation" (was "the theory concerning ethics, physics, and theology"); κριτὴς → "criterion"; μετριοπάθεια παθῶν kept "moderation of the passions".

## u01-rem — systematic term fixes
- "the highest apathy" → "the highest dispassion" (ἄκραν ἀπάθειαν).
- "The one who is impassive rests without fear" → "The dispassionate one rests without fear" (ὁ ἀπαθής).
- "the impassivity of the soul" → "the dispassion of the soul" (τὴν ἀπάθειαν τῆς ψυχῆς).
- "restrain a troubled temper… the turbulence of the temper" → "restrain troubled anger… the disturbance of anger" (ταρασσόμενον θυμόν… τῆς ταραχῆς τοῦ θυμοῦ).
- "to the impassive and the pure" → "to the dispassionate and the pure" (τοῖς ἀπαθέσι καὶ καθαροῖς).
- "the spirited part prevails in the demons; for a spirited temperament is unmerciful" → "the irascible part prevails in the demons; for anger is unmerciful" (τὸ θυμικὸν μέρος… ἀνελεήμων γὰρ θυμός), with "ὀξεῖα ὀργή" → "wrath is sharp".

## u02-open
- "the foal is of apatheia" → "the foal is of dispassion" (ἀπάθεια). Rest of section clean ("contemplation" already correct).

## u02-rem
- "But 'body' is the thymic and desiring part of the soul" → "the irascible and desiring part" (τὸ θυμικὸν καὶ ἐπιθυμητικὸν μέρος τῆς ψυχῆς), per the θυμός convention.

## u03-rem
- "for by analogy of our apatheia, we are made worthy of knowledge" → "for by analogy of our dispassion we are deemed worthy of knowledge" (κατ' ἀναλογίαν γὰρ τῆς ἀπαθείας ἡμῶν καταξιοῦμεθα γνώσεως).
- "refreshing the heat arising from the practical life" → "refreshing the heat that comes from practice" (περιψύχων τὸν ἐκ τῆς πρακτικῆς συμβαίνοντα καύσωνα). Verified: "τῶν γεγονότων τοὺς λόγους" nearby is correctly "the words of things that have happened" — no θεωρία there, left as-is.

## u04-rem
- "they say soul, [meaning] spirit and desire, which some call a passive part of the soul" → "by 'soul' they mean anger and desire, which some call the passionate part of the soul" (ψυχὴν δέ φασιν θυμὸν καὶ ἐπιθυμίαν, ὅπερ τινὲς παθητικὸν μέρος ὀνομάζουσι) — two fixes in one (θυμός + παθητικόν).
- "The one rich man purifies his passion through almsgiving" → "purifies his anger" (ὁ πλούσιος… καθαίρει θυμόν).
- "the passive part of the soul 'the soul', which is divided into spirit and desire" → "by 'soul' he meant the passionate part of the soul, which is divided into anger and desire" (τὸ παθητικὸν μέρος… διαιρεῖται εἰς θυμὸν καὶ ἐπιθυμίαν).
- "virtues of spirit and desire" → "virtues of anger and desire" (θυμοῦ καὶ ἐπιθυμίας εἰσὶν ἀρεταί).

## u05-open
- Fragment "deprivation of apatheia" → completed from the Greek: "Punishment is deprivation of dispassion and of the knowledge of God, with bodily pain; but judgment is the generation of an age, distributing to each of the rational bodies according to proportion." (τιμωρία μέν ἐστιν στέρησις ἀπαθείας καὶ γνώσεως θεοῦ μετ' ὀδύνης σωματικῆς).

## u05-rem
- "someone steals foreign theories" → "steals foreign contemplations" (ἀλλότρια θεωρήματα), twice.
- "the field as the theory of the world" → "the contemplation of the world" (τὴν θεωρίαν τοῦ κόσμου).
- "apathy of a rational soul, established from the practical [virtues], is a wall" → "A wall is the dispassion of a rational soul, constituted from the practical virtues" (φραγμός ἐστιν ἀπάθεια ψυχῆς λογικῆς, ἐκ τῶν πρακτικῶν συνεστῶσα).
- "into an impassive mind" → "into a dispassionate mind" (εἰς νοῦν ἀπαθῆ).
- "unable to contemplate through its own weakness by such a theory" → "unable through its own weakness to gaze continuously upon such contemplation" (τῇ τοιαύτῃ θεωρίᾳ).
- "by deprivation, the impassive one is named" → "the dispassionate one is named" (ὁ ἀπαθής).
- "An impassive mind delights in manifold wisdom" → "A dispassionate mind delights in manifold wisdom" (νοῦς ἀπαθής).

## u06-open
- "acquires apathy and knowledge of God" → "acquires dispassion and the knowledge of God" (ἀπάθειαν κτᾶται καὶ γνῶσιν θεοῦ).
- "the wall signifies apathy and the knowledge of God" → "the wall signifies dispassion and the knowledge of God".
- "the assumption of false dogmas and theories" → "false dogmas and contemplations" (ψευδῶν δογμάτων καὶ θεωρημάτων ὑπόληψις).

## u06-rem
- "having acquired passionless thoughts" → "having acquired dispassionate thoughts" (ἀπαθεῖς λογισμοὺς κεκτημένος).
- "a passionless thought is a sheep" → "a dispassionate thought is a sheep" (πρόβατόν ἐστιν λογισμὸς ἀπαθής).
- "those possessing passions and impasses" → "those possessing dispassionate and passionate thoughts" (τοὺς ἔχοντας λογισμοὺς ἀπαθεῖς καὶ ἐμπαθεῖς) — "impasses" was a nonsense rendering.
- "this one stores up his passion by parts… consumes his passion by parts through long-suffering" → "stores up his anger by parts… consumes his anger by parts" (ταμιεύεται τὸν θυμὸν κατὰ μέρος).
- "having acquired impassibility, it will linger in contemplation" → "having acquired dispassion, it will linger in contemplation" (ἀπάθειάν τε κτησάμενος).
- Garbled "for the passion is indeed [clothed in] practical and humble… / Wisdom, but desire [requires] temperance and self-control" → "for anger is clothed with gentleness and humility, and desire with temperance and self-control" (ἐνδέδυται γὰρ θυμὸς μὲν πραΰτητα καὶ ταπεινοφροσύνην, ἐπιθυμία δὲ σωφροσύνην καὶ ἐγκράτειαν).
