## Finding 1 — ch 1: Μαρκιανός identification (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…what sort of heresy Marcianus belonged to — who even opposed himself, not understanding what he was saying…"

**Finding:** The review observed the live site reading "Marcion" here — the famous 2nd-century heretic — which misidentifies the person and imports the wrong heresy. The Greek names Μαρκιανός, the local teacher at Rhossus whose community used the Gospel of Peter.

**Now:** "…what sort of heresy Marcian belonged to — who even opposed himself, not understanding what he was saying…"

**Why (Greek):** «ἡμεῖς δὲ, ἀδελφοί, καταλαβόμενοι ὁποίας ἦν αἱρέσεως ὁ Μαρκιανός, ὃς καὶ ἑαυτῷ ἠναντιοῦτο μὴ νοῶν ἃ ἐλάλει…» — Μαρκιανός (Marcian), not Μαρκίων (Marcion). The Air source already had the correct person ("Marcianus"); normalized to the review's "Marcian."
