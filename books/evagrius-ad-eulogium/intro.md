Evagrius Ponticus (c. 345–399) was born in Ibora in Pontus. He served as archdeacon under Gregory of Nazianzus in Constantinople, then lived as a monk in Egypt.

This English is his Treatise to Eulogius, addressed to the monk Eulogius. It is an early work, and it was long copied under the name of Nilus of Ancyra. It is not his Notes on Proverbs, and it is not his Exposition on Proverbs.

A reader meets the combat of virtue, silence, sorrow, meekness, stability, gratitude, envy, prayer, and the danger of spiritual pride.
