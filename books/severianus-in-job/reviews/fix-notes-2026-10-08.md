## Finding M-1 — §1 (u01-open [1]): person/voice collapse in God's speech (Job 40:8) (MAJOR ×2)

**Was:** "For thus, conversing with himself, he was saying: 'It seems to me that I have been used for you in another way, rather than so that you might appear righteous?'"

**Finding:** The 2nd-person question ("do you think") became 1st-person ("it seems to me"), and the active ("I have dealt") became passive ("I have been used"). God's challenge to Job became Job musing about himself.

**Now:** "For thus, conversing with himself, he was saying: 'Do you think I have dealt with you for any other reason than that you might appear righteous?'"

**Why (Greek):** «Οἴει δέ με ἄλλως σοι κεχρηματικέναι, ἀλλ' ἢ ἵνα ἀναφανῇς δίκαιος» — Οἴει (2nd sg., "do you think") + με… κεχρηματικέναι (active infinitive, "that I have dealt") + σοι ("with you"): God addressing Job. The Job 40:8 context (God speaking) confirms the person.
