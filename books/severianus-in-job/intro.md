Severianus of Gabala (fl. c. 400) was bishop of Gabala in Syria. He preached in Constantinople and became an enemy of John Chrysostom. Sermons of his, including sermons on Job, survive among works once credited to Chrysostom.

This English is those sermons on Job. It is not Didymus the Blind's commentary on Job, which is a different book. Severianus presents Job as an athlete. The devil attacks, and God permits the trial.

A reader meets Job's integrity, the devil's strategy, the silence of his friends, the purpose of the trial, and the double reward and the resurrection of his children.
