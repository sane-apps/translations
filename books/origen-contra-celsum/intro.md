Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) wrote Against Celsus in eight books. This file gives a short reading of the preface and of each book.

The preface says the Savior's silence is stronger than a spoken defense. Book 1 takes up secrecy, handmade gods, the virgin birth, and the prophets. Book 2 is the Jew of Celsus, the passion, and the empty tomb. Book 3 is the charge of factions, and the teaching of the simple. Book 4 asks whether God or the Son comes down as judge, and where evils come from.

Book 5 answers the claim that no God has come down. Book 6 sets the plain style of Scripture against Greek polish. Book 7 takes up the prophets and the Christ. Book 8 says why Christians do not serve demons.
