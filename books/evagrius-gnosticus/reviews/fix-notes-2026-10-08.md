# FIX-NOTES — evagrius-gnosticus

Both section files corrected (the book contains only u01-open and u01-rem; the "4 section pages" in the review reflects the work's structure, but only 2 files exist on disk). All 9 flagged findings fixed, plus a systematic term pass on u01-rem.

## u01-open — all 9 findings fixed

- **M-1 (ch. 3)** Was: "holds the word of salt for the impure." Finding: now "withholds the word of salt from the impure, but [offers the word] of light to the pure." Why: Greek "ὁ ἁλὸς μὲν λόγον ἐπέχων τοῖς ἀκαθάρτοις" — ἐπέχω = "hold back, restrain"; the gnostic withholds salt-words from the impure.
- **M-2 (ch. 4)** Was: "the elements." Finding: now "the subject-matters." Why: Greek τὰς ὕλας (here = subject-matters, topics), not τὰ στοιχεῖα.
- **M-3 (ch. 4)** Was: "brings to them its own words." Finding: now "admits their logoi." Why: Greek τοὺς αὐτῶν λόγους προσίεται — προσίημι = "admit, accept"; the mind receives the logoi of the realities.
- **M-4 (ch. 4)** Was: "anger and passion." Finding: now "wrath and anger." Why: Greek ὀργὴ καὶ θυμός — θυμός per the uniform convention.
- **M-5 (ch. 5)** Was: "above all is impassivity." Finding: now "above them all is freedom from anger." Why: Greek ἡ ἀοργησία ≠ ἀπάθεια; "impassivity" is a different, stronger technical term.
- **M-6 (ch. 6)** Was: "Let the Gnostic be secure in his descents." Finding: now "Let the Gnostic be on his guard in his condescensions." Why: ἀσφαλιζέσθω is a middle imperative (a task: secure oneself, be on guard), not a state.
- **M-7 (ch. 6)** Was: "descents." Finding: now "condescensions." Why: συγκαταβάσεσιν is the technical term for pastoral accommodation.
- **M-8 (ch. 6)** Was: "lest a state of descending happen to him without his noticing." Finding: now "lest the condescension, becoming a habit, escape his notice." Why: Greek "μήποτε λάθῃ αὐτὸν ἕξις γινομένη ἡ συγκατάβασις."
- **M-9 (ch. 8)** Was: "It is shameful for a Gnostic to judge both the one being wronged and the one doing wrong." Finding: now "It is shameful for a Gnostic to be judged, both the one being wronged and the one doing wrong." Why: τὸ δικάζεσθαι is a passive infinitive — the shame is in *being* judged (as litigant), not in judging.
- **Ch. 2 (term pass)** Was: "has acquired only the passive part of the soul to be impassive." Finding: now "has made only the passionate part of the soul dispassionate." Why: τὸ παθητικὸν μέρος (passionate, not passive) + ἀπαθές (dispassionate, per convention).

## u01-rem — systematic term pass
- **Ch. 31** Was: "He exhorts to respect the elders, but to restrain the bellies of the young; for the former are the spiritual, while the latter, the more excessive, are the bodily, and demons contend against them." Finding: now "Exhort the elders to restrain anger, and the young to restrain the belly; for the former are fought by the psychic demons, and the latter mostly by the bodily demons." Why: Greek "Γέροντας μὲν θυμοῦ, τοὺς δὲ νέους γαστρὸς κρατεῖν παρακάλει" — θυμοῦ is the genitive of θυμός governed by κρατεῖν ("restrain anger"), not "respect"; and "τοὺς μὲν γὰρ οἱ ψυχικοί, τοὺς δὲ ἐπὶ πλεῖστον οἱ σωματικοὶ διαμάχονται δαίμονες" = the psychic/bodily demons fight them.
- **Ch. 37** Was: "do not despise impassibility in a body that has been humbled by suffering." Finding: now "do not insult dispassion, humbling it with a fat body." Why: Greek "μὴ καθυβρίσῃς τὴν ἀπάθειαν σώματι παχεῖ ταπεινώσας αὐτήν" — ἀπάθεια = dispassion; the insult is to dispassion itself, by fattening the body.
- **Ch. 45** Was: "justice, impartiality, and mercy." Finding: now "justice, freedom from anger, and mercy." Why: Greek δικαιοσύνη καὶ ἀοργησία καὶ ἔλεος — ἀοργησία ≠ "impartiality".
- **Ch. 45** Was: "The former can even receive the passion-driven, but of the latter, only the passionless are receptive." Finding: now "The former can even receive the passionate, but of the latter, only the dispassionate are receptive." Why: τοὺς ἐμπαθεῖς / οἱ ἀπαθεῖς.
- **Ch. 47** Was: "love heals the burning fragments of the soul." Finding: now "love heals the inflamed parts of anger." Why: Greek "ἀγάπη δὲ τὰ φλεγμαίνοντα μόρια τοῦ θυμοῦ θεραπεύει" — θυμός, not "the soul".
- **Ch. 48** Was: "carry the matters of these in memory." Finding: now "carry the subject-matters of these in memory." Why: τὰς ὕλας (cf. M-2).
