Paul the Silentiary (fl. 563) was chief silentiary — a senior official of the imperial palace — under Justinian at Constantinople, and among the finest poets of his age.

His Description of the Pulpit celebrates the ambo of Hagia Sophia in 275 hexameter verses, with a 29-verse prologue: marble, silver, and carved Scripture raised into poetry, recited separately after the great church's rededication.

This volume holds the prologue and poem in full.
