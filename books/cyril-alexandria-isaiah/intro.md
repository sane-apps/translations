Cyril of Alexandria (c. 376–444), patriarch from 412, dominated the Christological controversies of his age. Alongside the polemics he produced commentaries on most of the Bible, written early in his episcopate.

His Commentary on Isaiah reads the prophet as the evangelist of the Old Testament: judgment on unfaithful Israel, comfort for the remnant, and the suffering Servant who is Christ.

This volume holds the prologue and Books 2 through 5.
