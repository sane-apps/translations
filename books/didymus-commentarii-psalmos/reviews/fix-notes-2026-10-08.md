## u09-rem-early (§34) — ὁ λόγος misreading fixed
**Was:** "for since the Logos constructs the Son to be consubstantial with the Father"
**Finding:** ὁ λόγος here is "the argument/discourse" (the commentary's own reasoning), not the divine Logos. Capitalizing it makes the Logos the agent "constructing" the Son's consubstantiality.
**Now:** "for since the argument establishes that the Son is consubstantial with the Father"
**Why:** Greek: "ἐπεὶ γὰρ κατασκευάζει ὁ λόγος ὁμοούσιον τῷ πατρὶ τὸν υἱὸν εἶναι" — ὁ λόγος is the discourse, not the divine Word.
