Gennadius of Constantinople (d. 471) was patriarch from 458. This file holds his fragments on the First and Second Epistles to the Corinthians, not a full commentary.

Paul sets the cloud and the sea beside the Corinthians as warnings. The cloud is a sign of the Spirit's grace. The crossing of the sea is what baptism has become: a defense against enemies. In baptism believers receive the Spirit of adoption and promise to live by Christ's commands.

The faith Paul names among the gifts is not the faith common to every believer. The Lord speaks of faith like a grain of mustard seed. On the second letter, Gennadius says the verse rebukes those who judge by what appears, not by works.
