Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) is the author of this reading of On First Principles. It follows the four books.

It begins from the church's rule, then argues that God is not a body, though Scripture calls him fire, spirit, and light. It speaks of Christ as Wisdom and Word, of the Holy Spirit, and of rational natures.

The later books take up free will, the opposing powers, the beginning and end of the world, and how to read the Scriptures when the letter makes the reader stumble. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
