Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403) was a Palestinian monk who became bishop of Salamis in Cyprus (367). Fierce enemy of heresy and friend of Jerome, he spent his long life cataloguing and confuting every false teaching he could find.

The Anchor of Faith (374) is his exposition of Trinitarian doctrine cast as a ship's anchor for believers tossed by Arianism: Father, Son, and Spirit confessed in unity, the church's faith set out creed by creed.

This volume holds Chapter II: the Spirit given to seekers, Peter's confession, and the Trinity in unity.
