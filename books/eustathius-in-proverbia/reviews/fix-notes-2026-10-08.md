## Finding M-1 — Frag. 75: genitive τῶν… μελῶν turned into the subject; Wisdom lost as subject (MAJOR)

**Was:** "Through the wine and the bread, the members preach the types of the physical things of Christ."

**Finding:** τῶν… μελῶν is genitive ("of the members"); the verb's subject is the implied Wisdom. The English made "the members" the subject and lost Wisdom.

**Now:** "Through the wine and the bread, [Wisdom] proclaims the antitypes of the bodily members of Christ."

**Why (Greek):** τὰ ἀντίτυπα τῶν σωματικῶν (μελῶν) τοῦ Χριστοῦ κηρύττει — τὰ ἀντίτυπα (accusative object, "the antitypes"), the genitive chain "of the bodily members of Christ," κηρύττει with implied subject Wisdom (cf. Prov 9:1–5, Wisdom's feast of bread and wine).

## Finding M-2 — Frag. 76: ἀσπαστέαν ("to be welcomed") → "as a greeting" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…proclaiming the visible wisdom and her commerce as a greeting before all things…"

**Finding:** ἀσπαστέαν is a verbal adjective ("to be welcomed, worthy of welcome"), not the noun "greeting."

**Now:** "…proclaiming the visible wisdom and her merchandise as to be welcomed before all things…"

**Why (Greek):** τήν τε ἐμπορίαν αὐτῆς <πρὸ> πάντων ἀσπαστέαν σημαίνων — "signifying her merchandise as to be welcomed before all [things]."
