## CRITICAL 1 — u05-open: αἱ ὑποστάσεις → "the substances" (ddt_u05_open_english.json, para 1)
- Was: "…so that he might also show the substances and the one divinity and will."
- Finding: αἱ ὑποστάσεις rendered "the substances" — teaches multiple divine substances.
- Now: "…so that he might also show the hypostases and the one divinity and will."
- Why: Greek 8.8 ἵνα καὶ τὰς ὑποστάσεις … δηλώσῃ.
