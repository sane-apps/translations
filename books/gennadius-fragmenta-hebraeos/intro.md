Gennadius of Constantinople (d. 471) was patriarch from 458. This file holds his fragments on the Epistle to the Hebrews, not a full commentary.

God spoke to the ancestors through the prophets, and at the end of the times he has spoken in the Son. The Son is called radiance, as light from the sun, so that no one can say the Father is older than the Son. Though he was Son, he learned obedience through what he suffered.

The fragments also set out the first teaching of the faith: repentance, faith, baptism, the laying on of hands, the resurrection, and the judgment. The lampstand, the table, and the bread of the Presence are read as signs of the stars, the earth, and the earth's produce.
