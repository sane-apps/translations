## Finding M-1 — §1: active → passive inversion (MAJOR, 180°)

**Was:** "As witnesses of faith, they destroy idols and suffer through their bodies the devil's torments, considering them as nothing."

**Finding:** The martyrs' trampling of the devil (active victory) became their suffering of it (passive victimization).

**Now:** "As witnesses of faith, they destroy idols, those who trampled the devil through the body, considering them as nothing."

**Why (Greek):** «οἱ διὰ τοῦ σώματος τὸν διάβολον πατήσαντες» — πατήσαντες (aorist active participle of πατέω, "trample") with τὸν διάβολον as direct object: "those who trampled the devil"; διὰ τοῦ σώματος = "through the body."
