Athanasius of Alexandria (c. 293/298–373) wrote this letter to Emperor Jovian in 363.

The letter presents the creed confessed by the Fathers at Nicaea as the true, apostolic faith. Athanasius explains that Arius and his followers corrupted this faith by teaching that the Son is from nothing, a creature, a thing made, and changeable.

The term catholic Church means the universal Church as opposed to heretical factions, and Holy Trinity refers to Father, Son, and Holy Spirit sharing one divinity.
