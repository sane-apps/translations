Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) preached these homilies on Isaiah and on Ezekiel. Jerome translated them. They are not the fragments of his commentary on Ezekiel, and they are not Cyril of Alexandria's commentary on Isaiah.

On Isaiah he takes up the death of Uzziah, the high throne, and the seraphim who cry holy. Ahaz will not ask, and the Lord gives Immanuel. Seven women seek one man. A coal cleanses the lips. He says here am I, send me, and behold, I and the children God gave me.

On Ezekiel he takes up the captives, the sins named so they can be healed, and Noah, Daniel, and Job. Then the sword, the two great eagles, the prince of Tyre, and the closed gate the Lord passes through.
