Didymus the Blind (c. 313–c. 398) is the teacher named on these notes. The opening is the title page of a printed set of notes on the Octateuch and the books of Kings.

The notes in this English are on the gathering of the elements, the thorns and the flaming sword, and Moses. They are not his long commentary on Genesis.

A reader meets the making of the lights, the thorns of the ground, and Moses going up. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
