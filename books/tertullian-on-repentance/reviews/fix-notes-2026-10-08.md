# FIX-NOTES — tertullian-on-repentance (Tertullian, On Repentance)

Built from site English (https://viapatrum.org/works/tertullian-on-repentance/11/). Only the flagged section (§11) is included in the corrected file; other sections are unchanged.

## Finding 1 — §11: "securium" misread as English "secure" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "We, though the desire for secure rods sustains us, doubt whether we can endure the danger of eternity?"

**Finding:** *Securium* (gen. pl. of *securis*, "axe") + *virgarumve* (gen. pl. of *virga*, "rod") = the *fasces*, the Roman magistrate's emblem. *Petitio* = candidacy for office. The sentence continues the contrast with office-seekers who endure hardships "for the fleeting joy of a single year."

**Now:** "We, though the pursuit of the fasces sustains us, doubt whether we can endure the danger of eternity?"

**Why (Latin):** «Nos, quod securium virgarumve petitio sustinet, in periculo aeternitatis tolerare dubitamus?» — *secures virgaeque* (fasces); *petitio* = canvassing for office.

## Finding 2 — §11: wrong scripture reference (MINOR, mechanical)

**Was:** "Woe to those who tie their sins together like a long rope (Jeremiah 9:4)."

**Finding:** The quotation is Isaiah 5:18 («Vae qui trahitis iniquitatem in funiculis vanitatis»), not Jeremiah 9:4.

**Now:** "Woe to those who tie their sins together like a long rope (Isaiah 5:18)."
