## Finding M-1 — §1: τρυγόνα → "Thyrsus" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…a well-clustered, flourishing, and untouched Vine; a pure Thyrsus; a stainless Dove…"

**Finding:** τρυγών is the turtledove (a standard Marian type); "Thyrsus" is a Bacchic staff — inexplicable.

**Now:** "…a well-clustered, flourishing, and untouched Vine; a pure turtledove; a stainless Dove…"

**Why (Greek):** «Τρυγόνα καθαράν» — τρυγών, -όνος = turtledove.
