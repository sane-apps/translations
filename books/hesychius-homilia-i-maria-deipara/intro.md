Hesychius of Jerusalem (c. 412–450) was a priest in Jerusalem. This English is Homily I on Saint Mary the Mother of God. It is not Homily II, which treats the Annunciation and the Magi.

The homily greets Mary as the God-bearer and gathers names for her. It then speaks of her conceiving while remaining a virgin, of Emmanuel in the manger at Bethlehem, and of her question to Gabriel.

A reader needs the angel's greeting and Mary's question how this can be, since she does not know a man. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
