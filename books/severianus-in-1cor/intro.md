Severianus of Gabala (fl. c. 400) was bishop of Gabala in Syria. He preached in Constantinople and became an enemy of John Chrysostom. Contemporaries knew him as the author of biblical commentaries and sermons.

This English is his fragments on the First Epistle to the Corinthians. The sections follow Paul's argument through the letter. They are not his sermons, which are a different body of work.

A reader meets the source of grace and calling, the power of the cross, the natural mind and the spiritual mind, the temple of God, the leaven of sin, the body, and the resurrection. The fragments do not treat every verse of the letter.
