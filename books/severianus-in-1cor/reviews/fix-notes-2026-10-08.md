# FIX-NOTES — severianus-in-1cor (Severianus of Gabala, Fragments on 1 Corinthians)

## Finding M-1 — §1 (u01-open [0]): τῦφος → "storm" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…he restrains the storm of those puffed up by words."

**Finding:** τῦφος means "pride, conceit" — not "storm" (a confusion with τυφῶν/θύελλα). The sentence concerns Paul checking the arrogance of the word-proud.

**Now:** "…he restrains the pride of those puffed up by words."

**Why (Greek):** τὸν τῦφον — τῦφος = pride/conceit (cf. 1 Tim 3:6 τετυφωμένος).

## Finding M-2 — §1 (u01-open [0]): ὁ δεῖνα → "fear and dread" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…he proposes [unity] in Jesus Christ, not in fear and dread."

**Finding:** ὁ δεῖνα is the standard "so-and-so" placeholder — "not in so-and-so and so-and-so," i.e., not named after this or that man — the point of the ecclesiological argument (named from Christ, not from men). "Fear and dread" is a false-friend invention.

**Now:** "…he proposes [unity] in Jesus Christ, not in so-and-so and so-and-so."

**Why (Greek):** «οὐκ ἐν τῷ δεῖνι καὶ τῷ δεῖνι» — ὁ/ἡ/τὸ δεῖνα = "so-and-so, such-and-such."
