# FIX-NOTES — diodorus-fragmenta-romanos (Fragments on Romans)

**Base used:** the Mac book `books/diodorus-fragmenta-romanos/translations/diofr_*_english.json` (fix target). The Mac `english` arrays are a condensed cut of the site text (e.g. Mac u01-rem-early has 4 sentences vs the site's 8+); review indices refer to the site, and every flagged phrase was content-matched into the Mac arrays (mapping noted per finding). Only files with changes emitted (8), original filenames.

**Authority per passage:** the review's recovered Greek cross-checked against the on-page Greek (OCR-scanned with intra-word spaces, but the words are recoverable — verified per passage; authority (2) per the hierarchy, since the `*_source.json` Greek was not separately consulted for this work).

## F1 — u01-rem-close §4 [25] → Mac u01-rem-close[7]: ἔπαθλα → "trials"
- **Was:** "…those who are struggling **fall into trials, the first and greatest of which was the resurrection from the dead**."
- **Now:** "…he says that prizes follow those who struggle, the first and greatest of which was the resurrection from the dead."
- **Why:** ἕπεσθαι τοῖς ἀγωνιζομένοις τὰ ἔπαθλά = "prizes follow those who struggle" — ἔπαθλα are prizes (resurrection as the first *prize*, not the first *trial*); ἕπεσθαι = "follow." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F2 — u01-rem-early §2 [13] → Mac u01-rem-early[2]: ἐλεεῖσθαι δίκαιοι → "as righteous"
- **Was:** "…these are **to be pitied by God as righteous**."
- **Now:** "…these are deserving to be pitied by God."
- **Why:** οὗτοι ἐλεεῖσθαι δίκαιοι παρὰ τοῦ θεοῦ = "these are deserving to be pitied by God" — δίκαιοι is the predicate of desert, not a declaration of the sinners' righteousness. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F3 — u01-rem-mid §3 [5] → Mac u01-rem-mid[0]: ἔνια → "years"
- **Was:** "…**there are years among other nations where one lives as good and lawful**, among others as lawless and being treated badly."
- **Now:** "…there are some things among other nations that are lived as good and lawful, among others as lawless and ill-treated."
- **Why:** ἔτι ἔνια παρ' ἄλλοις ἔθνεσιν ὡς καλὰ καὶ ἔννομα πολιτεύεται — ἔνια = "some things" (neuter plural); the point is some practices count as lawful among some nations and lawless among others. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F4 — u01-rem-mid §3 [19] → Mac u01-rem-mid[2]: ἐνεκροῦτο → "struck against"
- **Was:** "…**sin struck against the ignorance of those sinning**…"
- **Now:** "…sin was dead through the ignorance of those sinning…"
- **Why:** ἐνεκροῦτο ἡ ἁμαρτία = imperfect of νεκρόομαι, "sin was dead" (cf. Rom 7:8, ἁμαρτία νεκρά). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F5 — u01-rem-mid §3 [31] → Mac u01-rem-mid[6]: μετιέναι → "abandon"
- **Was:** "…the evils, **are permitted to me** for the purpose of doing them. And through this **'are permitted', he shows that it is not idle to abandon evil**."
- **Now:** "…for the evils, being more ready, lie before me for the doing. And through this 'lie before,' he shows that it is not toilsome to pursue evil."
- **Why:** διὰ δὲ τοῦ παράκειται δείκνυσιν ὡς οὐκ ἐργῶδές ἐστι μετιέναι τὴν κακίαν — παράκειται = "lie before," μετιέναι = "to pursue"; the point is evil is easy to pursue — the opposite of the base. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F6 — u02-open §5 [9] → Mac u02-open[2]: συναλγεῖν καὶ συνάχθεσθαι → "physically joined"
- **Was:** "To suffer **does not mean to be physically joined or to be mixed with** the one who has suffered…"
- **Now:** "To suffer with does not mean to feel pain together and be troubled together with the one who has suffered…"
- **Why:** τὸ συμπάσχειν οὐ τὸ συναλγεῖν καὶ συνάχθεσθαι τῷ πεπονθότι δηλοῖ — nothing about physical joining. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F7 — u02-open §5 [10] → Mac u02-open[2]: ἀμύνασθαι → "defend those who grieved"
- **Was:** "…nor did he grow weak, **desiring to defend those who grieved**."
- **Now:** "…nor did he grow weak, desiring to retaliate against those who grieved him."
- **Why:** οὐδὲ ἠσθένει βουληθεὶς ἀμύνασθαι τοὺς λυπήσαντας — ἀμύνασθαι = "to ward off, retaliate against"; Christ did not retaliate against those who grieved him, not defend them. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F8 — u02-open §5 [15] → Mac u02-open[3]: τῶν φθασάντων ἁμαρτημάτων → "those who have overcome sins"
- **Was:** "…the greatest gift is **the amnesty granted to those who have overcome sins**."
- **Now:** "…the gift is the amnesty granted for the sins that went before."
- **Why:** ἡ δωρεὰ τῶν φθασάντων ἁμαρτημάτων ἀμνηστείαν χαρίζεται — "the gift grants amnesty for the sins that went before" (past sins covered, not people who "overcame" sin). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F9 — u02-rem-close §8 [16] → Mac u02-rem-mid[4]: μὴ ἐρεῖ → "Does not... say?"
- **Was:** "'**Does not the thing formed say** to the one who formed it: Why did you make me thus?' (Rom 9:19-20)."
- **Now:** "'The thing formed shall not say to the one who formed it: Why did you make me thus?' (Rom 9:19-20)."
- **Why:** μὴ ἐρεῖ τὸ πλάσμα τῷ πλάσαντι; — a denial (per Rom 9:20), not a question affirming the speech. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F10 — u02-rem-early §6 [6] → Mac u02-rem-early[2]: ἄκουσα → "having heard"
- **Was:** "…since, **having heard the arrangement of the devil, it is worshipped by his fellow-slaves**…"
- **Now:** "…since by the devil's contrivance it is unwillingly worshipped by its fellow-slaves…"
- **Why:** ἄκουσα (unwilling) was read as ἀκούσα (having heard) — creation is worshipped *unwillingly*, the opposite. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F11 — u02-rem-early §6 [7] → Mac u02-rem-early[2]: ἀκουσίως → "false hearing"
- **Was:** "Thus, enjoying the honor of the master's **[false] hearing, the happening bears down heavily**…"
- **Now:** "Thus, unwillingly enjoying the master's honor, it bears what happens heavily…"
- **Why:** τῆς δεσποτικῆς ἀκουσίως ἀπολαύουσα τιμῆς βαρέως φέρει τὸ γινόμενον — ἀκουσίως = "unwillingly"; "false hearing" is nonsense. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F12 — u02-rem-mid §7 [22] → Mac u02-rem-mid[4]: περισσός → "is abundant"
- **Was:** "**And the one striving is abundant** (Rom 9:17)."
- **Now:** "And the one who strives is superfluous (Rom 9:17)."
- **Why:** καὶ περισσός ἐστιν ὁ σπουδάζων — περισσός here = "needless, superfluous" (mercy apart from God). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F13 — u03-open §9 [10] → Mac u03-open[3]: ἐκπέτασις → "falling away"
- **Was:** "It seems that **through the falling away of the hands, the people calls to himself** by stretching out the hands toward them…"
- **Now:** "He seems through the outstretching of his hands to call the people to himself, and through the same he shows the form of the cross (Rom 10:21)."
- **Why:** ∆οκεῖ διὰ τῆς ἐκπετάσεως τῶν χειρῶν τὸν λαὸν πρὸς ἑαυτὸν καλεῖν — ἐκπέτασις = "spreading out" (the outstretched hands of Rom 10:21 / Isa 65:2); "falling away" is the opposite image, and subject/object were inverted. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F14 — u03-open §9 [13] → Mac u03-open[5]: seven thousand → "seventy thousand"
- **Was:** "…not in a great multitude, **but in seventy thousand men** (Rom 11:5)…"
- **Now:** "…but in seven thousand men (Rom 11:5)…"
- **Why:** ἑπτακισχίλιοι = 7,000 (the remnant of Rom 11:4). Numeral error, factor of ten. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F15 — u03-open §9 [20] → Mac u03-open[7]: συγχώρησις → "forgiveness"
- **Was:** "It is the custom of Scripture to refer matters **concerning the forgiveness of God** happening to men **to the One who forgives** (Rom 11:8)."
- **Now:** "It is the custom of Scripture to refer to the one who permits the things that happen to men by God's permission (Rom 11:8)."
- **Why:** συγχώρησις = "permission/concession," not forgiveness (συγγνώμη/ἄφεσις) — the fragment explains how Scripture ascribes to God what he merely *permits*. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F16 — u03-open §9 [21] → Mac u03-open[7]: συγχωρεῖ → "forgives"
- **Was:** "**And God forgives according to each person's impulse to move toward what He wills**, so that they might not seem to seize freedom by force."
- **Now:** "And God permits each one's impulse to go where it wills, so that they might not seem to seize freedom by force."
- **Why:** συγχωρεῖ δὲ ὁ θεὸς τῇ ἑκάστου ὁρμῇ χωρεῖν ἐφ' ὃ βούλεται — God *allows* each impulse its course so as not to override freedom; "forgives" garbles the theodicy point. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F17 — u03-rem-close §12 [5] → Mac u03-rem-close[2]: Jn 9:4 misquoted
- **Was:** "'Until it is day, **work the works of light**; night comes when no one is able to work' (John 9:4)"
- **Now:** "'While it is day, work the works of him who sent me; night comes when no one is able to work' (John 9:4)"
- **Why:** ἕως ἡμέρα ἐστὶν ἐργάζεσθε τὰ ἔργα τοῦ πέμψαντός με — "the works of *him who sent me*," not "of light." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F18/F19 — u03-rem-close §12 [13]/[27] → Mac u03-rem-close[4]/[7]: βρῶμα → "filth"
- **Was:** "…the enjoyment of **filth**…" / "…the one touching **the filth** with faith…"
- **Now:** "…the enjoyment of foods…" / "…the one who with faith touches the foods…"
- **Why:** βρῶμα = "food" (Rom 14); "filth" was invented. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F20 — u03-rem-early §10 [21] → Mac u03-rem-early[5]: ἵνα μὴ μετασχόντες → "not participating"
- **Was:** "…so that, **not participating in grace and having made signs**, they might not be puffed up by grace and fall from the gift (Rom 12:3)."
- **Now:** "…lest, having partaken of grace and performed signs, they be puffed up by grace and fall from the gift (Rom 12:3)."
- **Why:** ἵνα μὴ μετασχόντες τῆς χάριτος — "lest, *having partaken* of grace"; negating the participle produced the incoherent "not participating in grace... puffed up by grace." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F21 — u03-rem-mid §11 [6] → Mac u03-rem-mid[2]: direction reversed
- **Was:** "…but **the law of piety is hated by us from the ungodly**, or [is directed] toward the ungodly."
- **Now:** "…but the institution of piety draws hatred upon us from the ungodly."
- **Why:** ἀλλ' ὁ τῆς εὐσεβείας θεσμὸς μῖσος ἡμῖν παρὰ τῶν ἀσεβῶν ἐπισπᾶται — piety *draws hatred upon us*; the English made us the haters. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F22 — u03-rem-mid §11 [12] → Mac u03-rem-mid[4]: ἀμύνασθαι to the wrong beneficiary
- **Was:** "…and the law, **by giving defense to those who have done wrong**, appears more excellent than [mere] authority…"
- **Now:** "…and the law, by granting retaliation to those wronged, appears more gentle than [mere] authority…"
- **Why:** καὶ νόμου διδόντος ἀμύνασθαι τοὺς ἀδικηθέντας — the law grants *retaliation* (ἀμύνασθαι) to those *wronged*; the English gave "defense to those who have done wrong" — wrong act and wrong beneficiary (reviewer's text-critical reading; the printed τοὺς ἀδικήσαντας is treated as corrupt). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F23 — u03-rem-mid §11 [18] → Mac u03-rem-mid[6]: ἀνελόντος → "having lifted up"
- **Was:** "Therefore, **having lifted up the continuous wars of God**…"
- **Now:** "Therefore, God having removed the continuous wars…"
- **Why:** ἀνελόντος τοίνυν τοῦ θεοῦ τοὺς συνεχεῖς πολέμους — ἀναιρεῖν τοὺς πολέμους = "to remove/abolish the wars"; "lifted up" reverses the providential point. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F24 — u03-rem-mid §11 [19] → Mac u03-rem-mid[6]: ἀπόνοια → "intellect"
- **Was:** "…in the present life, **it has a dangerous consequence for one's intellect**."
- **Now:** "…in the present life, it is dangerous to have folly."
- **Why:** ἐν τῷ παρόντι βίῳ ἐπικίνδυνον ἔχειν τὴν ἀπόνοιαν — ἀπόνοια = "folly, madness," nearly the antonym of "intellect." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F25 — u04-rem-mid §15 [2] → Mac u04-rem-mid[2]: συνουσία → "conscience"
- **Was:** "…so that they might instruct them **by their own conscience**."
- **Now:** "…so that they might instruct them by their dwelling together with them."
- **Why:** ἵνα τῇ ἑαυτῶν συνουσίᾳ παιδεύωσιν αὐτούς — συνουσία = "living/dwelling together" (the exiled Jews instructed the nations by living among them); "conscience" is συνείδησις. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.
