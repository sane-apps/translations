Evagrius Ponticus (c. 345–399) wrote this instruction for monks. It is not The Practice, and it is not the Sentences to Monks.

The counsels move from an exhortation to virtue and prayer, then to the fruits of the Spirit and the Trinity, and then to righteousness and obedience.

A reader meets the claim that faith is kept by prayer and by a righteous life. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
