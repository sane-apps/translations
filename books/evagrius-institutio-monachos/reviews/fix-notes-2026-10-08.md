## Per-finding notes

### M-1 — §1
- **Was:** "Stinginess restrains the love of money"
- **Finding:** ἀκτημοσύνη = "non-possession" (the virtue); "stinginess" is a vice
- **Now:** "Non-possession restrains the love of money"
- **Why:** Φιλαργυρίαν… συστέλλει ἀκτημοσύνη — ἀκτημοσύνη is the virtue of non-possession; rendering it by a vice inverts the maxim.

### M-2 — §1
- **Was:** "Long-suffering and psalmody curb the rushing of passion."
- **Finding:** Θυμοῦ… καταδρομὴν — θυμός is the technical term, καταδρομή = "onset/charge"
- **Now:** "Long-suffering and psalmody soothe the onset of anger."
- **Why:** θυμός→"anger" per the cross-work convention (the standing Evagrian error was flattening it to "passion"); καταδρομή = "onset, charge".

### M-3 — §1
- **Was:** "'I am dust and ashes' (Ps 103:14)"
- **Finding:** the saying is Abraham's in Gen 18:27; Ps 103:14 is a different verse ("he remembers that we are dust")
- **Now:** "'I am dust and ashes' (Gen 18:27)"
- **Why:** Ἐγώ εἰμι γῆ καὶ σποδός = Abraham's words in Gen 18:27 LXX.

### M-4 — §1
- **Was:** "while grasping at fleshly thinking."
- **Finding:** κατακρατέω = "prevail, hold sway", not "grasping at"
- **Now:** "while the fleshly mind prevails."
- **Why:** κατακρατοῦντος τοῦ σαρκικοῦ φρονήματος — genitive absolute, "while the fleshly mind prevails/holds sway".
