Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) preached these homilies on Exodus. Rufinus translated them. They are not his notes on Exodus, and they are not his selections on Exodus.

He takes up the midwives, Moses in the basket, and the plagues. The rod strikes the sea. Horse and rider are thrown in. After the song comes Marah, and the wood sweetens the water. Manna falls morning by morning.

Then the words "no other gods," the veil on Moses' face, and the tabernacle made from willing offerings. Amalek attacks, and the hands that grow heavy need companions.
