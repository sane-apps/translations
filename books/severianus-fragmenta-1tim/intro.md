Severianus of Gabala (fl. c. 400) was bishop of Gabala. This English is the fragments on the First Letter to Timothy transmitted under his name. It is not John Chrysostom's homilies on that letter, and it is not the fragments under Photius or Oecumenius.

The excerpts speak of one Christ from two natures, one hypostasis, and one nature of the incarnate Word, consubstantial with the Father and with us. As the text stands, one excerpt names the children of Nestorius and rejects the claim that Christ is not truly God after becoming man.

The other pieces take up the economy of Christ, the salvation of women, desire and conduct, and the manifestation of sins and good works. Paul is addressing Timothy, and the citations use the old names of the biblical books.
