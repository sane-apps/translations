Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) wrote this commentary on Matthew. This file is books 15 through 17, and the later series on the passion.

The Greek books take up the eunuchs, the rich man and the camel, the laborers in the vineyard, and the mother of Zebedee's sons. They go on to the fig tree, the wicked tenants, Caesar's coin, and the Sadducees' question about the resurrection.

The series then follows Peter's promise and denial, Gethsemane, Judas and the crowd, the title on the cross, and the guards after the rising. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
