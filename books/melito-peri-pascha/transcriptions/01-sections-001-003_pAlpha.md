## Notes

- The argument is already visible in the surviving words: τὸ πρόβατον,
  τὸ μυστήριον, ὁ τοῦ πάσχα λόγος, τὸν μὲν ... τὸν δέ. Reconstruction of the
  lost wording is NOT attempted here; it belongs to the collation pass, and
  anything the parallel witness (C) cannot support stays bracketed.
- This page, with p. Β (canvas 058), is the material published in 2017 as
  the recovered first leaf of Bodmer XIII (Nongbri & Hall, JTS 2017) per the
  route report; the canvases are served by the Bodmer Lab viewer as part of
  the codex sequence.
