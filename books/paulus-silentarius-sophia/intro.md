Paul the Silentiary (fl. 563), senior palace official under Justinian and the court's laureate in all but name, was chosen to give the rebuilt Hagia Sophia its poetic voice.

His Description of Hagia Sophia — 1,046 hexameters for the church's rededication in December 563, after the new dome rose over the city — walks the reader through nave and dome, exedras and piers: the great church as a meadow of marble.

This volume holds the poem complete in its three movements.
