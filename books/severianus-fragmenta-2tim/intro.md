Severianus of Gabala (fl. c. 400) is the author of these fragments on the Second Letter to Timothy. They are not John Chrysostom's homilies on that letter.

The fragments take up why one letter to Timothy is given by command and the other according to the promise of life, and Paul's charge to share in suffering and to preach the word in season and out of season.

A reader meets Paul writing to Timothy, and the plain sense of preaching at the right time and at the wrong time. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
