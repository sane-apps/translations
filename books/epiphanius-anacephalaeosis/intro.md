Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403) wrote this summary of his larger work against heresies.

Epiphanius explains that the first book contains three volumes and forty-six heresies, the second book contains two volumes and twenty-three heresies, and the third book contains two volumes and eleven heresies.

He names Barbarism, Scythianism, Hellenism, Judaism, and Samaritanism among the first book's heresies, and states that the first volume of the first book contains twenty heresies, beginning with Barbarism, Scythianism, Hellenism, and Judaism before the list breaks off.
