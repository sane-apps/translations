Hesychius of Jerusalem (c. 412–450) preached this second homily on the holy Pascha. It is not Homily I on the holy Pascha.

A trumpet filled by Bethlehem and ignited by Zion gathers the hearers. The tomb is a bridal bed that buried the bridegroom and raised the bride. The soldiers guard him as dead, and the gatekeepers of Hades see him as God.

He is the same one, not another and another. The Logos who became flesh is not separated from the flesh. The Nazarene who was crucified is not here, for he has risen, and Hosea speaks of the third day.
