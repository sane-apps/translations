# FIX-NOTES — apollinaris-fragmenta-matthaeum (Fragments on Matthew)

**Base used:** the work's book is not present on the Mac (`books/` has no `apollinaris-fragmenta-matthaeum`), so the fix base is the on-page English from viapatrum.org as captured in the translation-review site crawl (`~/workspace/scratch/c7-review/works_json/apollinaris-fragmenta-matthaeum.json`), whose sentence text matches the review's quoted English verbatim. Output JSONs use the Mac `*_english.json` schema (same keys/order); `baehrens`/`claim` strings are inferred from the psalmos pattern and `title` is the page title — flag to parent for normalization.

**Authority per passage:** the review's recovered Greek cross-checked against the on-page Greek (OCR-garbled). Where the page Greek was too garbled to verify, the review's Greek is the authority — noted per finding.

## F1 — u01-open §1 (Mt 1:16) [15]: editorial brackets mishandled
- **Was:** "…as the mother of Ephraim and **the father of Benjamin**…"
- **Now:** "Rachel is remembered by the prophet as the mother of <the father of> Ephraim, and she is mother also of Benjamin, of whose portion toward Jerusalem is the so-called Ramah."
- **Why:** ἡ δὲ Ῥαχὴλ ὡς μήτηρ τοῦ <πατρὸς τοῦ> Ἐφραὶμ… ἔστι δὲ μήτηρ καὶ τοῦ Βενιαμίν — the <…> marks an editorial insertion; the old text dropped "mother of" before it and attached "the father" to Benjamin. Authority: review Greek + page Greek (brackets visible).

## F2 — u01-rem-close §4 (Mt 10:10) [4]: τὸ πρᾶον → "the staff"
- **Was:** "…so **if you have the staff**, do not fear the rod…"
- **Now:** "…so if you have gentleness, do not fear the rod, which Paul holds up against the Corinthians."
- **Why:** τὸ πρᾶον = "gentleness" (cf. 1 Cor 4:21, rod vs. spirit of gentleness); the sentence contrasts rod and gentleness. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F3 — u01-rem-close §4 (Mt 10:11–15) [7]: παρῃτοῦντο → "entreated"
- **Was:** "…but those calling for a kingdom **entreated them**."
- **Now:** "…but those who called them to the kingdom were refused."
- **Why:** παρῃτοῦντο (middle of παραιτέομαι) = "they refused" — the cities refused those calling them to the kingdom; "entreated" is the opposite. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F4 — u01-rem-close §4 (Mt 10:29) [12]: συγχώρησις → "forgiveness"
- **Was:** "…but **not without God's forgiveness**…"
- **Now:** "…but not without God's permission, allowing toward a useful end, and these to the one choosing to work them out, just as I said concerning Job."
- **Why:** συγχώρησις here = "permission, concession" (the sparrows fall not without God's permission). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F5 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Mt 8:29) [4]: διὰ σαρκώσεως → "through the flesh of your flesh"
- **Was:** "…because Christ is about to come into the world **through the flesh of your flesh**…"
- **Now:** "…because Christ is about to come into the world through becoming flesh and because he is about to judge them in the consummation."
- **Why:** διὰ σαρκώσεως = "through incarnation." Page Greek too garbled to verify (anchor not found); review Greek is the authority.

## F6 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Mt 8:34) [8]: τηλικοῦτον → "such a small"
- **Was:** "Seeing **such a small sign**, the Gergesenes…"
- **Now:** "Seeing so great a sign, the Gergesenes entreat Jesus to depart from their borders (Mt 8:34), because they had no desire for the good."
- **Why:** τηλικοῦτον = "so great" — the opposite. Page Greek too garbled to verify; review Greek is the authority.

## F7 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Mt 8:34) [10]: διέκειντο → "dividing"
- **Was:** "…but were **lazily dividing** and sluggishly toward the grasping of the great good."
- **Now:** "Because they had no desire for the good, but were disposed lazily and sluggishly toward grasping the great good."
- **Why:** διέκειντο (imperf. of διάκειμαι) = "were disposed." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F8 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Mt 9:16–17) [13]: ἀσκοί → "fasts"
- **Was:** "And **the fasts** are old and the old men…"
- **Now:** "And the wineskins are old, and the old men; but new wine is the new Word, which they cannot contain…"
- **Why:** ἀσκοί = "wineskins" — the parable's own image. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F9 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Mt 9:16–17) [15]: εἰς μάτην → "for destruction"
- **Was:** "…only as much as it advanced toward them **for destruction**."
- **Now:** "not much, but only as far as it made headway among them, in vain."
- **Why:** εἰς μάτην = "in vain" (ἀλλ' ὅσον ἐπ' αὐτοῖς εἰς μάτην ἐχώρησεν). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F10 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Mt 10:9–10) [24]: ἀφρόντιδας → "prudent"
- **Was:** "…to be entirely free and **prudent**…"
- **Now:** "…to be entirely free and free from care, not being humbled by care for any single thing of the body (Mt 10:9-10)."
- **Why:** ἀφρόντις = "free from care"; "prudent" (φρόνιμος) is the opposite. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F11 — u02-rem-early §6 (Mt 12:31–32) [2]: τὸν καταρώμενον θεὸν → "the condemned God"
- **Was:** "…the law commands **the condemned God** to die…"
- **Now:** "For when the law commands the one who curses God to be put to death, and the Lord is held against the law, it does not grant forgiveness for such a thing."
- **Why:** καταράομαι = "curse" (Lev 24), not "be condemned." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F12 — u02-rem-early §6 (Mt 12:43–45) [11]: ἱππήλατος → "a rider"
- **Was:** "…the evil and reckless man **is a rider for the evil spirit** regarding the good."
- **Now:** "On the contrary, the evil man, reckless toward the good, is ridden by the evil spirit."
- **Why:** ἱππήλατος = "ridden upon" — rider and ridden were reversed. Page Greek too garbled to verify; review Greek is the authority.

## F13 — u02-rem-early §6 (Mt 12:43–45) [17]: ἐξήμαρτον → "had failed"
- **Was:** "…when **it had failed against the one who was called**…"
- **Now:** "After these things, when they sinned against the one who had called them, then the grace passed over to the Gentiles, and they were given over."
- **Why:** ἐξήμαρτον = "they sinned" (ὅτε ἐξήμαρτον εἰς τὸν προσκαλεσάμενον). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F14 — u02-rem-early §6 (Mt 12:43–45) [19]: ἐφόδου → "exodus"
- **Was:** "…and with a great multitude of **the exodus**."
- **Now:** "But the return to Israel is easier than before, and with a great multitude of the assault."
- **Why:** ἔφοδος = "attack, onset" (confused with ἔξοδος). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F15 — u03-open §9 (Mt 19:4) [12]: τὸν κτίσαντα υἱόν → "the created Son"
- **Was:** "…nor indeed separating **the created Son** from the creating Father…"
- **Now:** "…nor indeed separating the creating Son from the creating Father…"
- **Why:** τὸν κτίσαντα (aor. act. ptc.) = "the creating Son" — the fragment's whole point is the Son's co-creatorship; the passive is an Arian-sounding inversion. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F16 — u03-rem-early §10 (Mt 19:14) [8]: νηπιάζετε → "be sober"
- **Was:** "…'do not become children in mind, but **in wickedness be sober**' (1 Cor 14:20)."
- **Now:** "…'do not become children in mind, but in wickedness be babes' (1 Cor 14:20)."
- **Why:** νηπιάζετε = "be babes." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F17 — u04-open §13 (Mt 21:2) [5]: μονῶνυξ → "nocturnal"
- **Was:** "…because the donkey is **nocturnal** and does not chew the cud."
- **Now:** "…because the donkey is single-hoofed and does not chew the cud."

## F18 — u04-rem-close §16 (Mt 24:27–28) [2]: δορυφοροῦντα → "wielding spears"
- **Was:** "…and angels in host, **wielding spears**, shall be manifest…"
- **Now:** "…and troops of angels attending as his guard shall appear…"
- **Why:** δορυφορεῖν = "to attend as bodyguard" — the escort function, not spear-wielding. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F19 — u04-rem-close §16 (Mt 24:27–28) [4]: παράδεισον → "the parable"

- **Why:** παράδεισον = "paradise" (confused with παραβολήν); τὸν παράδεισον ἀπολείψονται. Page Greek too garbled to verify; review Greek is the authority.

## F20 — u04-rem-close §16 (Mt 24:31) [5]: subject/object inverted
- **Was:** "…the Son of Man will send, saying, his angels, **God showed the Son of Man**…"
- **Now:** "Regarding Matthew 24:31: ‘the Son of Man will send his angels,’ he says—this showed the Son of Man to be God; for angels are of God, and to send them is God's own."
- **Why:** the sending proves (ἔδειξε) the Son of Man [to be] God. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F21 — u04-rem-early §14 (Mt 22:16) [17]: reversed

- **Now:** "…saying that Herod is the Christ…"
- **Why:** οἱ τὸν Ἡρώδην Χριστὸν εἶναι λέγοντες = "those saying that Herod is the Christ" (the Herodians). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F22 — u04-rem-early §14 (Mt 23:15) [25]: ἰουδαΐσαι → "baptized"
- **Was:** "…whether the Gentiles, **before they were baptized**, were not sons of Gehenna."
- **Now:** "…whether the Gentiles, before they judaized, were not sons of Gehenna."
- **Why:** ἰουδαΐζω = "to judaize, live as a Jew" (≠ βαπτίζω). Page Greek too garbled to verify; review Greek is the authority.

## F23 — u04-rem-early §14 (Mt 22:1–14) [4]: κακῶς → "wickedly"
- **Was:** "…and **having destroyed them wickedly** (Mt 22:1-14)."
- **Now:** "…having suffered by them and having risen and having destroyed them miserably (Mt 22:1-14)."
- **Why:** κακῶς ἀπολλύναι (Mt 21:41) = "to destroy miserably"; "wickedly" attributes wickedness to the judgment itself. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F24 — u05-open §17 (Mt 26:24) [3]: γεγεννῆσθαι → "come to execution"
- **Was:** "…the good thing is **not to have come to execution** (Mt 26:24)…"
- **Now:** "It has been said that the good thing, with respect to the punishment, is not to have been born (Mt 26:24); for non-existence is preferable to a bad existence."
- **Why:** τὸ μὴ γεγεννῆσθαι = "not to have been born." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F25 — u05-open §17 (Mt 26:26) [13]: πρηνὴς/ἐλάκησεν mistranslated + wrong verse
- **Was:** "…'**having become rash he struck**' (John 13:27)…"
- **Now:** "And the Acts of the Apostles declare this, that 'having fallen headlong, he burst asunder' (Acts 1:18), and what follows."
- **Why:** πρηνὴς = "headlong, face-down"; ἐλάκησεν = "burst asunder"; the quotation is Acts 1:18, not John 13:27. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F26 — u05-open §17 (Mt 26:26) [14]: πρησθείς → "become so much a part of the flesh"
- **Was:** "…for **having become so much a part of the flesh**, such that he could not easily pass through **the narrowness**, it is said that his entrails were spilled out **by the narrowness**."
- **Now:** "But Papias, the disciple of John the apostle, recounts this more clearly, saying: ‘A great example of impiety walked about in this world—Judas; for having become so swollen in the flesh that he could not pass where a wagon easily passes, it is said that, having stumbled against the wagon, his entrails were emptied out.’"
- **Why:** re-derived from πρησθεὶς γὰρ ἐπὶ τοσοῦτον τὴν σάρκα, ὥστε μὴ δύνασθαι διελθεῖν ἁμάξης ῥᾳδίως διερχομένης, <λέγεται> ὑπὸ τῆς ἁμάξης πταισθέντα τὰ ἔγκατα ἐκκενωθῆναι (Papias on Judas): πρησθείς = "having become swollen"; "the narrowness" was invented. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F27 — u05-rem-close §20 (Mt 27:52–54) [3]: οὐκ ἔλαθεν → "did not perceive"
- **Was:** "…thus even the Jews **did not perceive**, and in this very passion and dishonor of the Savior, **the divine excess was not perceived**…"
- **Now:** "…thus the divine transcendence did not escape the notice even of the Jews, even in this very passion and dishonor of the Savior, but the usual forgetfulness of men weighed them down and the deception of the teachers led many astray."
- **Why:** οὐκ ἔλαθεν = "did not escape notice" — the divine excess WAS noticed by the Jews even in the passion; the old text inverted it. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F28 — u05-rem-close §20 (Mt 28:11–15) [9]: invented detail
- **Was:** "…reported to the chief priests, **beating their breasts terribly**, and they spoke of things that did not happen."
- **Now:** "…but the soldiers, having received sufficient silver—what they had seen they reported to the chief priests in advance, but the marvels they kept silent—and they spoke of things that did not happen."
- **Why:** τεράστια ἐσιώπησαν = "the marvels they kept silent"; "beating their breasts terribly" was invented (τεράστια misread as "terribly"). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F29 — u05-rem-mid §19 (Mt 27:28–30) [0]: scarlet cord garbled
- **Was:** "…this robe, which is **the red garment of Joshua in Jesus**, and the one who '**stretched out** his hand' being one of the sons of Judah from Tamar…"
- **Now:** "For this reason his killers clothed him, not understanding what they were doing—or rather, this robe is a symbol of the scarlet thread in the time of Joshua son of Nun, and of the one who bound ‘the hand,’ one of the sons of Judah by Tamar: a sign that the Savior took up the bloods of the world and of the men in it, for the salvation of all."
- **Why:** re-derived from τοῦ ἐν Ἰησοῦ τοῦ Ναυῆ κοκκίνου ῥάμματος (= Rahab's cord, Josh 2) καὶ τοῦ ἐνδήσαντος «τὴν χεῖρα» (= Zerah, Gen 38); ἐνδήσαντος = "having bound," not "stretched out." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F30 — u05-rem-mid §19 (Mt 27:45) [5]: ὄνειδος → "dream" + wrong Psalm
- **Was:** "'**I gave you a dream of a fool**; I was made mute and did not open my mouth, **(Psalm 22:2)**"
- **Now:** "'You made me a reproach to the fool; I was made mute and did not open my mouth, because it is you who made me' (Ps 38:9 LXX),"
- **Why:** ὄνειδος = "reproach" (confused with ὄνειρος, "dream"); the quotation is Ps 38:9 LXX (ὄνειδος ἄφρονι ἔδωκάς με), not Ps 22:2. The current site text truncates the quotation mid-verse; the full verse is restored from the Greek. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F31 — u05-rem-mid §19 (Mt 27:45) [10]: μιαιφόνους → "hybrids"
- **Was:** "…a sign of what **the hybrids** will take in the future…"
- **Now:** "For perhaps they thought him to be the sun—for that darkness was of wrath, and a sample of what the murderers were going to receive; for this is why, after all the acts of insult, this happens, when they had taken their fill of mockery and said all they wished."
- **Why:** μιαιφόνος = "blood-guilty, murderer"; re-derived from εἴτε γὰρ αὐτὸν ἐνόμιζον… ὀργιζομένου γὰρ ἦν τὸ σκότος ἐκεῖνο καὶ δεῖγμα τοῦ μέλλοντος λαβεῖν τοὺς μιαιφόνους…. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

- **u01-rem-mid §3 [18]:** "not to show that it is necessary" → "that we must not make a show of it" (τὸ μὴ δεῖν ἐπιδείκνυσθαι).
- **u02-rem-early §6 (Mt 12:43–45) [12]:** "(Ps 99:2)" → "(Isa 57:15)" for «ἅγιος ἐν ἁγίοις ἀναπαυόμενος».
