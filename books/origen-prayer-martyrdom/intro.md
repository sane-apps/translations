Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) wrote for his patron Ambrose — the wealthy convert who funded Origen's whole enterprise — as readily as he preached to the church at Caesarea.

On Prayer (c. 233) is the first Christian treatise on prayer: why we pray, to whom, and how — closing with a full exposition of the Lord's Prayer. The Exhortation to Martyrdom (235) urges Ambrose and Protoctetus to stand firm as Maximin's persecution begins.

This volume holds the complete surviving text of both treatises.
