## Charter

- Title: On the Glory of the Martyrs (Liber in Gloria Martyrum)
- Author: Gregory of Tours (d. 594)
- Scope: the whole book (short miracle chapters, cap. 1 to the end). Chapter 4 was
  locked and translated first (2026-10-20, Spark) because the Timeline dossier
  mary-s-life-end-death (question 67) quotes it: it is the first Latin account of
  Mary's body taken to paradise.
- Source: Bordier's edition, tome 1 (Paris, 1857) — Ruinart's text collated on new
  manuscripts, with a facing French translation. Public domain; the University of
  Toronto scan is openly posted on archive.org. Chapter division matches Krusch's
  MGH SRM 1.2 (cap. IV in both editions). The standard critical edition (Krusch,
  MGH SRM 1.2, 1885) could not be used: the openMGH digitisation family was not
  lawfully reachable in this run, and no other open scan of SRM 1.2 was found
  (HathiTrust and archive.org hold the series but posted no open copy of 1.2).
  The substitution is stated in the Timeline entry itself.
- Legal: private study; new English from allowed sources; no modern copyrighted English copy.
