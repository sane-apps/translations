Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403) is the name on this prayer and exorcism. The text is a short formula preserved under his name.

The speaker calls on God Almighty, the Lord Jesus Christ, the heavenly king, and the maker of heaven and earth. The voice then commands the unclean spirit.

The command tells the spirit to fear the one clothed with light as with a garment, the only one who has immortality and unapproachable light. The fragment ends with glory and dominion to God forever. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
