## Finding 1 — §(u02-open) [0]: broken syntax "if some sophistry them" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…for those who believe from signs are easily moved if some sophistry them that the signs did not happen rightly."

**Finding:** σοφίσωνται (aorist subjunctive of σοφίζομαι, "to use sophistry / persuade by sophistry") has τινὲς ("certain people") as its subject and αὐτοὺς ("them") as its object. The English dropped the verb entirely and forced the noun "sophistry" into the verb slot, producing an ungrammatical sentence that obscures the subject–verb–object relation.

**Now:** "…for those who believe because of signs are easily shifted, if some men persuade them with sophistry that the signs did not happen rightly."

**Why (Greek):** οἱ γὰρ ἀπὸ σημείων πιστεύοντες εὐχερῶς μετατίθενται, ἐάν τινες αὐτοὺς σοφίσωνται, ὅτι οὐκ ὀρθῶς γεγόνασι τὰ σημεῖα — "for those who believe because of signs are easily shifted, if certain people persuade them with sophistry that the signs did not happen rightly." μετατίθενται = "are shifted/moved (from one position)," τινὲς αὐτοὺς σοφίσωνται = "certain men deceive them by sophistry."
