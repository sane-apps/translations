Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) preached these homilies on Luke. Jerome translated them.

They begin with the many who tried to write a gospel, and with the four who were kept. They follow Zacharias and Elizabeth, Mary's greeting, the Magnificat, the birth at Bethlehem, Simeon and Anna, John's preaching, the baptism, and the temptation.

The later homilies take up Nazareth, the catch of fish, the leper, Levi, fasting and new wine, the Sabbath, the beatitudes, the centurion, the widow's son at Nain, and John's question. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
