## Per-finding notes

### M-1 — Sent. 1 (§1 u01-open)
- **Was:** "The beginning of salvation is self-knowledge."
- **Finding:** κατάγνωσις = "condemnation", not "knowledge"
- **Now:** "The beginning of salvation is self-condemnation."
- **Why:** Ἀρχὴ σωτηρίας ἡ ἑαυτοῦ κατάγνωσις — the famous Evagrian maxim turns on self-condemnation (condemning oneself), not self-knowledge; "knowledge" inverts the ascetic point.

### M-2 — Sent. 9 (§1 u01-open)
- **Was:** "Priest-like wisdom, life, and nobility, he produces."
- **Finding:** subject/object inverted; "he" invented
- **Now:** "Prudence and life and nobility make the priest."
- **Why:** Ἱερέα φρόνησις καὶ βίος καὶ εὐγένεια ποιεῖ — ἱερέα is accusative (object), the triad φρόνησις/βίος/εὐγένεια the nominative subject of ποιεῖ. The priest is *made* by these things; there is no "he".
