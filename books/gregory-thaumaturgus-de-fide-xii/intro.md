Gregory Thaumaturgus ("the Wonderworker," c. 213–c. 270) studied under Origen at Caesarea for five years and became bishop of Neocaesarea in Pontus, where legend credits him with converting nearly the whole city.

The Twelve Chapters on the Faith are transmitted under Gregory's name, though scholars doubt he wrote them — some ascribe the work to the circle of Apollinaris of Laodicea a century later.

This volume holds Chapters 1–2: Christ's body as created flesh of the uncreated Word, and the flesh not consubstantial with the Godhead.
