Hesychius of Jerusalem (c. 412–450) preached this homily on Lazarus and the palm branches. It takes up the raising and the entry into the city. It is not Homily II on Saint Lazarus.

The raising of Lazarus is the sign that God raises the nations. The daughter of Zion is told to rejoice. Angels cry out at the entry.

He tells the hearers to bind themselves to Christ and to follow him as the palm trees do. The prayer asks that the place of buying and selling become a holy temple, and not a den of robbers.
