## Finding M1 — §3: ἐπιστροφαῖς → "nurtures" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…and the eternal and endless circle is preserved for them both in their processions and in their nurtures."

**Finding:** This is Proclan doctrine in Christian dress: the Neoplatonic cycle of πρόοδος (procession) and ἐπιστροφή (return/reversion to the source). "Nurtures" (confusion with τροφή?) obscures the doctrine.

**Now:** "…and the eternal and endless circle is preserved for them both in their processions and in their returns."

**Why (Greek):** «καὶ ὁ ἀΐδιος τούτοις καὶ ἀτελεύτητος ἔν τε ταῖς προόδοις καὶ ταῖς ἐπιστροφαῖς κύκλος σώζεται» — ἐπιστροφή is the technical term for the reversion of what proceeds back to its principle.
