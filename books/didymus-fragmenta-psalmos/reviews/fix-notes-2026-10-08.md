# FIX-NOTES: didymus-fragmenta-psalmos

## u19-rem-close[24] — ὁμοούσιον clause restored
**Was:** "It should be noted, however, that the concurrent will is something else, standing as a different will from the deliberative hypostasis which is of the one Willing."
**Finding:** The entire point of the sentence — that the concurrent will *proves* the hypostasis is *consubstantial* with the divine essence — is dropped.
**Now:** "It should be noted, however, that the concurrent will, from another to another will, is indicative of the deliberative hypostasis being consubstantial with the essence of the One who wills."
**Why:** Greek: "σημειωτέον δὲ ὅτι τὸ συντρέχον βούλημα…παραστατικόν ἐστιν τοῦ ὁμοούσιον εἶναι τὴν βουλευτικὴν ὑπόστασιν τῇ τοῦ θέλοντος οὐσίᾳ."
