# FIX-NOTES — hesychius-in-petrum-paulum (Hesychius of Jerusalem, Encomium on Peter and Paul)

Built from site English (https://viapatrum.org/works/hesychius-in-petrum-paulum/). Only the flagged section (§1) is included in the corrected file; other sections are unchanged.

## Finding M-1 — §1: κρίνον → "leek" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "The rose is indeed beautiful… yet if it is struck together with leek, it rightly becomes even more fragrant."

**Finding:** κρίνον is the lily; "leek" is πράσον. The rose/lily fragrance image became a vegetable.

**Now:** "The rose is indeed beautiful… yet if it is joined with the lily, it rightly becomes even more fragrant."

**Why (Greek):** «τῷ κρίνῳ» — κρίνον = lily.

## Finding M-2 — §1: garbled tongue clause (MAJOR)

**Was:** "He is the trumpet of the mystery, a tongue that has taught one language and many…"

**Finding:** Garbled; the Greek is a clean participial phrase.

**Now:** "He is the trumpet of the mystery, a tongue that taught many tongues…"

**Why (Greek):** «ὡς γλῶσσα καὶ πολλὰς γλώσσας διδάξασα» — "as a tongue that taught many tongues."

## Finding M-3 — §1: κόλπος → "net" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…the bait is the sufferings, the hook is the cross, and the net is the resurrection."

**Finding:** κόλπος ("fold, bosom") → "net" (already used for σαγήνη). In the fishing metaphor, the fold is where the catch is gathered.

**Now:** "…the bait is the sufferings, the hook is the cross, and the fold is the resurrection."

**Why (Greek):** «κόλπον τὴν ἀνάστασιν» — κόλπος = fold/bosom.
