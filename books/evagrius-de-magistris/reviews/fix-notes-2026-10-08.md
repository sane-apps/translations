# Fix Notes — evagrius-de-magistris (On Teachers and Disciples)

**Verdict:** Section retranslated — systematic error profile (voice flips, lexical false friends, fabricated subjects, corrupt-text mishandling). All 4 section pages re-derived from the Greek in `*_source.json` (PG, De magistris et discipulis under the name of Nilus). Findings M-1–M-7 were sampled from §1 only, but §§2–4 showed the same garbling (e.g. φοίνικες→"phoenixes", ἀπάθεια→"apatheia"), so the full work was retranslated.

**Files written:** `corrected-english/edmd_u01_open_english.json`, `corrected-english/edmd_u01_rem_english.json` (u01-rem-early/mid/close).

**Term-consistency pass applied:** ἀπάθεια→"dispassion" (u01-rem-close); θυμός→"anger" (u01-rem-early: ὀξύτατον θυμὸν; u01-rem-mid: θυμῷ/θυμόν); πρᾶξις→"practice" (u01-open: ἐνάρετον πρᾶξιν); λογισμοί→"thoughts" (u01-rem-early).

## Per-finding notes

### M-1 — §1 (u01-open)
- **Was:** "An inexperienced shepherd turns away the sheep's ankles"
- **Finding:** ἐκστρέφω = "dislocate, wrench", not "turn away"
- **Now:** "An inexperienced shepherd has dislocated the sheep's ankles"
- **Why:** ἐξέστρεψεν is aorist active of ἐκστρέφω ("wrench out of joint, dislocate"); nothing about "turning away".

### M-2 — §1
- **Was:** "and an ignorant leader the disciples' paths." (verbless fragment)
- **Finding:** verb elided from the parallel clause
- **Now:** "and a leader the disciples' paths." (verb "has dislocated" carried over from the first clause)
- **Why:** Greek καὶ ἡγούμενος ὁδοὺς μαθητῶν elides ἐξέστρεψεν; the English must carry the same verb.

### M-3 — §1
- **Was:** "An ignorant shepherd is not recognized in the mist among sheep"
- **Finding:** διαγνώσεται is middle voice — "will not discern", not passive "is not recognized"
- **Now:** "An ignorant shepherd will not be discerned in the mist amid the sheep"
- **Why:** Οὐ διαγνώσεται… ἀνεπιστήμων ποιμὴν — the shepherd's own failure to discern; "be discerned" keeps the middle's reflexive sense in idiomatic English.

### M-4 — §1
- **Was:** "An inattentive disciple is a straying piece of wood"
- **Finding:** ἀνήκοος = "disobedient"; διεστραμμένον = "warped, twisted"
- **Now:** "A disobedient disciple is warped wood"
- **Why:** ἀνήκοος (not-listening = disobedient); διεστραμμένον (perfect passive participle of διαστρέφω, "twisted/warped").

### M-5 — §1
- **Was:** "being dissolved from the common melody through fellowship"
- **Finding:** dative of means + genitive misread; μέλος = "limb", not "melody"
- **Now:** "and by fellowship with him the common limb is dissolved."
- **Why:** τῇ κοινωνίᾳ λυομένη τοῦ κοινοῦ μέλους — τῇ κοινωνίᾳ is dative of means ("by fellowship"); μέλος = "limb/member".

### M-6 — §1
- **Was:** "A brother eating only the honey of bees' toil"
- **Finding:** κηφήν = "drone" (the bee); "honey" added
- **Now:** "A drone eats the labors of the bees"
- **Why:** Κηφὴν ἐσθίει μελισσῶν πόνους — the drone (male bee, eats without working) consumes the product of the bees' labors.

### M-7 — §1
- **Was:** "diminishes the fellowship, a profitable practice"
- **Finding:** genitive/accusative inverted — ἐνάρετον πρᾶξιν is the object, συνοδίας the genitive
- **Now:** "diminishes the company's virtuous practice."
- **Why:** ἐλαττοῖ συνοδίας ἐνάρετον πρᾶξιν — "diminishes the virtuous practice (acc.) of the company (gen.)"; πρᾶξις rendered "practice" per the cross-work convention.

## Unflagged sections retranslated (same error profile)

- **u01-rem-early ("The Spiritual Armament"):** full retranslation. Notable corrections: ἐκλύει→"slackens" (not "loosens the hands" as a fragment); θυρεῷ πίστεως→"shield of faith" (θυρεός, not θυρίς); ὀξύτατον θυμὸν→"the sharpest anger" (double accusative with μάχαιραν, both governed by δός); †δακτύλων† retained with dagger — the base text marks it corrupt, so it is translated literally ("of fingers") and flagged, not smoothed over; †κήνησον† read as κίνησον ("stir the feet").
- **u01-rem-mid ("The Teacher as Physician"):** full retranslation. τὴν ἧτταν fragment kept as a continuation ("…the defeat, by the threat of eternal punishment") since the verb belongs to the preceding page; Μὴ κινήσῃ θυμῷ→"let him not be moved with anger" (θυμός per convention); ἀνεξικάκως→"without malice"; final clause breaks off mid-sentence in the Greek ("οἶδε γὰρ ἡ…") and is marked with an ellipsis.
- **u01-rem-close ("The Path to Virtue"):** full retranslation. Key corrections: Αἰλείμ = Elim (not "Aileim"); φοίνικες = palm trees (Exod 15:27 — NOT "phoenixes", twice); δώδεκα πήγας = "twelve springs"; ἀπαθείας→"dispassion" (convention); τὴν τῶν παθῶν φλόγα→"the flame of the passions" quenched by the springs' waters (the old text had the waters *providing a flame* — inverted); the spurious "(Hebrews 11:10)" citation (wrong verse, absent from the Greek) dropped.

## Unfixable / caveats
- †δακτύλων† (u01-rem-early) and πυλῶνες (u01-rem-close, "gates" at Elim — no gates in Exod 15:27) are corrupt or anomalous in the PG base text itself; rendered literally and flagged rather than emended. A future pass against Guillaumont's critical text could resolve them.
- The ἀντίταξον φάλαγγα παθῶν ἡδονῇ clause (u01-rem-early) is genuinely compressed; rendered "draw up a phalanx in opposition against the pleasure of the passions" (παθῶν taken with ἡδονῇ).
