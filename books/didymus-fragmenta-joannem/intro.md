Didymus the Blind (c. 313–c. 398) was a Christian teacher in Alexandria. He lost his sight at the age of four and remained a layman. Athanasius made him head of the catechetical school.

This English is his Fragments on John. They are not the fragments of Ammonius, and they are not the fragments of Apollinaris of Laodicea. They are not one of the papyrus commentaries found at Tura.

A reader meets the nature of the Spirit, faith and spiritual nourishment, the gift of the Father and the fall of Judas, and the revelation of glory and eternal life.
