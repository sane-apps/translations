Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) preached these homilies on Joshua. Rufinus translated them. They are not his notes on Joshua, and they are not his selections on Joshua.

They begin with the name Jesus, the death of Moses, and the crossing of the Jordan. Rahab is saved by the scarlet cord. Jericho falls to the trumpets, and what is devoted stays devoted.

Later homilies take up the conquered kings, Caleb's request, the charge not to mix with the remaining nations, the choice of whom to serve, the death of Jesus son of Nun, and the bones of Joseph. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
