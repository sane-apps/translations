# FIX-NOTES — apollinaris-fragmenta-joannem (Fragments on John)

**Base used:** the work's book is not present on the Mac (`books/` has no `apollinaris-fragmenta-joannem`), so the fix base is the on-page English from viapatrum.org as captured in the translation-review site crawl (`~/workspace/scratch/c7-review/works_json/apollinaris-fragmenta-joannem.json`), whose sentence text matches the review's quoted English verbatim. Output JSONs are written in the Mac `*_english.json` schema (same keys/order); `baehrens`/`claim` strings are inferred from the psalmos pattern and the `title` is the page title — flag to parent for normalization against the real book if it exists elsewhere.

**Authority per passage:** the review's recovered Greek (quoted below) cross-checked against the on-page Greek (OCR-garbled; intra-word spaces) and standard texts (LXX/NT) where the passage quotes Scripture. Where the page Greek was too garbled to verify, the review's Greek is the authority — noted per finding.

## F1 — u01-open §1 [10]: hallucinated "Simon"
- **Was:** "The Jews sent, says **Simon**, from among the most tested…"
- **Finding:** φησίν = "he says" (the commentator); no Σίμων in the Greek.
- **Now:** "The Jews sent, **he says**, from among the most tested…"
- **Why:** φησίν is the standard catena "he says"; the name was invented. Authority: review Greek + page Greek (φη σίν visible).

## F2 — u01-rem-close §4 (Jn 6:54) [10]: ὁμογενές → "only-begotten"
- **Was:** "…nourishing all **the only-begotten** into life through the strength being reinforced in it, and by remaining sharing in the flesh, it makes them shine through likeness."
- **Finding:** ὁμογενές = "that which is of the same kind" (flesh sharing Christ's kind), not μονογενής.
- **Now:** "But now life-giving flesh is given, nourishing into life all that is of its own kind, through the power mingled with it and blended into the partaking flesh by way of likeness."
- **Why:** re-derived from νῦν δὲ ζωοποιὸς δέδοται σὰρξ τρέφουσα πᾶν τὸ ὁμογενὲς εἰς ζωὴν διὰ τῆς ἀνακραθείσης αὐτῇ δυνάμεως καὶ ταῖς μετεχούσαις σαρξὶν ἀνακιρναμένης καθ' ὁμοίωσιν. Authority: review Greek + page Greek (ὁμογενὲς visible).

## F3 — u01-rem-close §4 (Jn 6:55) [11]: ἐπίκουρος → "attacker"
- **Was:** "…which is not an **attacker** of the temporary and perishing life…"
- **Finding:** ἐπίκουρος = "helper, ally."
- **Now:** "True is the food which is no ally of the temporary and perishing life, but a preparer of the eternal."
- **Why:** exact opposite corrected. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F4 — u02-open §5 (Jn 7:28) [9]: sender/sent inverted
- **Was:** "yet **those sent by Him are not known by them**…"
- **Finding:** τὸν ἀποστείλαντα = "the one who sent him" (the Father).
- **Now:** "yet **the one who sent him was not known to them**, because they were most far removed from the true knowledge of God…"
- **Why:** agent restored. Authority: review Greek + page Greek (τὸν δὲ ἀποστείλαντα αὐτὸν… οὐ γινώσκεσθαι visible).

## F5 — u02-open §5 (Jn 7:39) [11]: temporal sequence inverted
- **Was:** "…he was going to provide a sanctification to men, truly given, **before the Word was incarnated**…"
- **Finding:** οὐ πρότερον… πρὶν σαρκωθῆναι = the true sanctification was NOT given before the incarnation.
- **Now:** "…he was going to grant from the Spirit a sanctification to men — one not truly given before the Word was made flesh and had run the whole human course of life and carried our dishonor into divine glory; for it is here that God, having made humanity receptive in Christ, bestows the Spirit as upon his own and upon those received,"
- **Why:** whole sentence re-derived from Ὡς τῆς σαρκὸς κοινωνίαν πρὸς ἡμᾶς ἀναδεξάμενος τοῦ πνεύματος ἔμελλεν παρέξειν οὐ πρότερον ἀνθρώποις ἁγιασμὸν τὸν ὄντως δεδομένον πρὶν σαρκωθῆναι τὸν λόγον… (confirmed by the next clause on "the Holy Spirit was not yet"). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F6 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Jn 3:19) [1]: πονήσαντος → "harming"
- **Was:** "…without anyone **harming** it in order to find it…"
- **Finding:** πονήσαντος (aor. ptc. of πονέω) = "with no one labouring."
- **Now:** "The light came of its own accord, with no one having labored to find it (John 3:19)."
- **Why:** wrong-verb misreading fixed. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F7 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Jn 4:33) [14]: direction reversed
- **Was:** "…for **to feed on Christ** is a greatest blessing…"
- **Finding:** τὸ τρέφειν Χριστόν (accusative) = "to feed Christ."
- **Now:** "…for to feed Christ is a very great blessing, which he did not begrudge to those willing."
- **Why:** giver/receiver restored (the disciples' service of offering him food). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F8 — u01-rem-mid §3 (Jn 5:5) [16]: δίκας ἐξέτισε → "tested the judgments"
- **Was:** "…the people **for forty years tested the judgments** in the wilderness…"
- **Finding:** δίκας ἐκτίνειν = "to pay the penalty."
- **Now:** "…the people paid the penalty in the wilderness for forty years…"
- **Why:** idiom corrected. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F9 — u01-rem-early §2 (Jn 2:4) [12]: μὴ παρεῖναι → "must not pass"
- **Was:** "…his hour for signs… **must not pass**."
- **Finding:** μὴ παρεῖναι = "was not yet present" ("my hour has not yet come").
- **Now:** "Therefore, he says that his hour for signs—that is, the hour according to reason and according to what is fitting—had not yet come."
- **Why:** sense restored. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F10 — u01-rem-early §2 (Jn 2:4) [16]: active → passive
- **Was:** "For, **being able to be offended**, he answered…"
- **Finding:** δυσωπεῖν (active) = "to put to shame, treat sternly."
- **Now:** "For he answered as one who could be stern with her, as was God's word to Moses, 'Let me alone'—as one able, that is, both to rebuke and to persuade."
- **Why:** voice restored; the "Leave me alone" (ἔασόν με, Ex 32:10) parallel confirms the stern reading. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F11 — u01-rem-early §2 (Jn 2:4) [17]: πιστικῶς → "in faith"
- **Was:** "…he was staying toward her **in faith**…"
- **Finding:** πιστικῶς = "persuasively."
- **Now:** "Knowing, then, that he was persuasively disposed toward her, and seeing from his answer that he was inclining toward the servants' request, she bids them do whatever he commands."
- **Why:** whole sentence re-derived from εἰδυῖα δὴ καὶ πιστικῶς πρὸς αὐτὴν διακείμενον καὶ ἐκ τῆς ἀποκρίσεως ὁρῶσα πρὸς τὴν ἀξίωσιν ἐγγὺς ἐπινεύοντα…. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F12 — u01-rem-early §2 (Jn 2:13–16) [18]: αἰσχροκέρδεια gutted; τοκίζειν → "provoke"
- **Was:** "…who have come **through shamelessness to provoke the silver**…"
- **Finding:** αἰσχροκέρδεια = "shameful love of gain"; τοκίζειν τὸ ἀργύριον = "to put the money out at interest."
- **Now:** "Allegorically, those who have come out of shameful love of gain to put out at interest the silver that belongs to the Word of God (John 2:13-16), and who moreover sell the gifts of the Spirit—called doves for their natural kinship with the Holy Spirit that descended upon the Savior in the form of a dove—"
- **Why:** re-derived from Κατ' ἀλληγορίαν τοὺς δι' αἰσχροκέρδειαν ἐληλυθότας ἐπὶ τὸ τοκίζειν τὰ τοῦ θεοῦ λόγου ὄντα ἀργύριον…. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F13 — u02-rem-close §8 (Jn 10:1–6) [7]: ἀσμένως → "unseemly"

## F14 — u03-open §9 (Jn 11:7–11) [20]: προσκόμματα → "fragments"
- **Was:** "…do not fear **the fragments of light** lying on the earth from a non-shining heaven…"

- **Now:** "…do not fear the stumbling blocks lying on the ground, since no light shines from heaven…"
- **Why:** from μὴ φοβεῖσθε τὰ ἐν γῇ κείμενα προσκόμματα φωτὸς ἐξ οὐρανοῦ οὐ λάμποντος. Page Greek too garbled to verify (anchor not found in OCR); review Greek is the authority. The sentence's garbled tail ("nor from men do you expect to be overcome…") is unflagged and untouched.

## F15 — u04-open §13 (Jn 13:25–26) [16]: κύρωσις → "condemnation"
- **Was:** "…**the condemnation of the passions by the devilish thoughts**."
- **Finding:** κύρωσις = "ratification, confirmation"; καταθυμίων = "of the heart's intentions."
- **Now:** "The evangelist called this the entrance of Satan—the confirmation, to the devil, of his heart's intentions by his thoughts."
- **Why:** mechanics restored: Judas's hidden counsels confirmed to the devil. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

- **Now:** "…and you who say you believe will flee a little later, once your faith has been shaken by fear."
- **Why:** τῆς πίστεως re-attached to ὑμῶν. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F17 — u05-open §17 (Jn 16–17) [16]: ἐπίστησον/ἐπιστασίαν → "perceive/perception"
- **Was:** "But **let them perceive the invisible perception**…"
- **Finding:** ἐπίστησον (aor. imp. of ἐφίστημι) = "set over"; ἐπιστασίαν = "oversight."
- **Now:** "But set over them the invisible oversight, keeping them as sons, since they have become sons, having received from me the name of sonship."
- **Why:** re-derived from ἐπίστησον δὲ αὐτοῖς τὴν ἀόρατον ἐπιστασίαν διαφυλάττων αὐτοὺς υἱοὺς ὥσπερ γεγόνασιν λαβόντες παρ' ἐμοῦ τὸ ὄνομα τῆς υἱότητος. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.
