Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403) is the name on this short piece on the Trinity. It is not the discourse on faith, and it is not the Panarion.

It says there is one glorification of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and one worship.

The seraphim cry "Holy, holy, holy," and in that cry they glorify the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit together. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
