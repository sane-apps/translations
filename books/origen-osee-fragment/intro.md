Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) was a Christian theologian and biblical scholar who led the Catechetical School in Alexandria.

The occasion is a passage in Hosea where plural verbs shift to a singular. Some readers suspect an error and alter the wording. Origen explains that the apparent awkward wording is deliberate, and that Scripture uses singular and plural to teach about unity with God.

To follow the argument, a reader should know that Hosea is cited as Osee, and that the apostolic quotations are heavily adapted or paraphrased rather than direct citations.
