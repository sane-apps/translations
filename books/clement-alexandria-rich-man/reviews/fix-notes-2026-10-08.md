# FIX-NOTES — clement-alexandria-rich-man (Clement of Alexandria, Who Is the Rich Man That Shall Be Saved?)

Built from site English (https://viapatrum.org/works/clement-alexandria-rich-man/16/, /18/, /20/, /36/, /42/). Only the five flagged chapters are included in the corrected file; other chapters are unchanged.

## Finding 1 — ch 16: subject/predicate inversion (MAJOR)

**Was:** "For the way itself now becomes the heart of the one who is pure…"

**Finding:** The Greek subject is αὐτὸς ("he himself" — the Savior); ὁδὸς is predicate nominative. The English swaps the roles.

**Now:** "For he himself already becomes a way for the one pure in heart…"

**Why (Greek):** «ὁδὸς γὰρ αὐτὸς ἤδη τῷ καθαρῷ τὴν καρδίαν γίνεται» — αὐτὸς (nominative) = subject; ὁδὸς (predicate nominative) = "a way"; τῷ καθαρῷ τὴν καρδίαν (dative + accusative of respect) = "for the one pure in heart" (cf. Jn 14:6).

## Finding 2 — ch 18: ἀτυφία → "freedom from ignorance" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…faith, hope, love, brotherly love, knowledge, gentleness, freedom from ignorance, and truth…"

**Finding:** ἀτυφία = α-privative + τῦφος (pride) = "freedom from pride." "Freedom from ignorance" duplicates γνῶσις ("knowledge") in the same list.

**Now:** "…faith, hope, love, brotherly love, knowledge, gentleness, freedom from pride, and truth…"

**Why (Greek):** «…γνώσει καὶ πραότητι καὶ ἀτυφίᾳ καὶ ἀληθείᾳ» — ἀτυφία ≠ ἀγνωσία.

## Finding 3 — ch 20: ἀσαφῶς → "clearly" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "They had heard well, as disciples, what the Lord had said in parable and clearly, and they perceived the depth of the words."

**Finding:** ἀσαφῶς = "obscurely" — the exact opposite. The disciples ask "Who can be saved?" because they perceived the depth of what was said obscurely.

**Now:** "They had heard well, as disciples, what the Lord had said in parable and obscurely, and they perceived the depth of the words."

**Why (Greek):** «τοῦ παραβολικῶς καὶ ἀσαφῶς λεχθέντος ὑπὸ τοῦ κυρίου» — ἀσαφῶς (alpha-privative + σαφής) = "unclearly, obscurely."

## Finding 4 — ch 36: ὑπερηφανέω → "boast" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "They boast of their own nobility being seen in the world."

**Finding:** With the accusative/infinitive, ὑπερηφανέω = "look down upon, scorn." The saints deliberately remain hidden; "boast of being seen" contradicts the passage.

**Now:** "They scorn their own nobility being seen in the world."

**Why (Greek):** «τὴν αὑτῶν εὐγένειαν ὑπερηφανοῦντες ἐν κόσμῳ βλέπεσθαι» — ὑπερηφανέω + acc. = despise/scorn.

## Finding 5 — ch 42: hidden hand shown instead of concealed (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…being baptized a second time with his tears, hiding his face in shame and showing only his right hand."

**Finding:** The Greek says he concealed *only his right hand* (the hand that had murdered); "his face" is added and the hand is inverted from hidden to shown.

**Now:** "…being baptized a second time with his tears, concealing only his right hand."

**Why (Greek):** «τοῖς δάκρυσι βαπτιζόμενος ἐκ δευτέρου, μόνην ἀποκρύπτων τὴν δεξιάν» — ἀποκρύπτων = "concealing"; μόνην τὴν δεξιάν = "only the right hand." No "face" in the Greek.
