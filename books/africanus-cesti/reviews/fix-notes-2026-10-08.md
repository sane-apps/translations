# FIX-NOTES — africanus-cesti (Julius Africanus, Miscellanies / Cesti)

Built from site English (https://viapatrum.org/works/africanus-cesti/46/, /73/, /88/, /89/). Only the four flagged chapters are included in the corrected file; other chapters are unchanged.

## Finding 1 — ch 46: garbled phrase "from any other pens to come" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "Take human blood, either from a vein-cutter or from any other pens to come, and throw it into a wide-mouthed vessel."

**Finding:** Nonsense mistranslation of a plain phrase.

**Now:** "Take human blood, either from a vein-cutter or from any other source, in whatever way it may happen, and throw it into a wide-mouthed vessel."

**Why (Greek):** «εἴτε ἐξ ἄλλου του οἵου δήποτε ἂν τύχῃ τρόπου» = "or from any other (source), in whatever way it may happen."

## Finding 2 — ch 73: corrupted sentence, dropped ingredient (MAJOR)

**Was:** "Of Africanus: for any beast's bite.ss and plaster it with a linen cloth; or soak the seed of this and pound it, and likewise set it on with a linen cloth."

**Finding:** Stray artifact "bite.ss"; the main clause "having pounded cardamom" dropped entirely, leaving "plaster it" with no stated subject.

**Now:** "Of Africanus: for the bite of every beast. Having pounded cardamom, plaster it on with a linen cloth; or soak the seed of this and pound it, and likewise apply it with a linen cloth."

**Why (Greek):** «Ἀφρικανοῦ· πρὸς δῆγμα παντὸς θηρίου Κάρδαμον λειώσας ἐπίπλασσε μετὰ ὀθονίου ἢ τούτου σπέρμα βρέξας καὶ λειώσας ὡσαύτως ἐπιτίθει μετὰ ὀθονίου» — Κάρδαμον λειώσας = "having pounded cardamom."

## Finding 3 — ch 88: incoherent weights sentence (MAJOR)

**Was:** "The Attic mina has 25 litra is of 24 ning minas there are myriad differences, and nothing of them is necessary for us to set out now."

**Finding:** Three clauses fused into word-salad; στατῆρας ("staters") lost; "remaining" → "ning."

**Now:** "The Attic mina has 25 staters; the Italian litra is of 24 staters; of the remaining minae there are myriad differences, and nothing of them is necessary for us to set out now."

**Why (Greek):** «Ἡ Ἀττικὴ μνᾶ στατῆρας ἔχει κεʹ· ἡ δὲ Ἰταλικὴ λίτρα στατήρων ἐστὶ κδʹ· τῶν δὲ λοιπῶν μνῶν μυρίαι διαφοραί…» — κεʹ = 25, κδʹ = 24.

## Finding 4 — ch 89: incoherent measures sentence (MAJOR)

**Was:** "The chous is the six-xestes measure: the weight of wine draws 10 l 9 ney leads 15 ry measurable matter there are many differences according to the weight."

**Finding:** Three quantities unrecoverable; ἄγει ("amounts to") → "leads"; μέλιτος ("honey") → "ney."

**Now:** "The chous is the six-xestes measure: the weight of wine is 10 litrai, that of oil 9 litrai, that of honey amounts to 15 litrai; and of every measurable matter there are many differences according to the weight."

**Why (Greek):** «ὁ μὲν τοῦ οἴνου σταθμὸς ἕλκει ˉλˉι ιʹ, ὁ δὲ τοῦ ἐλαίου λίτρας θʹ, ὁ δὲ τοῦ μέλιτος ἄγει ˉλˉι ιεʹ· εἰσὶν δὲ πάσης ὕλης μετρητῆς κατὰ τὸν σταθμὸν πολλαὶ διαφοραί» — ιʹ = 10, θʹ = 9, ιεʹ = 15 (litrai).
