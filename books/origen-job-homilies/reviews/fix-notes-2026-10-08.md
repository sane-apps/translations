## Finding 1 — §1 (u01-open [0]): devil→God agent swap (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…the sheep were uniquely hated by God, which is why fire was sent from heaven to destroy them."

**Finding:** The fragment's opening establishes ὁ ἀντίδικος ἡμῶν ὁ διάβολος ("our adversary the devil") as the subject of the καμήλους/ὄνους/βόας/πρόβατα series. "By God" and "uniquely" (δέ is merely adversative) are invented.

**Now:** "…the sheep he hated, and for this reason he brought fire down from heaven upon the sheep."

**Why (Greek):** «Τὰς μὲν καμήλους πεποίηκε γενέσθαι ὑπὸ τοὺς ἱππεῖς· τὰς ὀνάδας καὶ τοὺς βόας, τοὺς ἀροτριῶντας καὶ τὴν γῆν ἐργαζομένους, πεποίηκε γενέσθαι ὑπὸ τοὺς αἰχμαλωτίζοντας· τὰ πρόβατα δὲ ἐμίσησεν, διὰ τοῦτο πῦρ κατήγαγεν ἐξ οὐρανοῦ ἐπὶ τὰ πρόβατα» — ἐμίσησεν (active, "he hated") and κατήγαγεν ("he brought down") with the devil as subject; δέ = "but."

## Finding 2 — §1 (u01-open [1]): lemma + Origen's comment flattened into wife's words (MAJOR)

**Was:** "As much time passed, his wife observed that a great calamity had arrived. She noted that evil often strikes after a long duration;…"

**Finding:** The Greek contains two layers: the scriptural lemma (Κεφ. Βʹ, θʹ. Χρόνου δὲ πολλοῦ προβεβηκότος, εἶπεν αὐτῷ ἡ γυνὴ αὐτοῦ — "When much time had passed, his wife said to him") and Origen's commentary («Ὅρα τὸ κακοῦργον, ὅτε πολὺς διῆλθε χρόνος, τότε ἐπιτίθεται» — "Behold the evildoer: when much time has passed, then he attacks"). The English merged them and made the wife the observer of "a great calamity."

**Now:** "When much time had passed, his wife said to him. Behold the evildoer: when much time has passed, then he attacks; for it is then especially that the things of hope are tested, then especially that the things of strength grow weak.…"

**Why (Greek):** Lemma/commentary structure as above; «Ὅρα τὸ κακοῦργον» is Origen's gloss on the diabolical attack, not the wife's narration. The clause "hope is most severely tested…" was already correct and is retained.

## Finding 3 — §1 (u01-open [2]): "the breast" hallucination (MAJOR)

**Was:** "There comes a set time when the breast must fall under the power of others."

**Finding:** No "breast" (στῆθος/μαστός) exists anywhere in the Greek. The sentence renders «Εἰς χρόνον γὰρ τακτὸν ἡτοίμαστο πεσεῖν ὑπὸ ἄλλων» — "For at a set time he was appointed to fall under others" — with Job as subject.

**Now:** "For at an appointed time he was prepared to fall under others."

**Why (Greek):** «Εἰς χρόνον γὰρ τακτὸν ἡτοίμαστο πεσεῖν ὑπὸ ἄλλων» (Κεφ. ΙΒʹ, εʹ) — ἡτοίμαστο = "was prepared/appointed"; πεσεῖν ὑπὸ ἄλλων = "to fall under others." No noun for "breast" occurs.

## Finding 4 — u03-rem-mid [0]: "six" for seven (MAJOR)

**Was:** "The sacred text preserves the account of him receiving six sons."

**Finding:** The Greek has the numeral ζʹ = 7.

**Now:** "The sacred text preserves the account of him receiving seven sons."

**Why (Greek):** «Διὸ ἱερῶς ἡ γραφὴ τετήρηκε περὶ τοῦ ὅτι ἔλαβεν υἱοὺς ζʹ» — ζʹ is the Greek numeral for seven.
