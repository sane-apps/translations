## u03-rem-early (Job 42:12)
- **Was:** "the Lord blessed Job's latter days, or perhaps his former ones; his livestock included forty thousand sheep"
- **Now:** "the Lord blessed Job's latter days more than his former ones; his livestock included fourteen thousand sheep"
- **Why:** Ὁ δὲ Κύριος εὐλόγησε τὰ ἔσχατα Ἰὼβ, ἢ τὰ ἔμπροσθεν: ἢ is comparative ("than"), as in LXX Job 42:12; the LXX and the source give fourteen thousand sheep, not forty.
