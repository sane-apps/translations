# Source corrections

Each checked against the page scan; transcription errors only.

## 2026-10-18 (Internet Archive deimputationepri00lapl (Saumur: Lesnerius, 1661))

- §18: PDF page 44, printed p. 34: '& in ſigno rationis traducem'. Was `et insigni rationis traducem`; now `et in signo rationis traducem`.
- §100: PDF page 118, printed p. 108: 'amplecti, ſine dubitatione pro falſo habeo' (ſine split at the line end). Was `amplecti, me dubitatione pro falso habeo`; now `amplecti, sine dubitatione pro falso habeo`.
- §142: PDF page 151, printed p. 141: 'non fallendo fallitur'. Was `non fallendo falluntur`; now `non fallendo fallitur`.
- §573: PDF page 351, printed p. 341: 'arma mutuabitur à Viro docto'. Was `arma mutua biturus, Viro docto`; now `arma mutuabitur a Viro docto`.
- §582: PDF pages 352-353, printed pp. 342-343: 'Certè cùm ipſe peccauit, & peccando ſe corrupit: Etenim non ſibi ſoli peccauit, aut peccato ſuo nocuit, ſed vnius illius lapſu multi, imò omnes mortui ſunt.'. Was `Certe cum ipse peccavit, & peccando se corrupit. Multi, imo omnes peccato pro nobis, sed & vitio illius lapsi moniti sunt.`; now `Certe cum ipse peccavit, & peccando se corrupit: Etenim non sibi soli peccavit, aut peccato suo nocuit, sed unius illius lapsu multi, imo omnes mortui sunt.`.
