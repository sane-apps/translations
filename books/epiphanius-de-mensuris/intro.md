Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403), bishop of Salamis in Cyprus from 367, was the early church's great heresy-hunter — though his learning ranged far beyond controversy into Scripture, history, and antiquities.

On Weights and Measures, written near the end of his life, is his guide to the Bible's world: Hebrew weights, measures, and coins, the ancient translators of the Old Testament, and the books of Scripture — antiquarian lore in service of the reader.

This volume holds Chapter I: measures and weights, the ten prophetic genres, and prophetic signs.
