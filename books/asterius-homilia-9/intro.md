Asterius of Amasea (c. 375–405) was bishop of Amasea in Pontus. This English is his homily on Phocas, the martyr of Sinope. The collected Homilies are a different book.

He speaks of the charity associated with Phocas and of the conversion of barbarians.

The homily assumes familiarity with the basic story of Phocas and with the practice of venerating martyrs' remains.
