Severianus of Gabala (fl. c. 400) was bishop of Gabala in Syria and a preacher in Constantinople. This sermon was long copied among works attributed to John Chrysostom.

This English is On the Three Young Men, his sermon on the youths in the furnace. It is not one of his other sermons.

A reader meets the pride of man and the patience of God, pagan idolatry and divine providence, the fire that does not harm the youths, and the blessing that follows.
