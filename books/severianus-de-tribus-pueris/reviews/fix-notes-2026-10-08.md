## Mechanical fixes — wrong scripture citations (u02-open)

The "Bless…" refrains in this homily quote the Song of the Three Children (Daniel 3 LXX), not the Psalms.

**Was:** "Bless, he says, O sons of men, the Lord (Psalm 146:1)."
**Now:** "Bless, he says, O sons of men, the Lord (Daniel 3:82 LXX)."
**Why:** «εὐλογεῖτε, υἱοὶ τῶν ἀνθρώπων, τὸν κύριον» is Daniel 3:82 LXX (Prayer of Azariah / Song of the Three).

**Was:** "Bless, O Israel, the Lord (Psalm 135:1)."
**Now:** "Bless, O Israel, the Lord (Daniel 3:83 LXX)."
**Why:** «εὐλόγει, Ισραηλ, τὸν κύριον» is Daniel 3:83 LXX.
