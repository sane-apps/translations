Didymus the Blind (c. 313–c. 398) was a Christian teacher in Alexandria who, though blind from childhood, led the city's catechetical school and trained many students in the interpretation of Scripture.

This commentary on Ecclesiastes was produced in the late fourth century as lecturing exegesis for his students at the Alexandrian school.

To follow the commentary it helps to know that Didymus uses the Septuagint, that his Psalm numbers follow the Greek numbering, and that he distinguishes the literal sense from a spiritual or allegorical sense.
