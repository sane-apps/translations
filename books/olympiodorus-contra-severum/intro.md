Olympiodorus of Alexandria, the great philosopher and deacon, wrote this work against Severus of Antioch.

The surviving text is only an introductory lemma. It reports that in the second book of his work against Severus, Olympiodorus brought forward a quotation from Saint Athanasius that speaks of two wills in Christ, and then proceeded to discuss it.

The actual quotation from Athanasius and Olympiodorus's exposition are not preserved. A reader should know that the term "wills" renders the Greek for the two wills in Christ, and that "book" refers to a division of a multi-part work.
