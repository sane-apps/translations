Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) preached these homilies on Genesis. Rufinus translated them. They are not his notes on Genesis, they are not his selections on Genesis, and they are not Didymus the Blind's On Genesis.

He begins with the beginning, the firmament, and the waters, then the ark, the raven and the dove, and Noah. Abraham's name is changed. Three men stand at the oak of Mamre. Lot flees the fire. Isaac is born, and later he is bound on the altar.

Rebecca waters the camels. Esau sells the birthright. Jacob dreams of the ladder, serves for Rachel and Leah, and wrestles until daybreak. Joseph is cast into the pit, and at the end he is revealed to his brothers.
