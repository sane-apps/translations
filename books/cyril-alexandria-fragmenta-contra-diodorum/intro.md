Cyril of Alexandria (c. 376–444) left these fragments from the book against Diodore of Tarsus. The words that survive here are from the homily on the darkening of the sun and the saying that no one knows the day or the hour. Diodore is not named in these lines.

The disciples were reaching for more than had been given. Christ speaks in a veiled way, so that he does not grieve the sincere ones by a bare refusal.

The saying means that even a human being called to adoption must not seek what is beyond nature. The Father has not revealed it to the angels, and a human being will not know it either.
