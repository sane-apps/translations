## §1 (u01-open) — chs. 1–3 — VERIFIED

- Ch. 1 — **Was:** "those who place their trust in the pleasures of gluttony, that is, appetites" — **Now:** "those entrusted with the pleasures of gluttony, that is, the appetites." Why: πεπιστευμένοι is perfect passive participle — the demons are entrusted with the department, not the ones trusting.
- Ch. 1 — **Was:** "receive those who are being conquered by them." — **Now:** "take over those wounded by them." Why: τιτρώσκω = "wound," not "conquer."
- Ch. 2 — **Was:** "in which the mind, by imitating, carries the forms" — **Now:** "in which the mind, being imprinted, carries about within itself the forms of those things." Why: τυπούμενος is passive — "being imprinted" (μιμέομαι would be "imitating").
- Ch. 2 — **Was:** "and from the thing itself it otherwise recognizes" — **Now:** "and henceforth recognizes from the thing itself the demon that approached." Why: λοιπόν = "henceforth, therefore."
- Ch. 2 — **Was:** "If the thought that has encroached upon grudgefulness were to result in my being harmed and dishonored in my mind, it is reproved." — **Now:** "if the face of the one who harmed and dishonored me should occur in my mind, the grudge-bearing thought that suggested it is exposed." Why: clean conditional scrambled; ἐλέγχεται = "is exposed/detected," not "reproved."
- Ch. 2 — **Was:** "but as many memories as involve anger or desire are naturally associated" — **Now:** "but [only] those memories which draw thumos or desire along with them contrary to nature." Why: παρὰ φύσιν ("contrary to nature") was dropped entirely — the doctrinal hinge.
- Ch. 2 — **Was:** "being unable to refuse the phantasm established by the lawgiver." — **Now:** "being unable to receive the phantasy from the Lawgiver who framed it." Why: ἀναδέξασθαι = "to receive" — "unable to refuse" inverts it.
- Ch. 2 — **Was:** "Even if that brightness is lost by the deprivation … appearing to deviate from the ruling faculty." — **Now:** "if that brightness, in the privation of all the notions in things, appears to the ruling faculty in the time of prayer." Why: παραφαίνεται = "appears to" (180°); Εἴπερ = "if," not "even if."
- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "consuming the former with fastings, vigils, and chastity, and the latter with patience, grudge-forgetfulness, and almsgiving" — **Now:** "having consumed the one with fastings, vigils, and sleeping on the ground, and having tamed the other with long-sufferings, freedoms from rancor, and almsgivings." Why: χαμευνίαις = "sleeping on the ground"; καθημερώσας ("having tamed") was dropped.
- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "It is impossible for any of these passions to survive, without being completely overcome by money, wealth, and glory" — **Now:** "It is impossible for anyone to overcome these passions without having utterly despised foods and money and glory." Why: περιγενέσθαι (middle) = "to overcome" (subject inverted); ὑπεριδόντα = "having despised"; βρωμάτων = "foods" (not a second "money").
- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "and even by the desire for one's own body, because they attempt to strike it many times" — **Now:** "and even his own body, because of those who often attempt to beat it." Why: "desire for one's own body" invented; the ascetic despises the body despite demonic beatings.
- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "since we are withholding our reward" — **Now:** "since we have received our reward in full." Why: ἀπέχω = "receive in full" (Matt 6:2).

## §2 (u01-rem-early) — chs. 3–4 — VERIFIED

- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "how in the fantasies during sleep the demons strike our governing faculty" — **Now:** "how the demons imprint and shape our ruling faculty in the phantasies during sleep." Why: τυποῦσιν = "they imprint" (third τυπόω error).
- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "or again through a sense making" — **Now:** "or again through some sense or other." Why: ποιᾶς = "of some sort" (ποῖος), not participle of ποιέω.
- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "the memory receives these without the body." — **Now:** "these the memory moves without the body." Why: κινεῖ = "moves" — "receives" reverses the faculty's action.
- Ch. 3 — **Was:** "This also moves the soul, which is clear from the fact that we suffer this even in sleep while the body is at rest." — **Now:** (phantom first clause removed) "and this is clear from the fact that often we suffer this even in dreams while the body is at rest." Why: no Greek for the first clause.
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "as the sound of waves does for the many." — **Now:** "as in the case of those sailing, with the sound of the waves." Why: τῶν πλεόντων = "of those sailing."
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "Our passion, moving by nature, is extremely helpful to the demons' aim" — **Now:** "Our thumos, when moved contrary to nature, contributes greatly to the demons' aim." Why: παρὰ φύσιν inverted to "by nature" (180°); θυμός dissolved to "passion."
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "they immediately release him upon such pretexts as they please" — **Now:** "then they immediately release it under just pretexts." Why: δικαίαις = "just, righteous" — the cover of righteous indignation.
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "or gives an evil sword to those who are being subjugated" — **Now:** "nor give an evil sword to those who suggest them." Why: τοῖς ὑποβάλλουσιν = "to those suggesting" (agent/patient inverted).
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "And why do you feed the dog, promising it nothing?" — **Now:** "And why do you feed the dog, you who profess to have nothing?" Why: ἐπαγγελλόμενος modifies the monk (professed poverty), not the dog.
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "If this dog hunts and also attacks men, it is evident that he possesses something within" — **Now:** "But if it barks and attacks men, you are clearly shown to possess something within." Why: ὑλακτεῖ = "barks"; πρόδηλος εἶ is 2nd singular (subject shifts to the monk).
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "the passion is muddied, knowing such a thing." — **Now:** "knowing thumos to be the destroyer of such [prayer]." Why: λυμεῶνα = "destroyer" (accusative).
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "where dogs pursue people from houses at the time of prayer" — **Now:** "which drives the dogs out of the houses at the time of prayer." Why: the custom drives dogs out; inverted.
- Ch. 4 — **Was:** "'The wine of the wrath of dragons' Rev 17:6." — **Now:** "'Their wine is the venom of dragons' (Deut 32:33)." Why: Deut 32:33, not Rev 17:6.

## §3 (u01-rem-mid) — chs. 5–7 — VERIFIED

- Ch. 5 — **Was:** "bile and stench were undefiled to the gods" — **Now:** "gall and loin to be things not eaten by the gods." Why: ὀσφύν = "loin" (not ὀσμή "stench"); ἄβρωτα = "not to be eaten."
- Ch. 5 — **Was:** "for I consider that they thought the one was a symbol of wrath" — **Now:** "of which I myself consider the one a symbol of anger." Why: ἔγωγε — Evagrius's own allegorizing, reassigned to the sages.
- Ch. 6 — **Was:** "so that Jesus might exhale the thoughts of the crowd in the place of the intellect" — **Now:** "so that Jesus may withdraw, there being a crowd of notions in the place of the mind." Why: ἐκνεύσῃ = "may withdraw" (ἐκνεύω).
- Ch. 6 — **Was:** "who expected the crown of righteousness in soul and in nakedness." — **Now:** "in cold and nakedness, who awaited the crown of righteousness." Why: ψύχει = "cold" (2 Cor 11:27), misread as ψυχῇ.
- Ch. 6 — **Was:** "For the athlete will be hindered by the obviousness of the tunic" — **Now:** "For that athlete will of course be hindered by his tunic." Why: δηλονότι = "of course" (adverb).
- Ch. 6 — **Was:** "if he who says he is truly devoting his mind to treasure is telling the truth" — **Now:** "if he is a true [witness] who says that the mind clings to its own treasure." Why: garbled conditional restored.
- Ch. 7 — **Was:** "The Holy Spirit, having first been placed, attends to the thought" — **Now:** "The Holy Spirit, then, attends to the thought first set forth." Why: πρώτως τεθέντι agrees with λογισμῷ.
- Ch. 7 — **Was:** "because being humans and having grown old" — **Now:** "because, being men and wrestling with demons." Why: παλαίοντες = "wrestling" (παλαίω), not "grown old."
- Ch. 7 — **Was:** "nor to not have the evil thought repeatedly" — **Now:** "nor again keep the evil thought untried." Why: ἀπείραστον = "untried/unassailed."
- Ch. 7 — **Was:** "if anyone remains among the thoughts that are cut off in the place of the tempted one..." — **Now:** "Yet if any of the cutting thoughts lingers, it settles in the region of the one being tempted." Why: syntax restored — the thought lingers and settles.
- Ch. 7 — **Was:** "scattered down among the forms of the earth" — **Now:** "scattered somewhere below among the parts of the earth." Why: μορίοις = "parts."
- Ch. 7 — **Was:** "those making the lamp, the censer, the altars" — **Now:** "those who make the lampstand of the tabernacle, and the censer, and the bowls and the cups." Why: τῆς σκηνῆς ("of the tabernacle") restored; λυχνία = "lampstand."
- (Ch. 7 πεπίστευται "entrusted" finding falls in the truncated tail beyond the staged text — not verifiable here.)

## §4 (u01-rem-close) — chs. 8–11 — VERIFIED

- Ch. 8 — **Was:** "Do not be discouraged, and you will no longer follow it." — **Now:** "denounce the place hidden by him, and henceforth you will not follow him." Why: phantom sentence removed; actual command (καταμήνυε τὸν κρυπτόμενον τόπον) restored.
- Ch. 9 — **Was:** "and a death with much coldness of the eyelids" — **Now:** "and numbness with much coldness of the eyelids." Why: νέκρωσις = "numbness/deadness."
- Ch. 9 — **Was:** "which the Holy Spirit will dissolve by concise prayer" — **Now:** "which the Holy Spirit will dissolve by intense prayer." Why: συντόνῳ = "intense" (opposite counsel).
- Ch. 10 heading — **Was:** "CHAPTER 3." — **Now:** "Chapter 10." (ΚΕΦΑΛ. Ιʹ=10).
- Ch. 10 — **Was:** "because the love-seeking spirits are destroying it" — **Now:** "since the pleasure-loving spirits destroy it." Why: φιλήδονος = "pleasure-loving."
- Ch. 10 — **Was:** "but the physician of souls treats this friendship, and rather this difficult anger" — **Now:** "but this friendship — or rather, this hard-to-heal gangrene — the Physician of souls heals by abandonment." Why: γάγγραιναν = "gangrene."
- Ch. 10 — **Was:** "for he acknowledges that we suffer something fearful from them" — **Now:** "for He allows us to suffer something fearful from them." Why: συγχωρεῖ = "allows/permits" (providential permission).

## §5 (u02-open) — chs. 11–14 — VERIFIED

- Ch. 12 — **Was:** "The Lord in the Gospels commanded those seeing the weak and those visiting in prison to flee most especially from this demon" — **Now:** "Putting this demon most to flight, the Lord in the Gospels commanded visiting the sick and those in prison." Why: φυγαδεύων agrees with ὁ Κύριος (nominative).
- Ch. 12 — **Was:** "nor leave the house of grief" — **Now:** "nor leave the house of acedia." Why: ἀκηδία ≠ λύπη (next chapter's demon).

- Ch. 13 — **Was:** "it generates thoughts that lead him astray, with the soul advising him" — **Now:** "it gives birth to thoughts counseling the soul to lead itself away." Why: τῇ ψυχῇ is dative.
- Ch. 13 — **Was:** "'If only I could, he says, master myself' (Job 7:15-17)" — **Now:** "'For would that I could, he says, lay hands on myself' (Job 6:9)." Why: χειρώσασθαι = "lay hands on [kill] myself" (Job 6:9 LXX).

- Ch. 13 — **Was:** "'Command, he says to the Corinthians, love toward him' (1 Cor 5:5)" — **Now:** "'Confirm, he says to the Corinthians, love toward him' (2 Cor 2:7–8)." Why: Κυρώσατε = "Confirm/ratify"; 2 Cor 2:7–8, not 1 Cor 5:5.
- Ch. 13 — **Was:** "But we know that this becomes a tribulation for the spirit of man" — **Now:** "But it knows how to afflict men's spirit." Why: οἶδε is 3rd singular (the demon).
- Ch. 13 — **Was:** "those being stung by this and offering themselves to God" — **Now:** "those stung by this demon and fleeing to God." Why: προσφεύγοντας = "fleeing to."
- Ch. 14 heading — **Was:** "CHAPTER 44." — **Now:** "Chapter 14." (ΚΕΦΑΛ. ΙΔʹ=14).
- Ch. 14 — **Was:** "If anyone is mastered by passion, he is mastered by demons" — **Now:** "If anyone has mastered thumos, he has mastered demons." Why: κεκράτηκεν is perfect active — passivizing states the exact opposite of the maxim.
- Ch. 14 — **Was:** "(Matt 11:29)" — **Now:** "(Ps 25:9)." Why: "he will teach the meek his ways" is Ps 25:9 (LXX 24:9).
- Ch. 14 — **Was:** "the mind becomes difficult to manage when it retreats into the field of gentleness" — **Now:** "the mind of those who withdraw becomes hard to hunt down, fleeing to the field of gentleness." Why: δυσθήρατος = "hard to hunt/catch" (by demons).

### §9 (u03-open) — ch. 23 tail + ch. 24 (the untranslated section)

- **Completeness** — **Was:** English broke off mid-sentence at "…the thoughts of…" / "opposing demons" — the completion of *Τίς δὲ ἡ αἰτία τοῦ τὰ νοήματα τῶν αἰσθητῶν πραγμάτων, χρονίζοντα διαφθείρειν τὴν γνῶσιν* and the **entirety of ch. 24** (ΚΕΦΑΛ. ΚΔʹ: the three chief demons opposed to the praktike; the programmatic counsel *διατήρησον θυμὸν … κράτει γαστρός*; the imprinting vs. non-imprinting notions discussion with John 1:1 and Isa 6:1) were untranslated. **Finding:** MAJOR (incomplete chapter). **Now:** full rendering — "…has been stated in the chapters On Prayer. Chapter 24. Of the demons opposed to the praktike, three are the foremost archons… Then dispassion of heart will dawn for you, and you will see a starry mind in prayer… Therefore the notion of God is not in the things that imprint the mind, but in the notions that do not imprint the mind." **Why:** the Greek was present on the page; the English simply stopped.
- **Ch. 23, τυπῶν** — **Was:** "striking continuous lightning through the air of the cell". **Finding:** τυπόω error (4th occurrence in this work). **Now:** "stamping continuous lightnings through the air of the cell". **Why:** *ἀστραπὰς συνεχεῖς … τυπῶν* — τυπόω = "stamp/imprint", not "strike".
- **Ch. 23, ἐπευξάμενοι** — **Was:** "But we, having invited destruction through these thoughts". **Finding:** verb inverted. **Now:** "But we, having prayed destruction upon these thoughts". **Why:** *ἀπώλειαν ἐπευξάμενοι* — ἐπεύχομαι = "pray upon"; we invoke destruction *upon* the thoughts.
- **Ch. 23, δῆλον ὅτι** — **Was:** "nor can we bring anything out manifest that we are able". **Finding:** garbled (1 Tim 6:7). **Now:** "it is clear that we can bring nothing out" (cited 1 Tim 6:7–8). **Why:** *οὐδὲ ἐξενεγκεῖν τι δῆλον ὅτι δυνάμεθα* — δῆλον ὅτι = "it is clear that".
- **Ch. 23, καθυφασμένον** — **Was:** "was looking down because of these thoughts at the garment". **Finding:** wrong verb (Matt 22:11–13). **Now:** "had his garment woven from these thoughts". **Why:** *καθυφασμένον* = perfect passive of καθυφαίνω, "woven" — "had his garment *woven* from these thoughts".
- **Ch. 23, βρώματα** — **Was:** "the thoughts of filth, and the shameful thoughts born from filth". **Finding:** βρῶμα misread. **Now:** "the notions of foods — and of the shameful thoughts born from foods". **Why:** *τῶν βρωμάτων* = "of foods" (βρῶμα = food).
- **Ch. 23, ἀπάθεια** — **Was:** "the apathy of a rational soul having denied cosmic desires". **Finding:** uniform-vocabulary drift. **Now:** "the dispassion of a rational soul that has renounced worldly desires". **Why:** *ἀπάθεια ψυχῆς λογικῆς κοσμικὰς ἀρνησαμένης ἐπιθυμίας* — ἀπάθεια→"dispassion" (uniform), κοσμικός = "worldly".

### §10 (u03-rem-early) — ch. 24 (cont.), chs. 25–26, ch. 27 (start)

- **Ch. 24, ζητήσεις** — **Was:** "And just as desires, if they are directed toward bodies and their thoughts, act in such a way, so too they act toward the incorporeal and their logoi". **Finding:** drops *ζητήσεις* ("and you will inquire whether…") and invents "desires" — no ἐπιθυμία in the Greek. **Now:** "And you will inquire whether, as it stands with bodies and their thoughts, so it stands also with incorporeals and their logoi". **Why:** *Καὶ ζητήσεις, εἴπερ, ὡς ἔχει ἐπὶ τῶν σωμάτων…* — the sentence is an inquiry, not a statement about desires.
- **Ch. 24, τοῖς μὴ τυποῦσι** — **Was:** "but rather is in those who do not impress". **Finding:** neuter masculinized. **Now:** "but is rather in the non-imprinting notions". **Why:** *ἀλλ' ἐν τοῖς μὴ τυποῦσι μᾶλλον* — neuter plural (the notions), not "those who".
- **Ch. 25 heading** — **Was:** "Chapter 44." **Finding:** numeral misread. **Now:** "Chapter 25." **Why:** *ΚΕΦΑΛ. ΚΕʹ* = 25.
- **Ch. 25, ἀκροτάτης** — **Was:** "he then plunges the mind into a superficial desire for asceticism". **Finding:** opposite sense. **Now:** "he plunges the mind into the desire for the most extreme asceticism". **Why:** *εἰς ἐπιθυμίαν ἀσκήσεως ἀκροτάτης* — ἀκρότατος = "extreme", not "superficial".
- **Ch. 25, καταναγκάζει** — **Was:** "or began and were compelled to become imitators of these". **Finding:** active passivized onto the recluses. **Now:** "and he compels [the monk] to become an imitator of these". **Why:** *τούτων μιμητὴν γενέσθαι καταναγκάζει* — καταναγκάζει is active; the demon compels.
- **Ch. 25, κόρον / ἅπαξ** — **Was:** "and not for pleasure, and not just for a single day". **Finding:** two errors. **Now:** "and this not unto satiety, and once a day". **Why:** *οὐ πρὸς κόρον* = "not unto satiety" (κόρος again, not "pleasure"); *ἅπαξ εἰς τὴν ἡμέραν* = "once a day" — "not just for a single day" inverts it.
- **Ch. 25, apatheia definition** — **Was:** "but the [destruction of sin] according to the intellect, those... / It is cutting off the passionate thoughts" (truncated mid-phrase, resumed as fragment). **Finding:** sentence broken across the chunk seam. **Now:** "but that which cuts off the passionate thoughts in the mind, which the holy Paul also named the spiritual circumcision of the hidden Jew (Rom 2:29)". **Why:** *ἀλλὰ τὴν κατὰ διάνοιαν τοὺς ἐμπαθεῖς λογισμοὺς περικόπτουσαν* — one continuous definition, restored whole.
- **Ch. 26, ὁπωσοῦν** — **Was:** "but those who are purified in sight". **Finding:** wrong sense. **Now:** "But those purified in any measure". **Why:** *οἱ δὲ ὁπωσοῦν κεκαθαρμένοι* — ὁπωσοῦν = "in any measure", not "in sight".
- **Ch. 26, συμπαρεκτείνομαι** — **Was:** "are assisted in many things" / "being unable to be assisted in things". **Finding:** wrong verb (twice). **Now:** "are extended along with many things" / "being unable to be extended along with things". **Why:** *συμπαρεκτείνομαι* = "to be extended/spread along with" — "assisted" is wrong.
- **Ch. 26, καλάμη** — **Was:** "they gather reeds into an unlawful brick-making (Eccl 10:18)". **Finding:** wrong noun, wrong ref. **Now:** "they gather stubble for the unlawful brick-making (Exod 5:12)". **Why:** *καλάμη* = "stubble" (Exod 5:12, gathering stubble), not "reeds"; the brick-making is Exodus 5 (Pharaoh denying straw), not Eccl 10:18.
- **Ch. 26, δορκάς / ὄρνεον** — **Was:** "like doves from the thickets, and like an eagle from the snare (Ps 124:7)". **Finding:** both nouns wrong, ref wrong. **Now:** "like a gazelle from snares and like a bird from the trap (Prov 6:5)". **Why:** *ὥσπερ δορκὰς ἐκ βρόχων, καὶ ὥσπερ ὄρνεον ἐκ παγίδος* — δορκάς = "gazelle", ὄρνεον = "bird" (Prov 6:5).
- **Ch. 26, ῥᾷον / κόραι** — **Was:** "For it is easy to call back an impure soul … to health once more, if the demon of sorrow does not forgive … presents to the maidens at the time of prayer the idol of sin". **Finding:** comparative inverted into a conditional; wrong sense of κόρη. **Now:** "For it is easier to cleanse an impure soul than to call back to health again one that has been purified and wounded again, the demon of sorrow not permitting it, but always presenting to the pupils, at the time of prayer, the image of sin". **Why:** *Ῥᾷον γάρ* = "for it is *easier*" (comparative — easier to cleanse an impure soul *than* to restore a lapsed purified one); *ταῖς κόραις* = "the pupils" (of the eyes), not "the maidens".

### §11 (u03-rem-mid) — ch. 27 (cont.) + ch. 66

- **Ch. 27, ἀνάξιον** — **Was:** "A holy priest, saying such things are worthy of being made public". **Finding:** 180° inversion. **Now:** "a holy priest — who said it is unworthy for such things to be published". **Why:** *ἀνάξιον φήσας τὰ τοιαῦτα δημοσιεύεσθαι* — ἀνάξιον = "unworthy".
- **Ch. 27, κακολογήσαντας** — **Was:** "we have rebuked certain ones having unloving feelings toward them". **Finding:** wrong verb. **Now:** "we have often reproved some who had reviled us, not being lovingly disposed toward them". **Why:** *τινας κακολογήσαντας* = "some who had reviled/spoken evil [of us]" — "unloving feelings" is wrong.
- **Ch. 27, scripture ref** — **Was:** "(Matt 18:7)". **Finding:** wrong ref. **Now:** "(Ps 50:20)". **Why:** *«Καθήμενος κατὰ τοῦ ἀδελφοῦ σου κατελάλεις…»* is Ps 50:20 (LXX 49:20), not Matt 18:7.
- **Ch. 27, θεοποιῶν** — **Was:** "making this one a god; for whatever the mind praying sees, this also it confesses as worthy of God". **Finding:** weakens the warning. **Now:** "deifying him — for whatever the praying mind beholds, this it confesses worthy to be God". **Why:** *τοῦτον θεοποιῶν· ὃ γὰρ βλέπει πάντως ὁ νοῦς προσευχόμενος, τοῦτο καὶ Θεὸν ἄξιον ὁμολογεῖν* — deification-by-beholding: "worthy *to be God*".
- **Ch. 27, ἀκατάγνωστον** — **Was:** "a word without fault, healthy, unperceivable". **Finding:** wrong sense (Titus 2:8). **Now:** "an uncorrupt, sound, irreproachable word". **Why:** *ἀκατάγνωστον* = "irreproachable" — "unperceivable" is wrong.
- **Ch. 27, scripture ref** — **Was:** "(Ps 124:7)". **Finding:** wrong ref. **Now:** "(Ps 119:134)". **Why:** *«Ῥῦσαί με ἀπὸ συκοφαντίας ἀνθρώπων»* is Ps 119:134 (LXX 118:134), not Ps 124:7.
- **Ch. 27, ῥυσθείημεν** — **Was:** "May we be uprooted from them by the grace of Christ our God". **Finding:** wrong verb. **Now:** "From him may we also be delivered by the grace of Christ our God". **Why:** *Οὗ καὶ ῥυσθείημεν* — ῥύομαι = "deliver", not "uproot".
- **Ch. 27, οἰκεῖον** — **Was:** "chilling this with the proper body". **Finding:** wrong sense. **Now:** "chill it with their own body". **Why:** *τῷ οἰκείῳ σώματι* = "with *their own* body" — "proper" is wrong.
- **Ch. 27, σικύα** — **Was:** "we feel the head being pulled as if under fig trees with a shudder". **Finding:** wrong noun. **Now:** "we feel the head being drawn as if by a cupping-glass, with a gnashing of the teeth". **Why:** *ὡς ὑπὸ σικύας* — σικύα = "cupping-glass"; "fig trees" (συκῆ) is a different noun.
- **Ch. 27, ἐπισπώμενοι / κόραι** — **Was:** "having wrapped themselves in the heat stored up in the skull … might overflow with the juices of the eyes". **Finding:** two errors. **Now:** "drawing to themselves the heat stored up in the skull … may overflow upon the pupils of the eyes". **Why:** *πρὸς ἑαυτοὺς ἐπισπώμενοι* = "drawing [the heat] *to themselves*" — "wrapped themselves in" is wrong; *ταῖς κόραις τῶν ὀφθαλμῶν* = "the pupils of the eyes" — "juices of the eyes" is wrong.
- **Ch. 27, παρὰ φύσιν** — **Was:** "but for those which are by nature and arrangement weak, they make a yawning". **Finding:** garbled; παρὰ φύσιν misread a third time in this work. **Now:** "but the unnatural and strained [yawnings] they bring about, thinning themselves and touching the inside of the mouth". **Why:** *τὰς δὲ παρὰ φύσιν καὶ διατεταμένας, χάσμα ποιοῦσι* — "the *unnatural* and strained [yawnings] they produce".
- **Ch. 27, yawning-mouth construction** — **Was:** "of sealing the mouths that yawn". **Finding:** agent misassigned. **Now:** "the sealing of the mouth by those who yawn". **Why:** *τοῦ σφραγίζειν τοὺς χασμωμένους τὸ στόμα* — accusative + infinitive: it is *those yawning* who seal *their own* mouth (the ancient tradition of covering the yawning mouth), not an unnamed agent sealing the yawners' mouths.
- **Ch. 66 heading (added)** — **Was:** no heading (the English began "There are certain unclean demons…"). **Finding:** not scored in the review (glyph damaged), but the Greek prints a chapter marker *Ξ†ʹ* before *Εἰσί τινες τῶν ἀκαθάρτων δαίμονες* — the dagger is a mis-OCR of stigma ϛ, i.e. *Ξϛʹ* = 66, which completes the appended sequence 66–71 (followed by ΣΖʹ=67, ΞΗʹ=68, ΞΘʹ=69, Οʹ=70, ΟΑʹ=71). **Now:** "Chapter 66." **Why:** headings match the Greek throughout the staged files; 66 is the only value consistent with the sequence.

### §12 (u03-rem-close) — chs. 67–71

- **Headings 67–71** — **Was:** "66." / "67." / "94." / "98." / "99." **Finding:** numerals misread throughout (cascading off-by-one, then pure misreadings). **Now:** "Chapter 67." / "Chapter 68." / "Chapter 69." / "Chapter 70." / "Chapter 71." **Why:** Greek prints *ΣΖʹ*(=67; sigma is a mis-OCR of xi Ξ — the value is fixed by the sequence), *ΞΗʹ*=68, *ΞΘʹ*=69, *Οʹ*=70, *ΟΑʹ*=71.
- **Ch. 67, genitive absolute** — **Was:** "having given us no occasion through negligence". **Finding:** construction inverted. **Now:** "we having given no occasion through negligence". **Why:** *ἡμῶν μηδεμίαν ἀφορμὴν δεδωκότων ἐξ ἀμελείας* — genitive absolute with ἡμῶν as subject: *we* gave no occasion.
- **Ch. 67, διεδέξατο** — **Was:** "a demon has received us more harshly than the former". **Finding:** wrong verb. **Now:** "a harsher demon than the former has succeeded to us". **Why:** *χαλεπώτερος ἡμᾶς τοῦ προτέρου διεδέξατο δαίμων* — διεδέξατο = "succeeded" (a harsher demon *has succeeded* the former), not "received us".
- **Ch. 67, πολεμούμενος** — **Was:** "fighting much more intensely than the usual habit". **Finding:** passive activized. **Now:** "being warred upon much more violently than usual". **Why:** *πολλῷ σφοδρότερον παρὰ τὴν συνήθειαν πολεμούμενος* — πολεμούμενος is passive: the demon is *being warred upon* (by us).
- **Ch. 67, ἀθρόως** — **Was:** "having fallen continuously through the thoughts of yesterday and the third day". **Finding:** opposite sense. **Now:** "having suddenly fallen away from the thoughts of yesterday and the day before". **Why:** *ἀθρόως* = "all at once, suddenly" — "continuously" is the opposite.
- **Ch. 67, κουφίσας** — **Was:** "having struck the shield of faith". **Finding:** wrong verb. **Now:** "having lifted the shield of faith". **Why:** *τὸν θυρεὸν κουφίσας τῆς πίστεως* — κουφίζω = "lift".
- **Ch. 67, οἰκεῖον οὐρανόν** — **Was:** "looking up toward the inhabited heaven with tears". **Finding:** wrong word. **Now:** "looking up with tears to his own heaven". **Why:** *εἰς τὸ οἰκεῖον … οὐρανόν* = "to *his own* heaven" — "inhabited" (οἰκουμένη) is a different word.
- **Ch. 67, Θεὸν ὑπερασπιστήν** — **Was:** "Become for me a protector toward God". **Finding:** apposition misread (Ps 71:3). **Now:** "Become for me a defending God, and a place of refuge to save me". **Why:** *Γενοῦ μοι εἰς Θεὸν ὑπερασπιστὴν* = "[become] for me *a defending God*".
- **Ch. 67, τιτρώσκοντα** — **Was:** "being wounded much more than being wounded". **Finding:** active passivized. **Now:** "for the most part being wounded rather than wounding". **Why:** *τὰ πολλὰ τιτρωσκόμενον μᾶλλον ἤπερ τιτρώσκοντα* — the second participle is active: "being wounded rather than *wounding*".
- **Ch. 68, συμπαρεκτείνομαι** — **Was:** "are encouraged by many things". **Finding:** wrong verb. **Now:** "are extended along with many things". **Why:** *πολλοῖς συμπαρεκτείνονται πράγμασι* — "to be extended along with", not "encouraged".
- **Ch. 68, παρὰ φύσιν** — **Was:** "therefore they are moved more according to nature". **Finding:** third 180° inversion of this phrase in the work (contrast ch. 26, where the same sentence was rendered correctly — the translation was inconsistent with itself). **Now:** "whence they are also moved more contrary to nature". **Why:** *ὅθεν καὶ παρὰ φύσιν μᾶλλον κινοῦνται* — παρὰ φύσιν = "*contrary to* nature".
- **Ch. 68, πάσῃ φυλακῇ** — **Was:** "It is necessary, therefore, for every guard to keep the heart". **Finding:** dative misread (Prov 4:23). **Now:** "One must therefore keep the heart with all guarding". **Why:** *Δεῖ οὖν πάσῃ φυλακῇ τηρεῖν τὴν καρδίαν* — πάσῃ φυλακῇ is dative of manner: "*with all guarding*".
- **Ch. 68, δορκάς** — **Was:** "so that it may be saved like birds from thickets". **Finding:** wrong noun. **Now:** "so that it may be saved like a gazelle from snares and like a bird from the trap (Prov 6:5)". **Why:** *ὥσπερ δορκὰς ἐκ βρόχων* — δορκάς = "gazelle" (Prov 6:5), not "birds".
- **Ch. 68, ῥᾷον** — **Was:** "For it is not easy to make a pure soul clean again … to call it back to health". **Finding:** comparison inverted. **Now:** "For it is easier to cleanse an impure soul than to call back to health again one that has been purified and wounded again". **Why:** *Ῥᾷον γὰρ ἀκάθαρτον καθᾶραι ψυχὴν, ἢ καθαρθεῖσαν…* = "it is *easier* to cleanse an impure soul *than* to restore a purified-then-wounded one" — the old inverts the comparison ("not easy") and the first term ("pure" for "impure").
- **Ch. 68, κόραι** — **Was:** "but always leaps upon the maidens of the eyes". **Finding:** wrong sense of κόρη. **Now:** "but always leaping upon the pupils of the eyes". **Why:** *ταῖς κόραις τῶν ὀφθαλμῶν* — κόραι here = "pupils", not "maidens".
- **Ch. 69, τὸν θάνατον τοῦ Χριστοῦ** — **Was:** "and the Father of Him raises the dying soul through the knowledge of Himself". **Finding:** instrumental accusative dropped (same omission as §7). **Now:** "but His Father raises the dead soul — through the death of Christ — by the knowledge of Himself". **Why:** *τὴν ἀποθανοῦσαν ψυχὴν τὸν θάνατον τοῦ Χριστοῦ, ἐγείρει* — the accusative "through the death of Christ" was omitted.
