## u01-open (Gal 1:16)
- **Was:** "He does not say this to slander the apostles, but because I did not follow mortal flesh."
- **Now:** "He does not say this to slander the apostles; he means, "I did not take my course from mortal flesh.""
- **Why:** Οὐ διαβάλλων δὲ τοὺς ἀποστόλους τοῦτό φησιν, ἀλλ ὅτι οὐκ ἐστοίχησα θνητῇ σαρκί: ὅτι is recitative and introduces Pauls own words; it is not causal, and the commentator is not the "I".
