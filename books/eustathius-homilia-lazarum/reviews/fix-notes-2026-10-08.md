# FIX-NOTES — eustathius-homilia-lazarum (Eustathius of Antioch, Homily on Lazarus)

Book on Air: `eustathius-homilia-lazarum` · corrected file: `corrected-english/elaz_u01_open_english.json` (section u01-open, sentence [2])

## Finding M-1 — πεπεδημένον ("bound") → "leveled" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…the one having been leveled before a short time by Hades…"

**Finding:** πεπεδημένον is the perfect passive participle of πεδάω ("bind, fetter").

**Now:** "…the one lately bound in Hades' prison…"

**Why (Greek):** τὸν πρὸ βραχέος εἱρκτῇ ᾅδου πεπεδημένον — "the one a short time ago bound in Hades' prison" (εἱρκτή = prison; πρὸ βραχέος = "a short time ago").

## Finding M-2 — τροπαῖς ("defeat/rout") → "tropes" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…[breaking] the bonds of death with the tropes of death."

**Finding:** τροπή = "turning, rout, defeat" (here Christ's defeat of death); "tropes" imports an unrelated rhetorical term.

**Now:** "…[breaking] the bonds of death by death's defeat."

**Why (Greek):** τοὺς τοῦ θανάτου δεσμοὺς ταῖς τοῦ θανάτου τροπαῖς (dative of means) — "the bonds of death by the defeat of death."

*Segments corrected: 1 (u01-open [2]; both lexical fixes fall in the same sentence). The editor's "[breaking]" insertion is retained.*
