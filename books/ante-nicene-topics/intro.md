The writers gathered here are the Christians of the first three centuries — from the Didache and Ignatius through Justin Martyr, Irenaeus, Clement, Tertullian, Origen, and Cyprian down to Lactantius — defending and expounding the faith before Nicaea (325) gave the church an empire-wide creed. Twenty-nine voices in all.

This volume arranges their teaching topically rather than author by author: God, Christ, the Spirit, Scripture, salvation, the church, and the Christian life. The reader hears the whole early chorus on each doctrine in turn, each passage headed with its author and date.

Each topic joins excerpts from several Fathers with the running argument that connects them, followed by translation notes and a scripture index.
