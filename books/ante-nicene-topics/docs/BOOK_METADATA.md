## Logos / catalog description
Modern English excerpts from the ante-Nicene fathers, arranged by the main topics of Christian teaching — God, Christ, Scripture, Spirit, church, sacraments, salvation, human will, last things, and the Christian life. One Logos encyclopedia for private study. Famous voices and lesser-known ones appear together under each topic, earliest to latest. Not a complete dogmatics and not a critical edition of the Greek or Latin.
