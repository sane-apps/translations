Gregory Thaumaturgus ("the Wonderworker," c. 213–c. 270) shepherded the church of Neocaesarea in Pontus through persecution and the Gothic invasions that ravaged Asia Minor in the 250s.

The Canonical Letter, written after those raids, answers a fellow bishop's questions on discipline: how to treat Christians who ate food seized by the barbarians, and captive women assaulted under force.

This volume holds Canon I: on captives and their defilement by force.
