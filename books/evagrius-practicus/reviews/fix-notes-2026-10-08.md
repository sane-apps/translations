# FIX-NOTES — evagrius-practicus (The Praktikos)

Full fix of all 6 section files (12 pages: prologue, chs. 1–100, epilogue) from the device Greek (`epr_*_source.json`, PG 40). This was a deep-read work with pervasive error — misparsed participles/infinitives, dative misreadings, voice flips, false friends — so affected sentences were re-derived from the Greek rather than patched. All 80 MAJOR findings (M-1–M-80) plus selected MINORs addressed. Fresh renderings throughout; no published translation copied.

**Uniform technical vocabulary applied:** ἀπάθεια → "dispassion" everywhere (M-41: replaces the three-way "apathy"/"apatheia"/"impassibility" inconsistency — "apathy" in modern English connotes numbness, the opposite of Evagrius's dispassionate love); θυμός → "thumos"/"irascible part"; ἐπιθυμία → "desire"; λογισμοί → "thoughts" (heading "logismoi" kept); πρᾶξις/πρακτική → "practice"; θεωρία → "theoria/contemplation".

## u01-open (prologue, chs. 1–4) — M-1–M-6, M-41

- M-1 — **Was:** "which would be among the most inexplicable things to conceive and to say of God." — **Now:** "which would be among the most absurd things, to conceive and to say that anything is better than God." Why: Θεοῦ τι κρεῖττον ("anything better than God") — the punchline — was dropped; ἀτοπωτάτων = "most absurd," not "inexplicable."
- M-2 — **Was:** "repels all impurity and this part..." (truncated) — **Now:** "repels all impurity and commands this: 'It is good for a man not to touch a woman.'" Why: καὶ τοῦτο παραγγέλλει ("and commands this") was never rendered; the duplicate "It is announced..." opening the next sentence has been removed.
- M-3 — **Was:** "while cutting off the participation in the evils of the soul" — **Now:** "while cutting off the soul's evils by participation in the good." Why: μετουσίᾳ τοῦ καλοῦ is dative of means; τοῦ καλοῦ was dropped and the means became the object (inversion).
- M-4 — **Was:** "as if upon a secure Lord" — **Now:** "and secure for those who lean upon it as upon the Lord." Why: ἀσφαλής (fem. nom.) agrees with ἡ ῥάβδος (the staff), not Κύριον.
- M-5 — **Was:** "love is the door to natural knowledge which theology receives and the ultimate blessedness." — **Now:** "love is the door of natural knowledge, which theology succeeds, and [the door] of final blessedness." Why: διαδέχεται = "succeeds/follows"; the καὶ-clause was folded into nonsense.
- M-6 — **Was:** "having highlighted some and shadowed others" / "fifty toward the six hundred" — **Now:** "having concealed some and shadowed others" / "six hundred and fifty." Why: ἐπικρύψαντες = "having concealed" (opposite); πεντήκοντα πρὸς τοῖς ἑξακοσίοις = 650.
- Heading — **Was:** "1 THE SAME PRACTICAL LOGOS CHAPTER 4." — **Now:** "THE SAME PRACTICAL LOGOS — 100 CHAPTERS." Why: ΚΕΦΑΛΑΙΑ Ρʹ = 100 chapters, not "Chapter 4."
- M-41 — "apathy" → "dispassion" (prologue: "from which dispassion is born"; ch. 2: "the dispassion of the soul").
- Minor: τὴν ἐν Χριστῷ νηπιότητα → "infancy in Christ" (not "innocence"); καθεζομένῳ clarified as Evagrius dwelling in the Skete; "this" (fear of God) clarified as antecedent of ἐγκράτεια's object.

## u01-rem-early (chs. 5–12) — M-7–M-15

- M-7 — **Was:** "for sensation is without part and free from passion." — **Now:** "for what is without sensation is free from passion." Why: τὸ αἰσθήσεως ἄμοιρον — 180° inversion.
- M-8 — **Was:** "the second war is much more dull than the first" — **Now:** "the second war is much lighter than the first." Why: κουφότερος = "lighter, easier."
- M-9 — **Was:** "Whether to disturb all these in the soul or not to disturb them is not up to us" — **Now:** "Whether these all trouble the soul or do not trouble it is not up to us." Why: accusative-and-infinitive — the thoughts are the troublers; agency inverted.
- M-10 — **Was:** "compels bodies to desire diversely" / "so that they might cease without any relief" — **Now:** "compels to desire different bodies" / "so that, accomplishing nothing, they may give up." Why: σωμάτων διαφόρων is genitive object of ἐπιθυμεῖν; ἵν᾽ ὡς μηδὲν ἀνύοντες παύσωνται.
- M-11 — **Was:** "how it is painful to receive from others" — **Now:** "how shameful it is to receive from others the things for need." Why: ἐπονείδιστον = "shameful/disgraceful."
- M-12 — **Was:** "Regarding things related to need." — **Now:** (deleted). Why: phantom sentence, no Greek; duplicated fragment of τὰ πρὸς τὴν χρείαν.
- M-13 — **Was:** "a heat and a movement of the spirit" / "the face of the one who has been grieved" — **Now:** "a heating and movement of temper" / "the face of the one who caused the grief." Why: θυμός is the technical term; τοῦ λελυπηκότος is active perfect participle (the offender).
- M-14 — **Was:** "he makes seeing the sun difficult or motionless" — **Now:** "he makes the sun appear slow-moving or motionless to behold." Why: δυσκίνητον ἢ ἀκίνητον modify τὸν ἥλιον.
- M-15 — **Was:** "looking here and there lest any of the brothers be near" — **Now:** "looking here and there whether any of the brothers comes." Why: μή introduces an indirect question of hope, not fear.
- Minor: ὑποβάλλει → "suggests" (not "subjects"); ὕδρωψ → "dropsy"; ὀξύτατον → "sharpest."

## u01-rem-mid (chs. 12–22) — M-16–M-24

- M-16 — **Was:** "those who desire to easily publish their struggles and to watch the honors from men, crying out to demons, being flattered and being treated by women" — **Now:** "wishing to publish their struggles readily and hunting the honors from men, fashioning crying demons and women being healed." Why: four errors — βουλόμενος attaches to the thought; θηρώμενος = "hunting"; δαίμονάς…ἀναπλάττων = "fashioning crying demons" (demons are the criers); θεραπευόμενα γύναια = "women being healed."
- M-17 — **Was:** "having made him suspended with empty hopes, e delivers the one who was a prisoner and a holy priest a moment ago to the demon of fornication." — **Now:** full restoration: "...it flies away, leaving him either to the demon of pride to tempt him, or to that of sorrow, which also brings upon him thoughts contrary to his hopes; and sometimes it even hands over to the demon of fornication the one who a little before was a prisoner and a holy priest." Why: ~40 Greek words dropped; typo "e delivers" fixed.
- M-18 — **Was:** "He causes the mind... to stand in reading and wakefulness and prayer; but he withers the inflamed desire by pain and toil..." — **Now:** "Reading, wakefulness, and prayer steady the wandering mind; hunger, toil, and withdrawal wither the inflamed desire; psalmody, patience, and mercy calm the swirling thumos." Why: the remedies are the subjects; πεῖνα = "hunger" not "pain"; θυμόν = "thumos" not "a swirling passion"; phantom "He"/"A word of advice" removed.
- M-19 — **Was:** "so that it may become pleasant" — **Now:** "so that it may become thankful." Why: εὐχάριστος = "thankful, grateful."
- M-20 — **Was:** "For a boy desires various delicacies, but famine thinks the boy's bread to be a blessing." — **Now:** "For satiety desires various delicacies, but hunger counts satiety with bread to be blessedness." Why: κόρος = "satiety" (antithesis with λιμός), not "a boy."
- M-21 — **Was:** "Just as it is possible for life and death to happen at once to the same person... so it is possible for love to coexist with money in one of the weak" — **Now:** "Just as for life and death to occur together in the same person is among impossibilities, so for love to coexist with money in someone is among impossibilities." Why: τῶν οὐκ ἐνδεχομένων / τῶν ἀδυνάτων = "among impossibilities" (twice); the maxim was stated as its exact opposite.
- M-22 — **Was:** "Anger and hatred increase passion" / "might not despoil the soul" — **Now:** "Anger and hatred increase thumos" / "may not terrify the soul." Why: θυμός (technical); ἐκδειματόω = "terrify."
- M-23 — **Was:** "Having grasped a semblance of appearance, our passionate part of the soul is disturbed" — **Now:** "Whenever the irascible part of our soul, having seized a pretext, is disturbed." Why: πρόφασις = "pretext"; τὸ θυμικὸν μέρος = "the irascible part."
- M-24 — **Was:** "then they again work us to be hard and wild, calling us philanthropes" — **Now:** "then again they make us, the humane ones, harsh, calling us harsh and wild." Why: labels swapped — φιλανθρώπους ἡμᾶς is the monks' self-image; the demons call them σκληροὺς καὶ ἀγρίους.
- Minor: λοιπὸν → "finally"; ἔκστασις φρενῶν → "derangement"; νενευκότας → "most inclined"; λειποτάκτην ("deserter") restored.

## u01-rem-close (chs. 23–33) — M-32, M-41

- M-32 — **Was:** "and how being moved by passion, you passed over to the Christ-like apathy" — **Now:** "and how, being passionate, by Christ's mercy you crossed over to dispassion." Why: ἐλέει Χριστοῦ (dative, "by Christ's mercy") was dropped and refashioned into "Christ-like."
- M-41 — "apathy" → "dispassion" throughout.
- Ch. 24: θυμός → "thumos" (×3); "turn our thumos against the demons" (πρὸς not "toward"); παρὰ φύσιν = "contrary to nature."
- Minor: προφάσεις → "pretexts"; τοῦ πολλὰ καὶ πολλάκις σε ταπεινώσαντος → "which humbled you many times and often."

## u02-open (chs. 34–44) — M-25–M-31, M-42–M-46

- M-42 — **Was:** "for the incorporeal demon is more difficult than the corporeal war." — **Now:** "for the immaterial war is harder than the material." Why: ἐνύλου/ἄϋλος agree with πολέμου.
- M-43 — **Was:** "Those pertaining to the psychic passions persist" — **Now:** "Those set over the psychic passions persist." Why: οἱ προεστῶτες = the demons presiding over the passions.
- M-44 — **Was:** "or the passions require the attention of thought" — **Now:** "Whether thought moves the passions, or the passions thought — this must be attended to." Why: προσεκτέον governs the whole question.
- M-25 — **Was:** "the mind needs more remedies than desire... it is a bridle for the mind" — **Now:** "thumos needs more remedies than desire... it is a bridle for thumos." Why: ὁ θυμός (twice); conflating θυμός with νοῦς corrupts the psychology.
- M-26 — **Was:** "the fight against serpents (Exodus 15:25)." — **Now:** "serpent-fight." Why: Exodus 15:25 (Marah) has nothing to do with serpents; wrong citation removed.
- M-27 — **Was:** "the prevailing gloom of the demons" — **Now:** "the prevailing stench among the demons." Why: δυσωδία = "stench."
- M-28 — **Was:** "Hypotheses: ...as if they were a single one" — **Now:** "Counsels: ...as much as possible." Why: Ὑποθῆκαι = "Counsels" (false friend); ὡς ἔνι μάλιστα = "as much as possible."
- M-29 — **Was:** "do not fail to recognize such people." — **Now:** "do not fail to recognize such occasions." Why: καιρούς = "occasions, times."
- M-30 — **Was:** "patient with those who hinder them" — **Now:** "patient with those who serve them." Why: τοῖς ὑπηρετοῦσιν — 180°.
- M-31 — **Was:** "then we are most prone to yield to worldly attachments" — **Now:** "then especially, holding more firmly to self-control, we will be with worldly people." Why: 180° — the Greek counsels firmer self-control.
- M-45 — **Was:** "if your soul is occupied by thoughts" — **Now:** "your soul having been qualified by the thoughts." Why: πεποιωμένη = "having been made of such a sort" (assimilation, cf. ch. 39).
- M-46 — **Was:** "those constantly intruding" / "making them marvel at us and grieve at their failure." — **Now:** "those suddenly rushing in" / "make those, marveling at us and in pain, take flight." Why: ἀθρόως = "suddenly"; ἀποπτῆναι ("fly away") was dropped.

## u02-rem-early (chs. 44–56) — M-33–M-40, M-41

- M-33 — **Was:** "so that we may not stand against the Lord our God, nor dare to stretch out our hands after which such things have been thought." — **Now:** "so that we may not stand before the Lord our God, nor dare to stretch out our hands, against whom we have thought such things." Why: ἐναντίον = "before"; καθ᾽ οὗ = "against whom."
- M-34 — **Was:** "to work through all things" / "to pray to us unceasingly" / "binds our body to the work" / "through this, both the body and all the powers of the soul fight" — **Now:** "to work continually" / "it has been legislated for us to pray unceasingly" / "need our body for the work" / "being by nature fitted to pray even apart from this body, and to fight the demons on behalf of all the powers of the soul." Why: διὰ παντὸς = "continually"; ἡμῖν is dative of agent; προσδεῖται = "needs"; δίχα = "without/apart from" — 180°.
- M-35 — **Was:** "If any monk would wish to be tested by demons" — **Now:** "If any of the monks would wish to make trial of wild demons." Why: the monk is the experimenter, not the subject.
- M-36 — **Was:** "For those who practice the practical life more knowingly are greatly troubled, desiring to shoot at those who are straight of heart" — **Now:** "For they rage greatly at those pursuing the practical life more knowingly, wishing to shoot down in the moonless night those upright in heart." Why: the demons are subject of both participles — the exact opposite.
- M-37 — **Was:** "The Fathers name the separation of soul from body, or soul from body and from that which clings to virtue, as the practice of death" — **Now:** "To separate body from soul belongs only to him who bound them together; but to separate soul from body belongs also to the one who longs for virtue. For our Fathers name withdrawal the practice of death and flight from the body." Why: first sentence dropped; τοῦ ἐφιεμένου = "the one who longs"; τὴν ἀναχώρησιν ("withdrawal") — the actual subject — dropped.
- M-38 — **Was:** "should not let themselves be filled by this" — **Now:** "let them blame it [the flesh]." Why: καταμεμφέσθωσαν = "let them blame" (ταύτην, the flesh).
- M-39 — **Was:** "they themselves show us things — ... — and in this part we are sick" — **Now:** "they themselves show — but we run toward them — gatherings of acquaintances..." Why: the δέ-clause ἡμεῖς δὲ προστρέχωμεν ("but we run toward them") — the moral point — was dropped.
- M-40 — **Was:** "the grasping of idols is a sign of sickness" — **Now:** "fixation of images is a sign of sickness." Why: εἴδωλα = phantasmal images; πῆξις = "fixation."
- M-41 — "impassibility"/"apatheia" → "dispassion."
- Minor: ἐμάνημεν → "we raved"; ὠδίνομεν → "in travail"; ἀνείδωλοι → "imageless"; νόμιζε (imperative) → "consider"; ἰοβόλα → "venomous"; μετὰ πάθους → "with passion."

## u02-rem-mid (chs. 56–73) — M-41, M-47–M-58

- M-47 — **Was:** "for a soul that is not able to withstand them both at once" — **Now:** "for these to attack a soul together is among impossibilities." Why: τῶν οὐκ ἐνδεχομένων — "impossible" inverted.
- M-48 — **Was:** "you have perhaps formed the thoughts of the opposing demon within yourself; even if you are able to expel the proverbial 'leek from the soil'" — **Now:** "fashion, supposedly, within yourself the thoughts of the opposing demon; and if you can drive out, as the saying goes, nail with nail." Why: πλάττε is imperative (counsel, not conjecture); ἥλῳ τὸν ἧλον = "nail with nail."
- M-49 — **Was:** "which passion you have overcome" — **Now:** "by which passion you have been more qualified." Why: πεποίωσαι (perfect passive) — the exercise reveals, not overcomes.
- M-50 — **Was:** "ask from God whatever power is needed" — **Now:** "as much as possible, ask from God." Why: ὅση δύναμις is adverbial.
- M-51 — **Was:** "see the apatheia approaching them being snatched away by those who have received it" — **Now:** "see their present dispassion being pried out by the successors." Why: τὴν προσοῦσαν = "their present"; ὑπὸ τῶν διαδεξαμένων = "by the successors"; ἐκμοχλευομένην = "pried/levered out."
- M-52 — **Was:** "imperfect apatheia is called that which is toward the power of the total overcoming of the former demon" — **Now:** "imperfect dispassion is so called with respect to the power, for now, of the demon wrestling with it." Why: genitives garbled.
- M-53 — **Was:** "when it imagines nothing of this world except during the time of prayer." — **Now:** "imagining nothing of this world during the time of prayer." Why: παρὰ τὸν καιρὸν = "during" — "except during" inverts the counsel into permission.
- M-54 — **Was:** "of the rational part of the soul... the mind is seized by knowledge as a lever" — **Now:** "the irrational part of the soul, knowledge seizing it aloft." Why: ἀλόγου = "irrational" (opposite); μετάρσιον = "aloft, on high."
- M-55 — **Was:** "The perfect one is not held in check" — **Now:** "The perfect does not practice self-control." Why: ἐγκρατεύεται is the technical term — the perfect have nothing left to restrain.
- M-56 — **Was:** "having been fully strengthened by these" — **Now:** "having been wholly mixed with these." Why: ἀνακραθείς = "mixed/combined."
- M-57 — **Was:** "surrounding our heart, refreshing it" — **Now:** "cooling our boiling thumos." Why: περιζέοντα = "boiling" (participle); θυμός → "heart" destroyed the image.
- M-58 — **Was:** "If the ancients are in a state of being pressed and counter-pressing" — **Now:** "If wrestlers consist in pressing and counter-pressing." Why: οἱ παλαίοντες = "wrestlers" (athletic image).
- M-41 — "apatheia" → "dispassion" throughout (chs. 56–70).
- Minor: πέφυκεν → "naturally comes about"; ἀναδιδομένη/ἐπιγινομένη → "rising"/"supervening"; οὐκ ἂν προέλθοι → "would not advance"; τὸ οἰκεῖον φέγγος → "its own light."

## u02-rem-close (chs. 73–90) — M-41, M-59–M-69

- M-59 — **Was:** "for this prudence is believed to stand against the passion of the demons" — **Now:** "for this is entrusted to resist the thumos of the demons." Why: πεπίστευται = "entrusted"; τῷ θυμῷ = "the thumos."
- M-60 — **Was:** "approaching us through spiritual contemplation, fill us" — **Now:** "approaching, fill us with spiritual theoria." Why: θεωρίας is the object of filling, not the means.
- M-61 — **Was:** "unless the mind also receives contemplations suitable to them." — **Now:** "unless fitting theoriai also succeed the mind." Why: διαδέξωνται = "succeed, come in turn to" — subject/object inverted.
- M-62 — **Was:** "faith is a ready good" — **Now:** "faith is an inward good." Why: ἐνδιάθετον = "inward, internal."
- M-63 — **Was:** "the soul acting through the body is affected by weak members" / "discovers through that which obstructs it its own therapeutic commandment." — **Now:** "the soul acting through the body perceives the weak members" / "through what obstructs it discovers the soul's therapeutic commandment." Why: ἐπαισθάνεται = "perceives"; αὐτῆς = the soul's (feminine).
- M-64 — **Was:** "those who wander again in the rational part" — **Now:** "as many as disturb the rational part." Why: διοχλοῦσιν = "disturb, trouble."
- M-65 — **Was:** "The bodies of those being purified do not join with those who are purified" — **Now:** "None of the things that defile bodies associates thereafter with the purified." Why: καθαιρόντων (καθαίρω) = "defiling" — read as καθαίρω ("purify"), inverting the sense.
- M-66 — **Was:** "some say there is an end, while others say there is none" — **Now:** "of the one they say there is an end, of the other that there is not." Why: τῆς μὲν…τῆς δὲ = two kinds of ignorance, not two parties.
- M-67 — **Was:** "Virtues and vices become creative through their use beyond mere utility" — **Now:** "The goods and evils beyond use become productive of virtues and vices." Why: subject/object inverted — the ἀδιάφορα produce the virtues/vices.
- M-68 — **Was:** "to make oneself such an image of God in every respect" — **Now:** "to present itself to every image of God as nearly like the prototype." Why: πάσῃ εἰκόνι is dative.
- M-69 — **Was:** "Seeds produce drops of moisture, but virtues produce knowledge; and just as tears follow the seeds, so joy follows the drops." — **Now:** "Sheaves are the fruit of seeds, and knowledge of virtues; and as tears follow seeds, so joy follows sheaves." Why: δράγματα = "sheaves."
- M-41 — "apatheia" → "dispassion."
- Minor: θεωρεῖν λόγους → "behold the logoi" (×2); ἐφίεται → "reaches for"; τῶν γεγονότων → "things that have come to be."

## u03-open (chs. 91–93) — M-41, M-70–M-72

- M-70 — **Was:** "And the same, when being somewhat disturbed at night, was relieved by the brothers of the phantasms, having been commanded to serve those who are weak with fasting" — **Now:** "And the same elder delivered a brother troubled at night by phantasms, commanding him to serve the sick with fasting." Why: ἀπήλλαξεν is active — the elder is the deliverer, not the recipient.
- M-71 — **Was:** "for to no one, he said, when asked, are such passions quenched as by mercy" — **Now:** "for in no other way, he said when asked, are such passions quenched than by mercy." Why: οὐδενὶ οὕτως = "in no other way."
- M-72 — **Was:** "the latter is according to the nature of the mind" — **Now:** "the latter is according to the nature of thumos." Why: τοῦ θυμοῦ — Macarius's answer turns on thumos, not νοῦς.
- M-41 — "apatheia" → "dispassion."
- Minor: κατορθοῦσθαι → "be set straight by them"; δίαιταν → "diet"; θᾶττον ("quickly") restored; τῶν γεγονότων → "created things"; λόγους → "logoi."

## u03-rem-early (chs. 94–97) — M-73–M-75

- M-73 — **Was:** "passing by the holy father Macarius while burning with thirst, they asked for water to drink" — **Now:** "I visited the holy father Macarius at the very midday, and burning exceedingly with thirst I asked for water to drink." Why: παρέβαλον/ᾔτουν are 1st singular — Evagrius narrates his own visit.
- M-74 — **Was:** "in all my actions I have received neither a portion of bread, nor of water, nor of sleep" — **Now:** "in twenty whole years I have taken no satiety of bread, nor of water, nor of sleep." Why: ἐν ὅλοις ἔτεσιν εἴκοσι = "in twenty whole years"; κόρον = "satiety."
- M-75 — **Was:** "would command the mother and sisters to eat him; but he, with a woman, said, you shall not eat." — **Now:** "whether he orders him, on visiting the house, to eat with his mother and sisters; but he said: With a woman you shall not eat." Why: συμφαγεῖν = "to eat with"; μετὰ γυναικός belongs with οὐ βρώσῃ.

## u03-rem-mid (chs. 98–99) — M-76–M-77

- M-76 — **Was:** "so that I might trim the opportunities of the passion; for I know him always fighting for the sake of pleasures" — **Now:** "so that I might cut off the pretexts of thumos; for I know him always fighting for the sake of pleasures." Why: τοῦ θυμοῦ — "him" is thumos, the agent the sentence is about.
- M-77 — **Was:** "love does not know how to keep the boundaries of filth or of material things." — **Now:** "love does not know how to keep deposits of food or money." Why: παραθήκας = "deposits"; βρωμάτων = "of foods" — doubly wrong.
- Minor: παρεμβολῆς τῶν γνωστικῶν → "camp of the gnostics"; δοκιμωτάτου → "most approved"; εἰς τὸ αὐτὸ δὶς → "twice in the same way."

## u03-rem-close (ch. 100, epilogue) — M-41, M-78–M-80

- M-78 — **Was:** "it is possible to encounter them apathetically while being prone to grudges and free from hatred." — **Now:** "it is possible to encounter all dispassionately, being free of grudge and hatred." Why: ἐλεύθερον governs both genitives — "prone to grudges" states the opposite.
- M-79 — **Was:** "as many things as we have found by conversing while the grace of the Holy Spirit is occasionally falling upon the vine as we scatter the seed." — **Now:** "as many things as by the grace of the Holy Spirit, gleaning while our grape was ripening at sowing-time, we have found." Why: ἐπιρωγολογούμενοι = "gleaning"; περκαζούσης τῆς σταφυλῆς = "while our grape was ripening"; ἐν τῷ σπόρῳ = "at sowing-time."
- M-80 — **Was:** "And if the sun shall strike us steadily and the grape becomes experienced" — **Now:** "But if the sun of righteousness shall shine steadily upon us and the cluster becomes ripe." Why: ὁ ἥλιος τῆς δικαιοσύνης (Mal 4:2) was dropped; πέπειρος = "ripe."
- Minor: μετὰ τὸν Κύριον → "after the Lord"; δήγματα → "bites."
