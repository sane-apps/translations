Evagrius Ponticus (c. 345–399) was born in Ibora in Pontus. He left Constantinople, became a monk in Jerusalem, and then lived in Egypt.

This English is The Practice, the work he addressed to Anatolius. It is not his treatise On Evil Thoughts, and it is not his Treatise to Eulogius. He explains the monk's practical life.

A reader meets sorrow and anger, temperance, acedia and vainglory, the strategy of demons, apatheia, the tripartite soul, and words of the holy monks.
