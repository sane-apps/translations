Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403) was bishop of Cyprus. This homily was written for Holy Saturday, the day the church remembers the burial of Christ.

This English is the Homily on the Burial of Christ's Body. It praises Joseph of Arimathea, who asked for the body and buried it, and it tells of Christ's descent into Hades.

A reader meets the descent into Hades, the breaking of the gates, the defeat of the powers that held the dead, and the resurrection of Adam and Eve.
