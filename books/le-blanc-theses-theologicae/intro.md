Louis Le Blanc de Beaulieu (1604–1675) was minister and professor of theology at the Academy of Sedan, the Reformed school on France's eastern border. His Theses were disputed by students over many years and collected in a London folio (1675).

The Theological Theses range across the whole Reformed system — Scripture, God, predestination, grace, and justifying faith — in the crisp thesis-and-proof form of the seventeenth-century schools.

This volume holds the Theses on Theology I–XLIII and On Faith I–XXII, in eighteen sections.
