Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) was the most learned Christian of the third century: textual scholar of the Hebrew Bible, preacher at Caesarea, and the first writer of systematic theology. He died of injuries suffered in the Decian persecution.

The Dialogue with Heraclides records a real church council in Arabia (c. 244–249), where Origen gently corrected Bishop Heraclides on the Trinity and the resurrection — stenographers' minutes of theology done face to face. On Pascha sets out the meaning of Christ our Passover.

This volume holds the Dialogue and On Pascha, with navigable Bible references.
