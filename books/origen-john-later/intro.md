Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) wrote this commentary on John. This file is books 13, 19, 20, 28, and 32. It is not a continuous run from book 13 to book 32, and it is not Chrysostom's homilies on John.

Book 13 takes up the Samaritan woman, the well, and her five husbands, and answers Heracleon. Book 19 takes up knowing the Father, and the temple treasury. Book 20 opens with a word to Ambrose, then Abraham's children and the line that they are of their father the devil.

Book 28 takes up Lazarus, the stone taken away, and the words "I am the resurrection and the life." Book 32 takes up the hour before the feast, the washing of the feet, and Peter's promise to lay down his life.
