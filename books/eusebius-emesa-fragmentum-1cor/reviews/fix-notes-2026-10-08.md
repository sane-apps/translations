# FIX-NOTES — eusebius-emesa-fragmentum-1cor (Eusebius of Emesa, Fragment on 1 Corinthians)

Book on Air: `eusebius-emesa-fragmentum-1cor` · corrected files: `corrected-english/eem1cor_u01_open_english.json`, `corrected-english/eem1cor_u01_rem_english.json`

## Finding 1 — §1 (u01-open) [0]: ἐπιστὰς ("when he comes") → "knowing" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "For the Lord himself, knowing, will shine light on the hidden things of the ungodly in the darkness…"

**Finding:** ἐπιστάς is the aorist participle of ἐφίστημι ("to come upon, arrive") — the Lord's *coming* (cf. 1 Cor 4:5, ἕως ἂν ἔλθῃ ὁ κύριος). "Knowing" reads a different verb (ἐπίσταμαι) and loses the eschatological frame.

**Now:** "For the Lord himself, at his coming, will shine light on the hidden things of the ungodly in the darkness…"

**Why (Greek):** "ἐπείπερ αὐτὸς ὁ κύριος ἐπιστὰς τῶν μὲν ἀσεβῶν φωτίσει τὰ κρυπτὰ τοῦ σκότους" — ἐπιστάς (from ἐφίστημι) governs the temporal clause; the 1 Cor 4:5 context ("judge nothing before the time, until the Lord comes") confirms the coming, not knowing.

## Finding 2 — §4 (u01-rem-close) [0]: mid-sentence misparsing (MAJOR)

**Was:** "He says that [God] will reveal the self-seeking and the hidden purposes of the many, for whom also he says that praise will come from God."

**Finding:** Two errors: (a) ἰδιοπραγμοσύνη — "minding one's own business," a virtue — rendered as "self-seeking" (a vice); (b) λανθανόντων τοὺς πολλούς ("escaping the notice of the many," participle + object) misparsed as "the hidden purposes of the many" (noun + genitive). The sentence continues the §1 thought: God reveals the counsels of the hearts *of the quiet, unnoticed faithful*.

**Now:** "He says that God will reveal the counsels of the hearts of those who, by minding their own business, escape the notice of the many — for whom also he says that praise will come from God."

**Why (Greek):** "ἰδιοπραγμοσύνην καὶ λανθανόντων τοὺς πολλοὺς φανερώσει τὰς βουλὰς τῶν καρδιῶν, οἷς καὶ τὸν ἔπαινον γενήσεσθαί φησιν ἀπὸ τοῦ θεοῦ" — ἰδιοπραγμοσύνη (accusative of manner, "by minding one's own business"); λανθανόντων (genitive participle) + τοὺς πολλούς (object) = "escaping the notice of the many."
