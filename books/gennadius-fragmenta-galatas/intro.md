Gennadius of Constantinople (d. 471) was patriarch from 458. This file holds his fragments on the Letter to the Galatians, not a full commentary.

Moses mediated the law for one nation. The mediator for every nation is Christ, truly God and truly human, who makes the two one. The law was not given to cancel the promise. It walled people in until justification by faith.

Paul's elemental principles are not the sun and the moon. They are the first lessons of the law, or the observances of days, water, and fire. Gennadius notes a strange remark of Eusebius in the fourth book of the Proof of the Gospel, then Paul's warning that some welcome the Galatians only to shut them out.
