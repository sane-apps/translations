Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) wrote these notes on Joshua. They are not his homilies on Joshua, and they are not his selections on Joshua.

He takes up the Jordan, heaped like a heap of wheat and read as a type of the apostles, and the question why Jericho was devoted to destruction. It was the first city taken, offered like first spoils, and it fell by trumpets alone.

At Ai the plunder of the cattle is not devoted as Jericho was. Joshua holds out the spear so the men in ambush can see it and take the city. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
