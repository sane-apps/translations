Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403) is the name on this homily. Scholars do not treat it as a sermon by the bishop of Cyprus. The collection it belongs to is later.

This English is the Homily on the Resurrection of Christ. It is a festal sermon of praises and petitions. It is not the homily on the burial of Christ's body, which tells of the descent into Hades.

A reader meets the feast of the resurrection, the move from shadow to reality, prayers for the needy, the persecuted, and captives, and a doxology to the Trinity.
