# FIX-NOTES — marcellus-ancyranus-fragmenta (Marcellus of Ancyra, Fragments)

## M1 — "whose type Jesus was saving" — τύπον ἔσῳζεν misrendered (u01-open [2])

**Was:** "This, therefore, is the great priest, whose type Jesus was saving."
**Now:** "This, therefore, is the great priest, whose type Jesus of that time bore."
**Why (Greek):** «οὗ τύπον ἔσῳζεν ὁ τηνικαῦτα Ἰησοῦς» — ἔσῳζεν here = "preserved/bore" (kept the type intact), not the soteriological "saved." Marcellus's point: Joshua of Zech 3 bore the type of the true great priest.

## M2 — "labor tirelessly to create" — ἀπειργάσατο misrendered (u01-rem-early [0])

**Was:** "…did not only labor tirelessly to create the man formed within himself as 'firstborn of all creation'…"
**Now:** "…did not only accomplish that the man formed within himself be 'firstborn of all creation'…"
**Why (Greek):** «οὐ μόνον "πρωτότοκον πάσης κτίσεως" τὸν ἐν ἑαυτῷ ἄνθρωπον κτισθέντα ἀπειργάσατο» — ἀπεργάζομαι = "effect, accomplish" (resultative); "labor tirelessly" imports an intransitive sense absent from the Greek.

## M3 — 1 Cor 15:28 passive read as active (2 occurrences)

**Was (u01-rem-close [4]):** "'then he himself will subject to him all things who have subjected all things to him, so that God may be all and in all' (1 Cor 15:28)"
**Now:** "'then he himself will be subjected to him who subjected all things to him, so that God may be all in all' (1 Cor 15:28)"
**Was (u04-rem-early [4]):** "'he himself shall subject all things to him who subjected all things to him.'"
**Now:** "'he himself shall be subjected to him who subjected all things to him.'"
**Why (Greek):** «τότε αὐτὸς ὑποταγήσεται τῷ ὑποτάξαντι αὐτῷ τὰ πάντα» — future passive; Marcellus's central eschatological proof-text requires Christ as the one subjected. (The third occurrence, u04-rem-mid [5] "shall submit," was already correct and untouched.)

## M4 — "pierced" rendered "cast out" — ἐκκεντέω misread (u04-rem-early [4] and [5])

**Was [4]:** "…the pattern of those who were cast out after having seen the one who was cast out, as the prophecy says."
**Now [4]:** "…the pattern of those who pierced him, after having seen the one who was pierced, as the prophecy says."
**Was [5]:** "'they shall see him whom they cast out'; and they cast out, clearly, the flesh."
**Now [5]:** "'they shall look on him whom they pierced'; and they pierced, clearly, the flesh."
**Why (Greek):** «ἵνα τῶν τηνικαῦτα ἐκκεντησάντων… ἑωρακότων τὸν ἐκκεντηθέντα» and «"ὄψονται γὰρ" φησὶν "εἰς ὃν ἐξεκέντησαν"· ἐξεκέντησαν δὲ δηλονότι τὴν σάρκα» — ἐκκεντέω = "pierce" (Zech 12:10 / Jn 19:37, ὄψονται εἰς ὃν ἐξεκέντησαν).

## M5 — "as he was being applied" — ᾗ προσέχαιρεν misrendered (u02-rem-early [4])

**Was:** "'I was with him, fittingly joined to him; I was there as he was being applied' (Proverbs 8:29)"
**Now:** "'I was with him, fittingly joined to him; I was the one in whom he delighted' (Proverbs 8:30)"
**Why (Greek):** «ἤμην παρ' αὐτῷ ἁρμόζουσα· ἐγὼ ἤμην ᾗ προσέχαιρεν» (Prov 8:30 LXX) — προσέχαιρον = imperfect of προσχαίρω, "delight in." The verse reference is also corrected (8:29 → 8:30).

## M6 — "does not forgive" for οὐ συγιγνώσκει = "does not agree" (u03-rem-early [6])

**Was:** '(for even he who says, "Let us make man in our image and after our likeness" [Genesis 1:26], does not forgive)'
**Now:** '(for even he who says, "Let us make man in our image and after our likeness" [Genesis 1:26], does not agree)'
**Why (Greek):** «καὶ γὰρ ὁ λέγων "ποιήσωμεν ἄνθρωπον κατ' εἰκόνα ἡμετέραν καὶ καθ' ὁμοίωσιν" οὐ συγιγνώσκει» — συγγιγνώσκω here = "agree with, assent to" (Moses's text does not assent to the two-gods construction), not the NT "pardon" sense.
