Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) preached these homilies on Judges. Rufinus translated them. They are not his notes on Judges, and they are not his selections on Judges.

He sets the days of Jesus son of Nun against the days of the wicked. Israel serves the Baals. Deborah judges, Barak is summoned, and Jael takes the tent peg.

Gideon is clothed by the Spirit. The people are too many, so the water-lapping test leaves three hundred, with trumpets, jars, and lamps. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
