Gennadius of Constantinople wrote this short commentary fragment on part of the Second Epistle to the Thessalonians. He served as Patriarch of Constantinople and was known for careful, literal study of Scripture. Only a few pieces of his commentaries on the letters of Paul survive, and this is one of them.

The fragment explains a passage about the lawless one whose public appearance is being held back. Gennadius says that when God's appointed time reaches its end, and the present boundary that restrains is removed because it has been fulfilled, then the lawless one will be revealed immediately and without any delay.

A reader should know that the text comments on the Second Epistle to the Thessalonians, chapter 2, verses 7 and 8. The key terms are rendered here as the lawless one, what now restrains, boundary, appointed time, and will be revealed. The source notation for the verses appears irregular, and this translation notes that rather than silently correcting it.
