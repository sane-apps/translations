Didymus the Blind (c. 313–c. 398) was a Christian teacher in Alexandria. He lost his sight at the age of four and remained a layman. Athanasius made him head of the catechetical school, and he followed Origen.

This English is Against the Manichees. It answers their account of where evil comes from. It is not one of his biblical commentaries.

A reader meets the question whether evil is unbegotten, the body and the Son of Perdition, the likeness of sinful flesh, kinds of chosen evil, the justice of eternal punishment, and the nature of scorpions.
