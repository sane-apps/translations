# Fix Notes — Didymus the Blind, Contra Manichaeos (didymus-contra-manichaeos)

Corrected files: `corrected-english/dcm_u01_open_english.json`, `dcm_u01_rem_english.json`, `dcm_u02_rem_english.json` (`dcm_u02_open_english.json` untouched). Schema, keys, and all unflagged passages are untouched.

## MAJOR 1 — προστάττει → "protects" (u01-rem-mid[1])
- Was: "But he protects both the clean from defilement…"
- Now: "But he commands both to be cleansed from defilement…"
- Why: Greek Προστάττει δὲ ἑκάτερα καθᾶραι ἀπὸ μολυσμοῦ (προστάσσω = command, not protect).

## MAJOR 2 — καταλευόμενος → "being left behind" (u01-rem-mid[2])
- Was: "Stephen the apostle, being left behind and about to die…"
- Now: "Stephen the apostle, being stoned and about to die…"
- Why: Greek Στέφανος ὁ ἀπόστολος καταλευόμενος (καταλεύω = stone).

## MAJOR 3 — περὶ ἁμαρτίας → "through the sin" (u01-rem-mid[3])
- Was: "…judged sin in the flesh through the sin."
- Now: "…condemned sin in the flesh, concerning sin."
- Why: Greek περὶ ἁμαρτίας κατέκρινεν τὴν ἁμαρτίαν ἐν τῇ σαρκί (Rom 8:3; περὶ + acc. = concerning).

## MAJOR 4 — intrusive edition numeral "3" (u01-rem-mid[4])
- Was: "…lacking only in this from the other three kinds of flesh…"
- Now: "…lacking only in this compared with the other flesh…"
- Why: Greek τῶν ἄλλων 3 σαρκῶν — the "3" is an intrusive edition page-numeral, not content.

## MAJOR 5 — intrusive edition numeral "4" (u01-rem-close[6])
- Was: "For we say, for example, that there are kinds of being excellent, just, temperate, and wise."
- Now: "For we say that there are kinds of being excellent, just, temperate, and wise."
- Why: Greek Λέγομεν γοῦν εἴδη 4 σπουδαίου — the "4" is an intrusive numeral, fabricated into "for example."

## MAJOR 6 — ἐχῖδνα → "hedgehogs"/"scorpions" (u02-rem-mid[1],[3])
- Was: "'You breed of hedgehogs'…'hedgehogs' signifies…offspring of hedgehogs"; "…are scorpions…sons of scorpions…offspring of scorpions."
- Now: "'Serpents, offspring of vipers' (Matt 3:7)…by 'vipers' he means those around Abraham…offspring of vipers"; "…were vipers…sons of vipers…offspring of vipers."
- Why: Greek ἐχίδνας / ἐχιδνῶν (viper). "snakes" in the same passage renders ὄφεις (serpents) and is correct — kept.

## MAJOR 7 — λογικῶν → "logical evils" (u01-rem-close[5],[7])
- Was: "…of the logical [kinds], having become evil by choice…kinds of logical evils…"
- Now: "…kinds of rational beings, having become evil by choice…kinds of evil rational beings…"
- Why: Greek εἴδη λογικῶν (kinds of rational beings) — renders "rational beings" as "logical evils," inverting the anthropology.

## MAJOR 8 — ἐπιστήμων → "scientist" (u01-rem-close[6])
- Was: "…the one called a scientist from the genus…"
- Now: "…the one called knowledgeable from the genus…"
- Why: Greek τὸν παρονομαζόμενον ἀπὸ τοῦ γένους ἐπιστήμονα (ἐπιστήμων = knowing/skilled, not "scientist").
