Photius of Constantinople (c. 810–c. 893) was Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople from 858 to 867 and again from 877 to 886. He is recognized in the Eastern Orthodox Church as Saint Photius the Great. Photius wrote numerous scriptural works, including questions and answers on points in Scripture.

This work consists of fragments of commentary on the Epistle to the Colossians. The fragments are preserved in the edition of Photius's writings in PG 101-104 and in the Khazarzar edition. They represent Photius's engagement with the text of the letter.

A reader should know that the fragments are divided into units, each with an opening and remarks in early, mid, and close sections. The text assumes familiarity with the Epistle to the Colossians itself.
