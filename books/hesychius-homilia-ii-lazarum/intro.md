Hesychius of Jerusalem (c. 412–450) preached in Jerusalem. This English is Homily II on Saint Lazarus. It is not his Homily I on Lazarus, not Amphilochius's On Lazarus, and not Eustathius's homily on Lazarus.

He speaks of the raising of Lazarus, of the envy that followed the miracle, and of the final judgment, when the tombs will be shaken.

The homily assumes the Gospel story of Lazarus and the dispute over the man who was born blind. Christ is called the King of the angels and the Lord who returns Lazarus.
