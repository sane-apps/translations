# FIX-NOTES — hesychius-homilia-ii-lazarum (Hesychius of Jerusalem, Homily II on Lazarus)

Built from site English (https://viapatrum.org/works/hesychius-homilia-ii-lazarum/). Only the flagged section (§1) is included in the corrected file; other sections are unchanged.

## Finding M-1 — §1: πρόξενον → "anticipation" (MAJOR)

**Was:** "…the mourning of nature has been turned into the anticipation of mourning from the times beginning with the advent of the Savior."

**Finding:** πρόξενος means "procurer, author" — the mourning is turned back upon the one who procured it (the devil).

**Now:** "…the mourning of nature has been turned back upon the procurer of mourning from the times beginning with the advent of the Savior."

**Why (Greek):** «εἰς τὸν πρόξενον τοῦ πένθους ἀνέστραπται» — πρόξενος = procurer/author; ἀνέστραπται (passive of ἀναστρέφω) = "has been turned back."

## Finding M-2 — §1: ἀλοχεύτῳ/ἐμακαρίζετο double error (MAJOR ×2)

**Was:** "On the bridal couch the Virgin took root to give birth…"

**Finding:** ἀλόχευτος ("without birth-pangs") → "bridal couch"; ἐμακαρίζετο ("was blessed") → "took root."

**Now:** "On a pangless childbed the Virgin was blessed to give birth…"

**Why (Greek):** «Ἐπ' ἀλοχεύτῳ παρθένος ἐμακαρίζετο τόκῳ» — ἀλόχευτος (alpha-privative + λοχεία, childbirth) = "without birth-pangs"; ἐμακαρίζετο (imperfect passive of μακαρίζω) = "was blessed/called blessed"; τόκῳ (dative) = "in birth."
