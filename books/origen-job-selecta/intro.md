Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) is the author of these selections on Job. They are not his homilies on Job, and they are not his notes on Job.

The selections take up the Accuser's raid, unjust wealth, Elihu, God's command to gird like a man, and the dragon. One of them names Marcion.

A reader needs the story of Job's losses and the closing speeches, where God answers Job and the dragon is bound. The citations follow the old Greek Bible.
