Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) wrote these notes on Leviticus. They are not his homilies on Leviticus.

The notes take up unintentional sin, Mary's purification, the leper, the feasts, and the redemption of a vowed house or field.

A reader needs the laws in their plain sense, and the way a rule is read as a picture of the church. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
