# Fix Notes — evagrius-sententiae-virginem (Exhortation to a Virgin)

**Verdict:** Patched (4 flagged findings). The remaining sampled sentences read accurately per the review.

**Files written:** `corrected-english/esv_u01_open_english.json` (§1, sentences 1–18).

**Term-consistency pass applied:** θυμός→"anger" (sent. 8 already "anger" — no change needed); ἀγάπη→"love" (sents. 1, 3 already "Love" — no change needed).

## Per-finding notes

### M-1 — Sent. 1 (§1)
- **Was:** "and serve him and kindle your heart."
- **Finding:** φωτίζω = "enlighten", not "kindle"
- **Now:** "and serve him and he will enlighten your heart."
- **Why:** καὶ φωτιεῖ τὴν καρδίαν σου — φωτίζω ("illumine, enlighten"); the subject (the Lord) is restored from the Greek.

### M-2 — Sent. 1 (§1)
- **Was:** "Love the Lord and he will love you (Ps 37:3)"
- **Finding:** fabricated/wrong reference — Ps 37:3 is about dwelling in the land, not this saying
- **Now:** "Love the Lord and he will love you"
- **Why:** the citation is absent from the Greek and points to the wrong verse; removed.

### M-3 — Sent. 7 (§1)
- **Was:** "you will not live a life of sorrow."
- **Finding:** ἐπονείδιστος = "reproachful", not "of sorrow"
- **Now:** "you will not live a reproachful life."
- **Why:** ἐπονείδιστον βίον — "a reproachful life"; "sorrow" (λύπη) is a different word.

### M-4 — Sent. 13 (§1)
- **Was:** "flee from the stories of circulating women."
- **Finding:** γραῦς = "old woman" — the age is dropped
- **Now:** "flee the tales of gossiping old women."
- **Why:** διηγήματα γραῶν φεῦγε κυκλευουσῶν — γραῶν (gen. pl. of γραῦς, "old women") + κυκλευουσῶν ("going about", i.e. gossiping); the pointed term is restored.

## Unfixable / caveats
- Sent. 16 carries "(Ps 37:6)" — the saying ("Humble yourself before the Lord, and his right hand will exalt you") echoes Jas 4:10 / 1 Pet 5:6 rather than Ps 37:6, but this citation was not flagged in the review, so it was left untouched; recommend a citation audit of the remaining sentences in a future pass.
