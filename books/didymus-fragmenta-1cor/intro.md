Didymus the Blind (c. 313–c. 398) is the name on this file. These are his fragments on the First Letter to the Corinthians, not a full commentary, and not his fragments on the Psalms.

Some in Corinth said the soul is corruptible and the resurrection of the body is superfluous. Paul makes the gospel known: Christ died, was buried, and rose. Didymus also answers people who follow Marcion, and the claim of a fifth essence, incorruptible like the stars.

The notes reach the last chapter of the letter. Timothy is to come without fear. Apollos, Fortunatus, and Origen are named.
