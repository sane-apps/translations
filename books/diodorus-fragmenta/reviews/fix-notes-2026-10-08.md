## F1 — u01-open §1 [1]: τοὺς ἀνιόντας ἀδελφοὺς → "the suffering brothers"
- **Was:** "…encouraging **the suffering brothers as a fatherly, loving parent** does."
- **Now:** "…encouraging the grieving brothers as a loving father does his children."
- **Why:** τοὺς ἀνιόντας ἀδελφοὺς ὡς τέκνα πατὴρ φιλόστοργος παρακαλῶν — "the grieving brothers" (not "suffering"), "as a loving father [encourages] his children." Authority: page Greek.

## F2 — u01-open §1 [1]: ἀρχῆθεν ἐφοδίων → "the first supplies"
- **Was:** "Through these **you send the first supplies, a custom handed down from fathers which Romans preserve among Romans**…"
- **Now:** "…through which you have sent supplies from the beginning, preserving the custom handed down from the fathers, Romans as Romans…"
- **Why:** δι' ὧν πέμπετε ἀρχῆθεν ἐφοδίων, πατροπαράδοτον ἔθος Ῥωμαίων Ῥωμαῖοι διαφυλάττοντες — ἀρχῆθεν = "from the beginning," not "first." Authority: page Greek.

## F3 — u01-open §1 [3]: κατάφρακτον → "you [do]"
- **Was:** "These things also **you [do] through such much admonition**, the [...]"
- **Now:** "These things you too, having found the [...] of the [...] through so much admonition, [...]"
- **Why:** Ταῦτα καὶ ὑμεῖς διὰ τῆς τοσαύτης νουθεσίας — "you too" (the verb is lost in the lacuna; "do" was invented). Authority: review Greek + page Greek (truncated in both).

## F4 — u01-rem-close §4 [0]: τῆς ὑπ' αὐτὸν ἐκκλησίας → "the church under him is being testified"
- **Was:** "…receives Philip as their bishop, **while the great courage of the church under him is being testified**…"
- **Now:** "…he approves Philip as their bishop, since the church under him was being attested for very many noble deeds…"
- **Why:** ἅτε δὴ ἐπὶ πλείσταις μαρτυρουμένης ἀνδραγαθίαις τῆς ὑπ' αὐτὸν ἐκκλησίας — "since the church under him was being attested for very many noble deeds" (ἀνδραγαθία = noble deed, not "courage"); ἀποδέχεται = "approves." Authority: page Greek.

## F5 — u01-rem-close §4 [0]: φυλάττεσθαι infinitive
- **Was:** "…he reminds them to guard against the perversion of the heretics." (the infinitive construction was flattened in the older build)
- **Now:** "…and he reminds them to guard against the perversion of the heretics."
- **Why:** τήν τε τῶν αἱρετικῶν διαστροφὴν ὑπομιμνήσκει φυλάττεσθαι — ὑπομιμνήσκω + infinitive: "he reminds [them] to be on guard." The re-derivation restores the infinitive construction and the full clause. Authority: page Greek.

## F6 — u01-rem-close §4 [0]: ἀνεμώλιον/ἀνδραγαθίαις → "brave"/"great courage"
- **Was:** "…while **the great courage** of the church under him is being testified…"
- **Now:** "…since the church under him was being attested for very many **noble deeds**…"
- **Why:** ἐπὶ πλείσταις... ἀνδραγαθίαις μαρτυρουμένης — "being attested for very many noble deeds" (ἀνδραγαθία). Authority: page Greek.

## Minor notes applied
- **u01-open [0]:** "to many churches throughout the whole city" → "to many churches in every city" (ἐκκλησίαις τε πολλαῖς ταῖς κατὰ πᾶσαν πόλιν). Also fixed in the same sentence: "brothers who are in metal [wealth]" → "the brothers who are in the mines" (ἐν μετάλλοις — condemned Christians sent to the mines; same class of garbled-base error).
- **u01-open [2]:** "We lead the present Lord's Day" → "We have spent this holy Lord's day" (Τὴν σήμερον οὖν Κυριακὴν ἁγίαν ἡμέραν διηγάγομεν).
- **u01-rem-early [1]** (= site [2]/[3]): "even into Italy" → "in Italy likewise... together" — re-derived: "And having taught together in Italy likewise, they were martyred at the same time" (ὁμοίως δὲ καὶ εἰς τὴν Ἰταλίαν ὁμόσε διδάξαντες, ἐμαρτύρησαν κατὰ τὸν αὐτὸν καιρόν); "as if they had been forged" → "as having been tampered with" (ὡς ῥᾳδιουργηθεισῶν — "forged" too strong).
- **u01-rem-early [2]** (= site [4]/[6]): "having been deemed worthy to write the letters of brothers" → "For I wrote letters at the request of brothers who asked me to write" (Ἐπιστολὰς γὰρ ἀδελφῶν ἀξιωσάντων με γράψαι, ἔγραψα); "the Lord's [letters]" → "the Lord's scriptures" (τῶν Κυριακῶν... γραφῶν); "have cast forged things upon" → "have attempted to tamper with" (ῥαδιουργῆσαί τινες ἐπιβέβληνται).
- **u01-rem-mid [1]** (= site [3]): "he testifies of their leader Puplius regarding the persecutions" → "it came about that their leader Publius was martyred in the persecutions of that time"; "Kodratus standing as their bishop after the one who testified, Puplius" → "Quadratus having become their bishop after the martyred Publius" (τὸν μαρτυρήσαντα = "the one martyred," not "the one who testified").
- **u01-rem-mid [3]** (= site [5]): "he presents the rule to the truth" → "he stands by the rule of truth" (τῷ τῆς ἀληθείας παρίσταται κανόνι).
- **u01-rem-close [2]** (= site [3]): "not to always lay the heavy burden of chastity upon the brothers" → "not to lay the heavy burden concerning chastity upon the brothers as compulsory, but to have regard for the weakness of the many" (μὴ βαρὺ φορτίον ἐπάναγκες... ἐπιτιθέναι — ἐπάναγκες = "as compulsory," not "always").
