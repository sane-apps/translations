Diodorus of Tarsus (c. 330–c. 390) is the author of these fragments. They are not his fragments on Romans, and they are not a commentary on Genesis or the Psalms.

The pieces take up the Roman custom of charity, the planting of the churches, and forged letters. They then describe the epistles and summarize them.

A reader needs the difference between a genuine apostolic letter and a forged one. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
