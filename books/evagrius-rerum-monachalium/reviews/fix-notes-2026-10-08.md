## Per-finding notes

### M-1 — §1 (u01-open)
- **Was:** "and he will make those who are chosen and immortal fruits of the heavenly life."
- **Finding:** ἀειθαλής = "ever-green", not "chosen"
- **Now:** "and he will make the ever-green and immortal fruits of the heavenly life."
- **Why:** τοὺς ἀειθαλεῖς καὶ ἀθανάτους… καρπούς — ἀειθαλής ("ever-green, unfading") describes the καρπούς (fruits); "chosen" is a different word (ἐκλεκτός).

### Term-consistency fixes (unflagged, in scope per the systematic-defect mandate)
- "fleshly logismoi and desires" → "fleshly thoughts and desires" (λογισμοί→"thoughts").
- "you shall become incorporeal, impassive, and outside of every desire" → "incorporeal, dispassionate, and outside of every desire" (ἀπαθής→"dispassionate").
