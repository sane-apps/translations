## Finding 1 — §5: impatience misattributed to Moses instead of Israel (MAJOR)

**Was:** "For Moses, when meeting with the Lord, impatiently received the necessary delays."

**Finding:** The subject of *exceperat* is Israel (the continuing subject: "Israhel per impatientiam semper in deum deliquisse"). "Moysei cum domino congredientis" is a genitive phrase modifying *moras* — "the delays of Moses' meeting with the Lord." It was Israel that impatiently received them (the golden-calf episode, Exodus 32).

**Now:** "For they impatiently received the necessary delays of Moses' meeting with the Lord."

**Why (Latin):** «tam necessarias enim Moysei cum domino congredientis impatienter exceperat moras» — *Moysei… congredientis* (genitive) modifies *moras*; *exceperat* (3rd sg.) has Israel as subject from the preceding «Israhel… deliquisse manifestum est».

*Sections in corrected file: §5 only (4 sentences, 1 corrected). Minor findings 2–3 (aemulatio, mira aequanimitatis fides) are non-mechanical and out of scope.*
