Cyril of Alexandria (c. 376–444) led the church of Alexandria from 412 to his death. Best remembered for his clash with Nestorius over the person of Christ, settled at the Council of Ephesus (431), he was also the most learned biblical interpreter of the early fifth century.

On Adoration and Worship in Spirit and Truth is a long dialogue with Palladius unfolding how the Law of Moses is fulfilled in Christ — sacrifice, priesthood, and festival all read as shadows of the gospel.

This volume holds Book 1, on true worship and the meaning of sacrifice.
