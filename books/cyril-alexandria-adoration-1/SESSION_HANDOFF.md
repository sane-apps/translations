## Charter

- **Title:** Cyril of Alexandria: On Adoration and Worship in Spirit and Truth, Book 1
- **Author:** Cyril of Alexandria (patriarch 412–444; d. 27 June 444)
- **Type:** Monograph. Language: English (new translation).
- **Why this book:** *De adoratione* (CPG 5200) has **no complete English**. Book 1 is a complete dialogue with Palladius: man’s turn to worthlessness, captivity to sin, calling and return. Shipping Book 1 does not claim the 17 books are done.
- **Scope:** Book 1 only (PG 68.134–211). Out of scope: Books 2–17, Thesaurus, Trinity Dialogues, Glaphyra.
- **Era:** Post-Nicene. Public site and Logos front matter must say so. This is not Cyril of Jerusalem.
- **Legal:** private study. New AI-assisted English from locked PD Greek (Migne PG 68). Modern English (Villani German 2021; Catholic Library machine English; any unpublished academia.edu English of later books) may be used only as a sense/style check — never copied. No TLG scrape. No purchase.
- **Sources:** see `sources/manifest.json`. Prefer PG 68 Greek; 16th–17th-c. Latin of Agellius is a check, not the copy-text.
- **Enrichment:** inline Bible links + Headword TN marks only. No Word footnotes. No `logosres:`.
