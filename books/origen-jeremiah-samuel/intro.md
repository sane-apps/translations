Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) preached daily at Caesarea in the 230s and 240s, his sermons taken down by shorthand writers as he spoke. No early Christian preacher survives in such bulk.

The Homilies on Jeremiah press judgment and repentance on a comfortable church, while the Homily on 1 Samuel 28 tackles the darkest text in Samuel — the medium of Endor and the apparition of the prophet.

This volume holds the Jeremiah homilies with the 1 Samuel 28 homily, with navigable Bible references.
