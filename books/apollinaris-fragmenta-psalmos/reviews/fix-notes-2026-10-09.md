## u05-rem-early (issue #5)
- **Was:** "...my eye has flowed out from affliction, it has given out' (Ps 87:9)."
- **Now:** "...(Ps 87:10)."
- **Why:** Οἱ ὀφθαλμοί μου ἠσθένησαν ἀπὸ πτωχείας is LXX Ps 87:10 (English 88:9).
- **Was:** "just as it was previously spoken in the Psalms that Akylas has established His throne in judgment."
- **Now:** "just as Aquila had also rendered it before in the ninth psalm: 'He set His throne in judgment' (Ps 9:8)."
- **Why:** καθὰ καὶ ἐν Θ προείρηκε ψαλμῷ Ἀκύλας Ἥδρασεν ἐν κρίσει τὸν θρόνον αὐτοῦ: Aquila's rendering of LXX Ps 9:8 (English 9:7), quoted from the ninth psalm.

## u06-open (issue #6)
- **Was:** "says all things and that Israel might not be seen by God, which it risked suffering..."
- **Now:** "says what belongs to all: he asks not to be overlooked by God, the very thing Israel risked suffering..."
- **Why:** τὸ μὴ περιοφθῆναι ὑπὸ θεοῦ ὅπερ ἐκινδύνευσε παθεῖν ... ὁ Ἰσραήλ: Israel risked being overlooked (περιοφθῆναι); the speaker asks not to be.

## u10-rem-close (issue #7)
- **Was:** "'In peace I will lie down and sleep' (Ps 4:8)."
- **Now:** "(Ps 4:9)."
- **Why:** Ἐν εἰρήνῃ ... κοιμηθήσομαι καὶ ὑπνώσω is LXX Ps 4:9 (English 4:8); the work cites LXX numbering.
