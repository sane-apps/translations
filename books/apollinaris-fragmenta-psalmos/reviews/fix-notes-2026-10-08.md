# FIX-NOTES — apollinaris-fragmenta-psalmos (Fragments on the Psalms)

**Base used:** the Mac book `books/apollinaris-fragmenta-psalmos/translations/apofp_*_english.json` (the fix target per the task). NOTE on segmentation: the Mac `english` arrays are a condensed cut — consecutive site sentences are merged into single array entries (e.g. Mac u01-open[2] = site [2]+[3]+[4]). The review's sentence indices refer to the site's finer segmentation; every flagged phrase was located in the Mac arrays by content match and corrected in place. Only files with changes are emitted (14 files), with original filenames.

**Authority per passage:** the review's recovered Greek cross-checked against the on-page Greek (OCR-garbled; intra-word spaces). Where the page Greek was too garbled to verify, the review's Greek is the authority — noted per finding.

## F1 — u01-open §1 (Ps 8:5) [2]: subject inverted
- **Was:** "…he says, '**I will make a memory** of one fallen from you and **I will visit** one brought to sickness'"
- **Now:** "…he says, 'you remembered the one who had fallen away from you, and visited the one brought down into sickness'"
- **Why:** καὶ μνήμην ἐποιήσω… καὶ ἐπεσκέψω are 2nd person (God "remembered" and "visited"); the old text made the psalmist the subject in the future tense. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F2 — u01-open §1 (Ps 16:10–12) [8]: λιπαρωτάτους/στέατι → "lean… surrounded by bones"
- **Was:** "…they are **extremely lean in body, surrounded by bones**…"
- **Now:** "…they are very fat of body, enclosed in tallow…"
- **Why:** λιπαρωτάτους = "very fat"; στέατι περιπεφραγμένους = "enclosed with tallow" (Ps 16:10) — the exact opposite. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F3 — u01-open §1 (Ps 16:14c) [10]: Ἰουδὰ → "Judah"
- **Was:** "…according to what is said **against Judah**…"
- **Now:** "…according to what is said of Judas…"
- **Why:** the curse (Ps 108:18) is applied to Judas (Ἰουδὰ = Judas Iscariot), a different person from Judah. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F4 — u01-open §1 (Ps 26:4) [12]: ἀσμένως → "poorly"
- **Was:** "…or a book **poorly**…"
- **Now:** "…or a book gladly…"
- **Why:** ἀσμένως = "gladly." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F5 — u01-rem-close §4 (Ps 39:14) [12]: ἐπαύξεσθαι → "resting"

## F6 — u01-rem-early §2 (Ps 35:8) [21]: subject/object inverted

- **Why:** Πάλιν τὸ τῆς δικαιοσύνης ὑψηλὸν ὄρεσι παραβάλλει — the psalmist compares righteousness's height to mountains. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F9 — u02-open §5 (Ps 40:10) [1]–[2]: πτέρνη → "wing"

- **Why:** πτέρνη = "heel" (Gen 3:15; confused with πτέρυξ, wing); the appended verse is Ps 40:10 LXX, not 40:11. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F14 — u04-open §13 (Ps 75:5–7) [22]: Σίτησιν/Σύμμαχος garbled
- **Was:** "…'Blessing his prey I will bless it,' **which indeed Sitias said in that [Psalm] Symmachus**."
- **Now:** "…'Blessing her prey I will bless it'—which Symmachus rendered 'food' in that psalm."
- **Why:** "Sitias" was invented; the clause means Symmachus rendered the word as σίτησιν ("food"). Authority: review Greek; page Greek too garbled to verify.

## F15 — u04-open §13 [23]: Ἀκύλας → "Achilles"; numerals → "Psalm 181/182"
- **Was:** "And in **[Psalm] 182**, 'He gave prey to those who fear him,' **Achilles** said, and Symmachus."
- **Now:** "And in Psalm 110, 'he gave prey to those who fear him,' said Aquila, and Symmachus." (and "in [Psalm] 181" → "in Psalm 131")
- **Why:** ΡΛΑ = 131, ΡΙ = 110 (Greek numerals misread as 181/182); Ἀκύλας = Aquila, not Achilles. Authority: review Greek; page Greek too garbled to verify.

## F18 — u04-rem-close §16 (Ps 78:4) [2]: δικαιολογία → "lifted up as acceptable righteousness"

## F19 — u04-rem-close §16 (Ps 78:7) [4]: δίκην → "judgment"

## F20 — u04-rem-close §16 (Ps 79:3–4) [13]: τῇ σκιᾷ → "the eighteenth shadow"; ἀκρεμόνων → "the unblemished ones"
- **Was:** "With **the eighteenth shadow**, indeed, the aforementioned vine covered the mountains… and with the outstretching of **the unblemished ones**, the cedars of God [were moved]…"
- **Now:** "With its shade, indeed, the aforementioned vine covered the mountains… and with the spreading of its branches, the cedars of God [were covered]…"
- **Why:** τῇ σκιᾷ = "with its shade" (misread as a numeral); ἀκρέμων = "branch." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F21 — u04-rem-close §16 (Ps 80:7) [26]–[27]: ἀπέστησεν → "stood away from swellings"
- **Was:** "**Removals**, that is, of the burdens, of **the swellings**… Their back, therefore, He says, **stood away from swellings**, that is, He freed it from the burdens (Ps 80:7)."
- **Now:** "‘Burdens’—that is, of the loads, of the carrying; for those serving in brick-making, cutting the clay with a spade, carry it on their backs. Their back, therefore, he says, he removed from the burdens—that is, he freed it from the carrying (Ps 80:7)."
- **Why:** ἀπέστησεν ἀπὸ ἄρσεων = "he removed [it] from burdens" (Ps 80:7 LXX); ἐπάρσεων = "of carrying." Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F22 — u06-open §21 [6]: θαρρεῖν → "to dare to rebel"
- **Was:** "…**to dare [to rebel] against all things was not just** by the power of salvation."
- **Now:** "…for those who were eyewitnesses of so many and such wonders, it was right to be bold in all things through the power of the one who saved them."
- **Why:** θαρρεῖν = "to be courageous" (τοὺς… θαυμάτων αὐτόπτας θαρρεῖν εἰς ἅπαντα δίκαιον ἦν); the sense was inverted. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F23 — u06-open §21 (Ps 105:28) [9]: Βεελφεγὼρ → "Beelzebub"; νεκρῶν misread
- **Was:** "…to be initiated into the things of **Beelzebub** and to be joined in **sacrifices performed by the dead**."
- **Now:** "…to be initiated into the rites of Baal-peor and to be defiled along with them by sacrifices of the dead."
- **Why:** Βεελφεγὼρ = Baal-peor (Beelphegor), not Beelzebub; νεκρῶν is genitive of material — "sacrifices of the dead" (Num 25). Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F24 — u06-open §21 (Ps 106:23–32) [20]: ΚΑ → "105th Psalm"

- **Why:** ΚΑ = 21; the quotation is Ps 21:27 LXX. Both number and verse fixed. Authority: review Greek + page Greek.

## F32 — u08-open §29 (Ps 118:99) [0]: subject/object inverted
