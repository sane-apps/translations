Hesychius of Jerusalem (c. 412–450) was a priest in Jerusalem. This English is Homily II on Saint Mary the Mother of God. It is not his Homily I on Mary, and it is not Epiphanius's homily in praise of Mary.

The homily moves from the Annunciation to the Magi, then to a condemnation, and then to prophecies of Jesus. Mary answers the angel, and the preacher tells the hearers to accept the gospels.

A reader meets Gabriel, Mary, the Magi, and the prophets. One prophecy is the virgin who will bear a son, and another is the prophet to be raised up from among the brothers. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
