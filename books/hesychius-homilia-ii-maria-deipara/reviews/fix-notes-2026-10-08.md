## Finding M-1 — §1: ἀστενοχωρήτως → "in a narrow space" (MAJOR, 180°)

**Was:** "…it celebrates the virgin who surpassed all others, even willingly receiving God the Word to be borne by her in a narrow space."

**Finding:** The paradox of the Virgin containing the uncontainable is inverted — "uncontainedly/spaciously" became "in a narrow space."

**Now:** "…it celebrates the virgin who surpassed all others, even willingly receiving God the Word, contained by her uncontainedly."

**Why (Greek):** «χωρηθέντα παρ' αὐτῆς ἀστενοχωρήτως» — ἀστενοχωρήτως (alpha-privative + στενοχωρέω, "confine in narrow space") = "uncontainedly, spaciously"; χωρηθέντα (aorist passive participle) = "contained."
