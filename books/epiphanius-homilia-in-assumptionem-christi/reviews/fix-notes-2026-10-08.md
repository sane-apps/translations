## Finding M-1 — ἀνεξικάκως ("with forbearance") misread as a negative (MAJOR)

**Was:** "If you wish to learn, do not strain my mind needlessly."

**Finding:** ἀνεξικάκως is a positive adverb ("forbearing, patient"); συντείνατέ μοι τὸν νοῦν = "lend/apply your mind to me." The English inverted the adverb into a prohibition ("do not… needlessly") and turned the audience's task (lend *me your* mind) into straining *my* mind.

**Now:** "If you wish to learn, lend me your mind with forbearance."

**Why (Greek):** ἀνεξικάκως συντείνατέ μοι τὸν νοῦν — ἀνεξίκακος ("not returning evil, forbearing") is standard; μοι is the dative of the speaker (the preacher asks the hearers' patient attention).

*Segments corrected: 1 (u01-open [1], first clause only). The rest of the sentence is unchanged.*
