Epiphanius of Salamis (c. 310/320–403) was bishop of Salamis. This English is his homily on the Ascension of Christ. It is not a sermon on the death or assumption of Mary.

He calls this feast the head of the other feasts, because it celebrates the Ascension of Christ in the flesh. He then speaks of people who tried to hide the event, and of a voice that confesses it mistook Christ for a mere man and is now defeated.

The close turns to the parable of the sower. The earth belongs to the Lord, and the Church is the field that bears fruit.
