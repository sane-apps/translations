Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) wrote these notes on Judges. They are not his homilies on Judges, and they are not his selections on Judges.

One note reads the jawbone another way. Instead of God breaking a wound in the jawbone, he split the earth by the blow, and waters came out. The other note asks why Israel was defeated by Benjamin after going out justly. He says it was because of earlier causes.

After two defeats, wrath came on the wrongdoers and Israel won a just judgment. The note quotes the psalm about judging in due time, and the line that Benjamin is a ravenous wolf. The citations use the old names of the biblical books.
