Severianus of Gabala (fl. c. 400) was bishop of Gabala in Syria and a preacher in Constantinople. This sermon was long copied among works attributed to John Chrysostom.

This English is On the Blind Man and Zacchaeus. It is not his sermon On the Man Born Blind, which is a different book.

A reader meets the blindness of the soul, faith and grace, the net of love, Abraham, the books of life and judgment, and the oversight of the farmer.
