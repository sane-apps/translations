# FIX-NOTES — evagrius-de-vitiis

Both section files retranslated in full from the Greek (systematic error profile: false friends, missed privatives, voice flips, nouns turned into verbs). Also restored the missing **Chapter 1** heading (the Greek carries ΚΕΦΑΛ. Αʹ and Βʹ; the English showed only "Chapter 2").

## u01-open — full retranslation

- **Missing heading**: ΚΕΦΑΛ. Αʹ now rendered "Chapter 1." before the preface text.
- **M-1** Was: "through a direct/cutting-to-the-point method." Finding: now "in summary" (δι' ἐπιτόμου), three times.
- **M-2** Was: "until we have cleared the mind thus." Finding: now "seeing that thus far, having plowed the mind, we have cast the thorns of the thoughts out of the sown land." Why: ἕως μὲν = "seeing that thus far" (not "until"); ἀροτριάω = "plow" (not "clear").
- **M-3** Was: "having become consistent with some of their actions." Finding: now "having become witnesses of some of the deeds." Why: σύνιστορες = "witnesses, co-knowers."
- **M-4** Was: "the schemes of the foolish." Finding: now "the plots of the mind-deceivers." Why: τῶν φρεναπατῶν.
- **M-5** Was: "those who toil in works, or those who have begun to be taught, you have been satisfied" (third person). Finding: now "you who toil in works; already you who have begun to be taught have been satiated" — vocative + 2nd plural restored.
- **M-6** Was: "I have been instructed in reasoning." Finding: now "I have borrowed reasoning." Why: δεδάνεισμαι = "I have borrowed."
- **M-7** Was: "I have received a judgment." Finding: now "I gave counsel with judgment." Why: παρῄνεσα = "I counseled, advised."
- **M-8** Was: "if anyone has held onto forgiveness toward you." Finding: now "if any forgiveness is held in store by you." Why: κεκράτηται is perfect passive ("is held"); παρ' ὑμῶν = "by you" (not "toward you").
- **M-9** Was: "so that I may also subject." Finding: now "so that we too may set down." Why: ὑποτάξωμεν is 1st plural ("we may set down"), not 1st singular "subject".
- **M-10** Was: "liberality." Finding: now "non-possession" (ἀκτημοσύνη).
- **M-11** Was: "humility" (for ἀκενοδοξία). Finding: now "freedom from vainglory" — ταπείνωσις, which follows, is humility.
- **M-12** Was: "generosity." Finding: now "freedom from envy" (ἀφθονία).

## u01-rem — full retranslation (chs. 2–4, the sententious definitions)

- **M-13** Was: "the imitation of filth… a uselessness of filth." Finding: now "the portrait of foods… the cheapness of foods" (βρωμάτων = "of foods," twice).
- **M-14** Was: "an unbridled ass." Finding: now "a loose foal" (ἀνειμένος πῶλος).
- **M-15** Was: "the shadow-sketch of gluttonies." Finding: now "the sketch of seasonings" (σκιαγράφος ἀρτυμάτων).
- **M-16** Was: "a cheerful death." Finding: now "a smileless death" (ἀμειδὴς — alpha-privative; 180°).
- **M-17** Was: "a chew for hunger." Finding: now "the whip of insatiability" (ἀπληστείας μάστιξ).
- **M-18** Was: "a balm for light eating." Finding: now "the muzzle for gluttony" (ἀδηφαγίας κημός — 180° on both words).
- **M-19** Was: "a wave of gluttony." Finding: now "the offspring of gluttony" (λαιμαργίας κύημα).
- **M-20** Was: "a bridegroom of idols." Finding: now "the bridesman of idols" (νυμφαγωγὸς εἰδώλων).
- **M-21** Was: "an unnatural act." Finding: now "an unrelated deed" (ἀσυγγενὴς πρᾶξις).
- **M-22** Was: "a crushing of excess." Finding: now "the axe of licentiousness" (ἀσελγείας πέλυξ).
- **M-23** Was: "a calculation of those who depart." Finding: now "the reckoning of depositors" (ἐναποθέτων λογιστεία).
- **M-24** Was: "a sweetness of diseases." Finding: now "the fatness of diseases" (νοσημάτων πιότης).
- **M-25** Was: "a charm of many years." Finding: now "the prophet of many years" (πολυετίας μάντις).
- **M-26** Was: "Lack of restraint." Finding: now "Non-possession" (Ἀκτημοσύνη; "lack of restraint" would be ἀκρασία).
- **M-27** Was: "I uproot greed for silver, but plant the root of non-greed." Finding: now "the uprooting of love of money, the rooting of freedom from love of money" — ἐκρίζωμα/ῥίζωμα are nouns, not 1st-person verbs.
- **M-28** Was: "a breath of love." Finding: now "the vote for love" (ἀγάπης ἔμψηφος — ἔμψηφος = "voting, enrolled").
- **M-29** Was: "a reconstruction of order." Finding: now "the dismantling of the good condition" (καταστάσεως ἀνασκευή — 180°).
- **M-30** Was: "a law of passion." Finding: now "the law of anger" (θυμοῦ νόμος), per the θυμός convention.
- **M-31** Was: "blessing the blasphemed." Finding: now "being blasphemed, it blesses" — βλασφημουμένη is a passive participle; the voice flip is fixed.

## Terms applied uniformly
ἀπάθεια→"dispassion" (n/a here), θυμός→"anger", ἀκηδία→"listlessness", θεωρία→"contemplation" (γνώσεως θεωρία = "the contemplation of knowledge"). Fresh renderings from the Greek only.
