Evagrius Ponticus (c. 345–399) is the name on this file. It sets vices against the virtues that oppose them. The English also gives it under the name of Nilus of Ancyra. It is not the sentences to monks.

Whatever a person has was received, so there is no boast. The pure in heart are named. Gluttony, vainglory, and pride are among the vices, and pride is set against lowliness.

The list is offered to someone who loves study. It clears the mind and takes the thorns of the logismoi off fruitful ground.
